**Interpretation:** The earlier checkpoint is the most defensible saved policy because it is independently better on the disjoint comparison and late training slightly degraded measured behavior. The experiment made no researcher-owned code changes, so the scientific recipe should be kept. The selected policy should remain available for future development, but terminal assessment should not be used to resolve the remaining uncertainty; further training is the appropriate next experiment after closure.

The parent remains the most defensible reusable policy because experiment 1 selected it on disjoint evidence; that selection evidence and the fixed task-reference panel are not independent confirmation of the current result. No final assessment is requested because development evidence remains below the objective and further training is the appropriate separate next experiment.

**Interpretation:** Restore the established working recipe and preserve the existing best-known designation. The parent remains the most defensible reusable policy because it ties the intervention's best measured checkpoint on the disjoint panel and avoids the final checkpoint's degradation. The seed-7000 result is development evidence, not independent confirmation of the previously selected lineage, and the fixed task-reference panel is also permanently reused. No final benchmark is requested; further training remains a separate future experiment.

This is evidence about one fresh training realization, not a claim that every fresh seed fails.

**Interpretation:** The replication provides no basis for replacing the established policy or changing its scientific recipe. The working lineage is supported by the new disjoint panel and has `977/1000` pooled successes across six distinct development panels, including `196/200` on seed 9000; this is strong readiness evidence but remains non-official. Request the final benchmark for the existing best-known model. Any further training is a separate post-closure experiment, not part of this decision.

## 229ee49c-81d2-4d70-b9d1-83d2c786555a / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The existing PPO `working`/`best_known` lineage remains the strongest saved policy, with `977/1000` pooled successes across six distinct research panels. Experiment 5's fresh unchanged-recipe replication scored `148/200` at its measured proxy plateau and `136/200` at its final checkpoint, while the working control scored `196/200` on the same disjoint panel. The replication therefore did not challenge the current lineage, and its late degradation is consistent with the instability seen in earlier continuations.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward are checkpoint-selection signals, not task scores. Across the campaign, residual failures include both missed tolerance and interrupted or incomplete holds; changing target coverage, hold-exit reward, learning rate, or fresh initialization has not produced a measured replacement for the working policy. The seed-9000 working result is disjoint from the panels used to select it, but all research panels, including the fixed task-reference panel, remain development evidence rather than the official assessment.

**Open questions:** The separate official 200-episode assessment remains the only unresolved question for the selected policy. The current evidence supports expecting the existing best-known lineage to satisfy the objective, while the failed replication does not justify retaining its challengers or preparing further training before the terminal assessment.

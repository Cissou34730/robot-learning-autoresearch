# Research postmortems

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The baseline learned a useful but sub-objective reach-and-hold controller. Checkpoint-100352 is the strongest measured late policy, with 531/560 successes (94.8%) across three disjoint research panels. Its 98.0% fixed task-reference result is not independent confirmation, and neither checkpoint-110592 nor checkpoint-120832 improved the measured late-policy position. The residual failures remain coupled reach, entry, settling, and hold behavior rather than evidence of one isolated stage deficit.

**Lessons and limits:** Training reward and training success tracked the emergence of competence but did not establish the human objective. Across the recorded diagnostics for checkpoint-100352, 18 of 29 failures never entered tolerance and 11 entered it without completing the uninterrupted hold. One detailed panel shows non-entry concentrated in a negative-angle sector and includes several inner-radius targets, but this is descriptive evidence from one panel and does not identify a cause or exclude trajectory, posture, settling, or local-regulation effects. Development panels remain below the 98% objective and cannot establish the official result; checkpoint-110592 scored 94.5% where checkpoint-100352 scored 95.5%, with two discordant episodes favoring checkpoint-100352.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved how target radius and angle, inverse-kinematic branch, trajectory and velocity, settling, and sustained local regulation interact in the residual failures. It is also unresolved whether changes in complete success would reflect fewer tolerance-entry failures, fewer hold interruptions, or a trade between those behaviors. The existing measurements do not identify those mechanisms or establish behavior outside the sampled development panels.

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a useful learned policy but did not demonstrate the human objective. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as the working and best-known lineage for this closure.

**Observed behavior:** Checkpoint-100352 achieved 94.375%, 94.5%, and 95.5% on three disjoint research panels, totaling 531/560 successes. It achieved 98.0% on the reused fixed task-reference panel. Checkpoint-120832 achieved 94.375% and 93.5% on two disjoint panels, while checkpoint-110592 achieved 94.5% on the third panel and lost the paired comparison to checkpoint-100352 by two discordant episodes. The late checkpoints therefore remained below the 98% objective on fresh research panels despite high training-time proxy values.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline established substantial reach-and-hold competence and produced a measurable candidate for future work, but it did not establish a policy satisfying the 98% objective. The conclusion is limited to this training recipe, these saved checkpoints, and the recorded development panels; it does not explain the residual failures or rule out future improvements.

**Interpretation:** The policy commonly reaches and holds the target, but the complete behavior remains unreliable across the full target distribution. The failure diagnostics show both failure to enter the tolerance region and failure to sustain an entered tolerance region, consistent with the Scientific model's coupled approach, settling, and hold constraints. Since the additional late checkpoint did not improve the measured leader, the available evidence supports resolving lineage around checkpoint-100352 without treating its favorable fixed-panel score as independent confirmation.

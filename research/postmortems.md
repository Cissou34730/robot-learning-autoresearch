# Research postmortems

## 59709a6b-6a0b-4fa1-95e7-9b9cba8cdeed / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% official success. The best measured policy remains the experiment-2 baseline checkpoint-100352 (`working`): 3313/3400 = 97.44% pooled over six distinct research panels, with all 87 measured failures in the folded-required sector, the ~5.5% of uniform targets whose open-elbow IK shoulder angle, once wrapped to ±180°, exceeds the arm's ±170° hinge range. The sector is not uniform. Success orders sharply by the wrapped open-branch shoulder value rather than by target depth or radius: about 94% for |wrapped| 170-176° but 0-2% for 176-180° (experiment 6 measured 26/26 in 170-174°, 7/9 in 174-176°, 0/15 in 176-180°). A kinematic reconstruction from the recorded target geometry and joint limits reproduces that split: the smallest end-effector distance achievable while holding the shoulder at its ±170° limit and choosing the elbow freely is below the 1 cm tolerance for essentially all 170-176° targets but for only about 10% of 176-180° targets. On the experiment-6 panel the folded successes have minimum distances ≤0.83 cm and the folded failures 0.9-1.7 cm, i.e. neither far-misses nor sub-millimetre near-misses of a correct but imprecise strategy. The consistent reading is that the policy approaches folded-required targets by saturating the shoulder against its joint limit — the out-of-range open-branch solution — and reaches tolerance only where that clamped geometry happens to allow it, apparently never selecting the alternative folded-branch configuration, which is comfortably within joint limits (folded shoulder near 90°). Four interventions left the objective unmet and read as one negative family: experiment 3 (radius coverage) and experiment 6 (open-shoulder wrap projection) were flat or harmful, while experiment 4 (terminal-precision reward) and experiment 5 (folded-target substitution) degraded behavior, and 5 and 6 show that editing the branch inputs under transfer destroys the branch distinction the policy relies on. Training proxies (success_rate, ep_rew_mean) disagreed with measured behavior in every run.

**Lessons and limits:** Reusable: measured task outcome, not training success_rate or ep_rew_mean, must select models; behavior within a late plateau is homogeneous, so checkpoint selection inside a plateau is low-value. The folded/open kinematic partition is the cleanest structural predictor of failure and, inside it, the wrapped open-branch shoulder value; representation edits to the branch inputs are high-variance under transfer and any change must preserve the branch contrast. New: the folded-required residual is quantitatively consistent with open-branch saturation rather than with precision near-misses, which localizes it to branch selection and to what the training distribution exposes. Limits: one training seed and one recipe lineage; seven checkpoints measured; the branch partition, band ordering and clamped-open distance model are kinematic computations from recorded target geometry and joint limits, not observations of the policy's internal state or of its joint trajectories, so the saturation account is an association consistent with the data rather than a demonstrated mechanism; without an unchanged-continuation control the degradations in experiments 3-6 cannot be separated from ordinary weight drift under continued training; the task-reference panel is reused and selection-contaminated.

**Open questions:** Whether the deep folded-required residual is a training-exposure or optimization property of the current method or an intrinsic limitation of the 64x64 policy and distance-based reward is not decided; the clamped-open agreement is strong circumstantial evidence for the former but does not observe which branch the policy chooses. Whether the marginal folded failures are static offsets or small limit cycles is not captured by the recorded diagnostics. Whether the 98.0% task-reference reading reflects a genuine level or one-panel variance remains open. These are recorded uncertainties, not priorities and not a queue of required actions.

## 59709a6b-6a0b-4fa1-95e7-9b9cba8cdeed / Experiment 3

**Result:** Fine-tuning the working policy on the full official 0.06-0.20 m target radius range did not improve measured task success. On one shared, previously unused 600-episode research panel the parent scored 96.67% (580/600) and the two measured fine-tuned checkpoints 96.33% (578/600) and 96.50% (579/600); the parent won both paired comparisons, and the failure set was almost exactly unchanged.

**Observed behavior:**
- Round 1 measured all three models on the identical fresh panel seed 22000, episodes 22000-22599 (600 episodes, research_evaluation semantics `543af51fd137`): `working` (experiment-2 checkpoint-100352) 580/600 = 96.67%; `checkpoint-120832` 579/600 = 96.50%; `checkpoint-105472` 578/600 = 96.33%. - Failure sets are near-identical: 23 union failures over the panel, 19 failing in all three models. Distinct failures per model were 20 (`working`), 22 (`checkpoint-105472`), and 21 (`checkpoint-120832`); `working`-unique {(273)}, `checkpoint-105472`-unique {(216)}, `checkpoint-120832`-unique {}. - Failure margins are marginal against the 1 cm tolerance: minimum distance 0.45-1.70 cm. Failure modes: `working` 7 never-reached / 13 reached-then-lost-hold; `checkpoint-105472` 3 / 19; `checkpoint-120832` 6 / 15. Hold failures usually show 1-3 interruptions and max held 1-8 steps; two `checkpoint-105472` episodes (targets 14.4 cm/−141.9° and 18.4 cm/−156.1°) reached tolerance but oscillated (hold_interruptions 240 and 218, max held 5 and 47).
- Training log for experiment 3: the transferred run started already near its ceiling (`success_rate` 1.0 and `ep_rew_mean` ≈124 at step 1024) and showed no task-progress trend over 120,832 steps: `success_rate` drifted 1.0 to 0.94 and `ep_rew_mean` 124 to 111, with `entropy_loss` rising from ≈−1.16 to ≈−0.15. This mirrors the unreliable training proxies seen in experiment 2 and provides no evidence of a separate, better policy in the run.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted. The confirmatory prediction was that full-range fine-tuning would lift measured success above the 96.2% pooled estimate toward 98%, with fewer failures at angles about −117 to −155 degrees and radii below 14 cm. Instead all three policies landed within 0.34 points of each other, the untouched parent was marginally best, and the same folded-branch angles failed at both small and large radii. The pre-stated alternative — a control/representation limitation at the shoulder-limit folded-elbow branch rather than a restricted-radius coverage artifact — is supported by this test. Limits: one fine-tuning run and one 600-episode panel; the two measured fine-tuned checkpoints are the run's endpoint and training-proxy extreme, so an unmeasured mid-run checkpoint is not covered; the folded-branch attribution remains a kinematic feasibility computation from the joint limits, not a causal diagnosis of the policy's internal state; the small success-rate differences (≤3 discordant episodes of 600) are within panel resolution.

**Interpretation:** Removing the training/deployment radius mismatch did not change the policy's behavior or its failure set. Because 19 of 23 failures persist across independently fine-tuned weights, including several at radii both recipes trained on, the residual deficit is not explained by training-distribution coverage. It is localized to the shoulder-limit folded-elbow branch, where the policy approaches the tolerance boundary only marginally and usually cannot sustain the 100-step hold. The measured plateau is behaviorally homogeneous at the episode level: the parent and the fine-tuned checkpoints disagree on at most three of 600 episodes. This redirects the next investigation to the folded-branch control/representation (for example hold-stability reward shaping near tolerance, or observations that expose joint-limit/branch geometry) rather than to the radius distribution, and it does not justify promoting an experiment-3 checkpoint or requesting terminal assessment.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted. The diagnostic proposition was that projecting the wrapped open shoulder onto the +/-170 degree range would recover folded-required success, move the previously-failing 176-180 degree band toward the shallow-sector level, and leave open-feasible success at 100%. Both stated contradicting conditions occurred: the 176-180 degree band stayed at 0/15, folded-required success overall fell (66.0% -> 44.0%/48.0%), and open-feasible success fell below 100% (950/950 -> 944/950 and 942/950). The pre-stated alternative - an intrinsic precision or optimization limit of the 64x64 policy in the folded configuration rather than the wrap seam - is consistent with this result, and the collateral open-feasible failures are an additional unexpected degradation the seam explanation does not predict. Limits: one transfer run and one training seed; one fresh 1000-episode panel; only the two training-proxy plateau extremes (`checkpoint-100352` and `checkpoint-120832`) of 24 candidates were measured, though late-plateau behavior has been homogeneous in prior runs and the two agree 8 vs 8 here; without an unchanged-continuation control the run cannot separate the representation change from ordinary weight drift under continued training; the folded/open partition and the seam bands are kinematic computations from recorded target geometry, not observations of the policy's internal state.

**Interpretation:** Removing the open-branch wrap seam did not help the hardest folded-required band and hurt behavior elsewhere, including open-feasible targets the projection never alters, so the seam representation is not the operative cause of the residual and the intervention should not be carried forward. The most consistent reading of the deep band is a precision limit: `working` failures there are near-misses against the 1 cm tolerance, and targets whose raw open shoulder wraps farther from the seam (raw < -184 degrees) succeed at 94.7%, so the failure is localized to the wrapped 176-180 degree band rather than to the folded configuration generally. Experiment 6 repeats the campaign pattern that editing the branch representation under transfer is high-variance: it reproduced the collateral loss seen in experiments 4 and 5, now extending to open-feasible targets, and the training proxies again disagreed with measured behavior. The 98.30% reading is `working`'s most favorable research panel, not a policy change; the same artifact now pools to 3313/3400 = 97.44% over six distinct research panels, still below the 98% objective with all 87 measured failures in the folded-required sector (folded-sector success 99/186 = 53.2%).

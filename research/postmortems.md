# Research postmortems

## 76a0e44c-15e1-4f0a-aad3-e75ef2d20c09 / Scientific strategy

**Direction:** Improve official-task generalization from the near-threshold baseline by addressing the persistent lower-left negative-angle failures through a control-precision or carefully normalized observation change, while preserving the baseline's learnability and hold/reach behavior.

**Lessons and limits:** The fresh PPO baseline trained on radii 14-20 cm, while the official task spans 6-20 cm (`robot_learning/scenario/environment.py`, `research/scenario.md`). The transferred full-coverage recipe did not demonstrate a reproducible task-performance gain, and the moderate hold-exit forfeiture transfer was substantially worse: on the identical 500-episode panel it scored 95.4%, 83.4%, and 85.6% at measured checkpoints versus 97.4% for the baseline parent, with more interruption failures and no reduction in no-reach failures. Its task-reference results were 96.0%, 84.0%, and 85.0%, below the baseline's 98.0% development result. The fresh target-coordinate observation run never exceeded 0% training proxy success; its final checkpoint scored 8.4% on research evaluation and 12.0% on task reference, with 447 of 458 research failures never reaching tolerance and failures spread beyond the lower-left cluster. The fresh sine/cosine branch-encoding run eventually reached 97% training proxy success at 95,232 steps, but both measured checkpoints scored 51.2% on the 500-episode research panel; task-reference scores were 51.5% and 54.5%. The challenger had 235 and 205 no-reach failures and 12 and 44 interrupted-hold failures, respectively, versus 6 no-reach and 8 interrupted failures for the baseline parent, and its failures were broad rather than concentrated only in the lower-left negative-angle cluster. Removing PPO entropy regularization in experiment 6 also failed: the transferred challenger scored 83.2% and 86.8% on the identical research panel versus 97.4% for the parent, with paired net losses of 71 and 53 episodes, and 80.5% and 86.5% on task-reference versus 98.0% for the parent. The final checkpoint partially recovered 3.6 research points from 86,016 steps, but its diagnostic failures remained broad and shifted from mostly interruptions (63) to many no-reach cases (38), rather than resolving the target failure mode. These results reject the tested 15-feature fresh recipe and the tested entropy-removal transfer, while not proving that every continuous angular encoding or control intervention is ineffective. Failures in the retained baseline remain concentrated at negative angles around -117 to -157 degrees and include both failure to reach tolerance and interrupted holds. Research evaluation provides a useful reach/hold diagnostic split; task-reference evaluation independently measures the protected task but reports only success and target geometry. Training success and reward are proxies, and all panels are development measurements rather than the official benchmark.

**Open questions:** Whether the persistent negative-angle no-reach failures arise from control precision or from a more narrowly normalized observation that preserves the baseline's learnability. The selected policy's performance on the official benchmark also remains unknown.

**Conditional next steps:** If research continues, restore the baseline recipe and test one targeted control-precision or minimally changed observation intervention, using the existing diagnostic split and a paired development panel. Do not retain the hold-forfeiture, direct-coordinate, tested sine/cosine, or entropy-removal recipes; require both baseline-like learnability and a substantial task-performance signal before task-reference measurement of a future challenger.

## 76a0e44c-15e1-4f0a-aad3-e75ef2d20c09 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a strong but not officially validated policy. `checkpoint-100352` achieved 97% on research evaluation and 98% on the independent task-reference development panel; `checkpoint-120832` achieved 97% on both.

**Observed behavior:** This was a fresh baseline with the default PPO recipe, not a changed recipe, continuation, or replication. The training proxy rose from 0% to 97% by 97,280 steps, then remained around 93-97% through 120,832 steps while proxy reward declined from its peak. On the identical 200-episode research panel, the two measured checkpoints both had 6 failures and the paired comparison had one win each with two discordant episodes. On the protected task-reference panel, the 100,352-step checkpoint had 4 failures (98%) and the final checkpoint had 6 failures (97%). The late-checkpoint task-reference failures were concentrated at radii 6.7-9.9 cm and angles -116 to -128 degrees. Research-evaluation failures similarly concentrated at negative angles; diagnostics showed both episodes that never reached tolerance and episodes that reached it but repeatedly interrupted the hold. The task-reference evaluator is independent of the researcher-owned environment and reward, but both panels are fixed development measurements.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The baseline hypothesis was to establish an initial reference for the human objective, and it is supported as a useful near-threshold reference. It does not establish that the objective is reached: the 98% task-reference result is one development panel, the research panel is 97%, and no official benchmark was run. The late checkpoint is preferred over the final checkpoint on the available task-reference evidence, but the difference is uncertain and does not support a causal claim about training duration.

**Interpretation:** The default recipe learns the task substantially, but its restricted 14-20 cm training distribution leaves residual generalization and hold-stability failures on the official 6-20 cm task. This pattern motivates changing training coverage; it is an observed association rather than proof that radius coverage caused the failures.

## 76a0e44c-15e1-4f0a-aad3-e75ef2d20c09 / Experiment 2

**Result:** Broadening transferred-policy training from radii 14-20 cm to the full official 6-20 cm range produced a near-threshold challenger but did not establish an improvement over the baseline parent. The baseline recipe remains the working and best-known lineage.

**Observed behavior:** This was a changed-recipe transfer experiment, not a continuation or replication. The training proxy rose from 96.6% at 5,120 steps to 100% at 24,576 and 105,472 steps, then ended at 94% at 120,832; these are training facts, not evaluation results. The 100,352-step challenger scored 98.0% on the 200-episode research panel, 97.4% on the 500-episode research panel, and 98.5% on the task-reference panel. The 105,472-step challenger scored 98.0% on both 200-episode panels, and the final 120,832-step challenger scored 98.0% on research evaluation and 98.5% on task reference. On the identical 500-episode panel, the challenger and the baseline parent both scored 97.4% with no discordant outcomes. The task-reference panel changed from baseline failures at seeds 7300, 7310, 7384, and 7402 to challenger failures at 7384, 7402, and 7475; the 100,352- and 120,832-step challengers had the same three failures. Detailed research diagnostics still place failures at negative lower-left angles, with short-radius and farther-radius cases and both no-reach and interrupted-hold outcomes.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The coverage hypothesis is weakened under the tested transfer, budget, and PPO conditions. The expected consistent reduction in short-radius/lower-left failures without sacrificing other behavior was not observed: the larger paired panel was exactly tied, while the fixed task-reference panel showed only a one-episode net improvement and a changed failure identity. The intervention may still alter failure trajectories, but the evidence supports no reliable task-performance gain and cannot establish that coverage caused either the small panel difference or the unchanged paired result.

**Interpretation:** The persistent failure geometry after adding short-radius training examples shifts the useful next investigation toward observation/representation, control precision, or hold stability. The 200-episode research results and 98.5% task-reference result make the challenger viable, but their panel-specific variation is insufficient to replace the established parent. No further measurement round is needed for this lineage decision; repeated development panels would not resolve the remaining causal uncertainty, and the campaign still has a scientifically useful next intervention.

**Observed behavior:** This was a changed-recipe transfer experiment, not a continuation or replication. The training proxy peaked at 98% around 30,720 steps, fell to 93% at 100,352 steps, and returned to 98% at 120,832; these are training facts rather than task evaluation. The failures still include the persistent negative-angle lower-left cluster, with additional failures at other angles in the degraded later checkpoints.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The hypothesis is contradicted under the tested transfer, reward coefficient, PPO recipe, and training budget. The expected reduction in incomplete holds was not observed: interruption failures increased rather than decreased, and overall success degraded on both the researcher-owned panel and the independent task-reference panel. The evidence does not establish a general causal explanation for the degradation because checkpoint quality and training trajectory also differ, but it is sufficient to reject this recipe as a useful path toward the objective. The short 35,840-step checkpoint was less degraded than the later challengers but still below the parent and does not support retaining the intervention.

**Interpretation:** A 0.5 forfeiture of accumulated hold capital did not solve the hold-stability problem and was associated with substantially worse learned policies in this transfer run. The diagnostic split strengthens the alternative that the persistent lower-left failures require observation or control changes, while the reward intervention may also have disturbed reach behavior and introduced broad instability. The task-reference measurements remain development evidence, not an official 98% verdict.

**Result:** The transferred entropy-removal recipe is rejected. The retained baseline remains the working and best-known lineage.

**Observed behavior:** This was a changed-recipe transfer experiment, not a continuation or replication: it resumed the working checkpoint and changed only `ppo.ent_coef` from 0.01 to 0.0. Training proxy success was baseline-like early, reached 100% at 30,720 steps, and ended at 95% at 120,832; these are training facts rather than task evaluation. On the identical 500-episode research panel, `checkpoint-86016` scored 83.2% and `checkpoint-120832` scored 86.8%, versus 97.4% for the measured working parent. The diagnostic split showed 21 no-reach and 63 interrupted failures at 86,016 steps, then 38 no-reach and 28 interrupted failures at 120,832, compared with 6 no-reach and 7 interrupted failures for the parent. On the independent 200-episode task-reference panel, the challenger scored 80.5% and 86.5%, versus 98.0% for the parent; its failures included both broad-angle no-reach cases and persistent lower-left failures.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The hypothesis is contradicted under the tested transfer, PPO recipe, and 120,000-step budget. The expected reduction in negative-angle no-reach and interrupted-hold failures, baseline-like task success, and task-reference performance did not occur. The later checkpoint's 3.6-point recovery over the earlier challenger is a partial training-trajectory signal, but it remains 10.6 points below the parent on research evaluation and 11.5 points below it on task reference. The evidence rejects this complete entropy-removal recipe as a useful path toward the objective; it does not prove that entropy can never be tuned usefully in another training design.

**Interpretation:** Removing entropy regularization did not address the residual control problem and was associated with broad degradation after transfer. The paired research results and matching task-reference degradation make additional measurement of this lineage disproportionate; no unmeasured checkpoint is a plausible replacement for the parent given the observed trajectory and measured endpoints. The result narrows the next intervention toward a direct control-precision or conservative observation change, while leaving the mechanism of the baseline's lower-left failures unresolved.

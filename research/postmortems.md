# Research postmortems

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The human objective is at least 98% official episode success. The fresh PPO baseline learned substantial reach-and-hold behavior, and its selected checkpoint-100352 remains the strongest measured lineage: it scored 96% on each of the disjoint seed-7500 and seed-7700 research panels, 97.5% on seed 7900, 97% on seed 8100, and 97% on the latest seed-8300 panel. Its 98% task-reference result is selection-contaminated. The full-support continuation tied the incumbent on seed 7900 and on the task-reference panel without an aggregate advantage. The later angular-oversampling continuation also failed to improve the incumbent on seed 8100: its measured checkpoints scored 95% and 97%. The retained experiment-3 checkpoint-110592 scored 96.5% on the latest seed-8300 panel versus 97% for the incumbent, with one paired incumbent win and no challenger wins. The geometry-bearing failures are reach failures concentrated in a negative-angle sector, but the current development evidence remains below or selection-contaminated relative to the official objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Training reward and training success are not reliable substitutes for task success: the full-support run reached a 1.0 training-success peak while its measured checkpoints tied the incumbent, and the angular run’s reward and proxy-success peaks did not transfer to a better task policy. The seed-7500, seed-7700, seed-7900, seed-8100, and seed-8300 research panels are disjoint and directly comparable; task-reference-v1 is fixed and selection-contaminated. The research evaluator emits no target geometry, so it cannot test a geometry-specific mechanism. In the geometry-bearing artifacts, residual failures truncated at 500 steps with final distance just above the 1 cm threshold; they were reach failures rather than observed hold interruptions. Full-support training changed one failure pattern without improving aggregate success, and angular oversampling did not establish a sector-specific benefit. The latest saved-lineage comparison removes the only retained checkpoint-selection uncertainty that could have changed the standing submission: the reward-proxy peak was not task-superior. This does not prove that no intervention could help, but it lowers the expected value of another ungrounded full training run relative to obtaining the official verdict.

**Open questions:** It is unknown whether the residual failures are primarily caused by radial support, angular/control behavior, hold stability, or learning-process variance. The standing policy’s official readiness remains uncertain because the best disjoint development result is 97.5% and the official benchmark has not been run. These uncertainties record the limits of the evidence and do not prescribe a next action.

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a strong but not yet objective-satisfying learned policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as working and best-known; checkpoint-110592 and checkpoint-115712 are retained as uncertain late-run alternatives. Terminal assessment is deferred.

**Observed behavior:** The run completed 120832 steps with no researcher-owned code or parameter changes. Training success was 0 through 70656 steps, rose to 0.71 at 90112, reached 0.97 at 97280-100352, then varied between 0.93 and 0.96 through the end. The reward proxy peaked at 163.85 at 86016 and was 160.85 at 90112, then was 117.32 at 100352 and 112.02 at 120832. On the reused task-reference-v1 panel, checkpoint-90112, checkpoint-100352, and checkpoint-120832 achieved 93%, 98%, and 97%. On the disjoint research-evaluation panel covering episodes 7500-7699, checkpoint-100352 achieved 192/200 (96%) and checkpoint-120832 achieved 190/200 (95%). The fresh-panel failure sets were not nested: checkpoint-100352 failed one episode that checkpoint-120832 passed, while checkpoint-120832 failed three episodes that checkpoint-100352 passed; seven failures were shared.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The baseline establishment objective is supported: the run supplies a reproducible saved-policy reference and measured task progress. The measurements also partially support the practical expectation that late training would produce useful task behavior, but they weaken any stronger inference from training proxies to the human objective. The reused 98% result is not independent confirmation, and the disjoint 96% result is below the 98% objective. Because this was a baseline rather than an intervention test, no causal claim about why checkpoint-100352 is better is warranted.

**Interpretation:** The policy has learned the core reach-and-hold behavior and is close to the objective on development measurements, but the independent panel shows residual failures and does not justify terminal assessment. Checkpoint-100352 is the best-supported aggregate choice among the measured candidates and should remain available for the next experiment. The two retained late alternatives preserve unresolved checkpoint-selection uncertainty without treating unmeasured candidates as failed.

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Experiment 2

**Result:** The full-support training intervention produced a useful but not superior measured policy. The incumbent remains working and best-known; the changed-radius checkpoint-105472 is retained as an alternative, and the experiment recipe is reverted.

**Observed behavior:** Experiment 2 continued the incumbent lineage while changing training target radii from 14-20 cm to 6-20 cm. Training success reached 0.99 at checkpoint-100352 and 1.0 at checkpoint-105472, then fell to 0.94 at checkpoint-120832; the reward proxy was 113.72, 110.18, and 110.99 at those checkpoints. On the disjoint research panel covering episodes 7900-8099, the incumbent, checkpoint-105472, and checkpoint-120832 each achieved 195/200 successes (97.5%). Both candidate-versus-incumbent paired comparisons had zero candidate wins, zero incumbent wins, and zero discordant episodes. All three policies failed the same episode seeds: 7955, 7988, 7999, 8015, and 8031. The research evaluator emitted no target radius or angle fields, so the requested inner-radius-specific outcome was not observed.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The diagnostic hypothesis is weakened. Expanding the training support did not improve aggregate success or change any success outcome relative to the incumbent on the fresh common panel, despite the candidate's 1.0 training-success peak. The result is not a complete refutation: it is one disjoint panel, and the evaluator did not expose target geometry with which to test the proposed inner-radius mechanism. The shared failures make an intervention-specific explanation of this panel unsupported.

**Interpretation:** The incumbent has stronger comparable evidence across three disjoint research panels (96%, 96%, and 97.5%) than checkpoint-105472 has alone, and the new candidate offers no paired task advantage. The 97.5% result is meaningful progress toward the human objective but remains below the 98% objective and cannot be attributed to the radius change because the control matched it exactly. Reverting the current recipe avoids treating an undemonstrated intervention as the default, while retaining checkpoint-105472 preserves a full-support policy with uncertain future value for later comparison or training.

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Experiment 3

**Observed behavior:** The angular-oversampling run completed 120832 steps. Training success was non-monotonic: it declined to 0.79 at checkpoint-80896, recovered to 0.94 at checkpoint-115712, and ended at 0.90 at checkpoint-120832. The reward proxy likewise varied, reaching 103.53 at checkpoint-110592 and ending at 100.10. On the new disjoint research panel covering episodes 8100-8299, the working policy achieved 194/200 successes (97%), checkpoint-115712 achieved 190/200 (95%), and checkpoint-120832 achieved 194/200 (97%). The paired comparison gave the working policy five versus one candidate wins for checkpoint-115712 across six discordant episodes, and one versus one for checkpoint-120832 across two discordant episodes. Each working-policy failure truncated at 500 steps; checkpoint-120832 had six failures as well, with a different failure set including one replacement failure. The fixed task-reference panel is reused development evidence, not independent confirmation.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The exploratory question is weakened. The fresh panel does not show an aggregate improvement from focused negative-angle coverage: checkpoint-115712 is 2 percentage points below working, while checkpoint-120832 ties it. This is task evidence against selecting the angular continuation over the incumbent under the tested evaluator, but it does not establish that angular coverage can never help. The late proxy recovery and non-monotonic reward are orthogonal signals; they did not identify a superior task policy. Because the research evaluator emits no target geometry, these results cannot determine whether the intervention changed the targeted negative-angle failures or introduced failures elsewhere.

**Interpretation:** The learned policies demonstrate substantial reach-and-hold progress but remain below the 98% human objective on this independent panel. The incumbent has the strongest accumulated comparable task evidence and remains the working lineage; checkpoint-120832 is worth preserving because it ties the incumbent while changing the individual failure pattern, and checkpoints 110592 and 115712 preserve unresolved late-run proxy/task-selection uncertainty. The observed measurements do not support a causal claim that angular oversampling caused either the tie or the regression. Reverting the experiment recipe avoids making an unconfirmed intervention the default, and the evidence is not sufficient to request the irreversible final benchmark.

**Result:** The incumbent remains working and best-known; the angular-oversampling recipe is reverted. Checkpoints 110592, 115712, and 120832 are retained as reusable alternatives, and no final benchmark is requested.

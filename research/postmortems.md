# Research postmortems

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The saved `working`/`best_known` lineage (`checkpoint-100352`) is a strong but sub-target policy: it reached 94.5% on the disjoint experiment-2 research panel and 530/560 on the pooled research panels, while the 98.0% task-reference result used for selection is not independent confirmation. The experiment-2 hold-progress forfeiture challengers reached only 81.5% and 81.0% on their shared panel, leaving the baseline as the strongest measured policy. The baseline was trained on target radii 14-20 cm even though the official distribution spans 6-20 cm.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies identify the learned transition but do not establish task success. Adding `HOLD_EXIT_FORFEIT_FRACTION=0.5` did not improve the trained policy: the measured checkpoints were 26.5 and 27.0 percentage points below the saved parent, and paired comparisons favored the parent by 27-to-1 and 28-to-1 discordant episodes. The baseline's failures include both missed reaches and interrupted holds, while the failed reward intervention also produced severe hold instability; this does not show that every hold intervention fails. Development panels remain non-final, and the fixed task-reference panel is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether exposing the policy to the full official target-radius range improves the underrepresented inner-radius cases while preserving outer-radius reach and hold behavior. The contribution of target coverage versus continued transfer training is not yet separated from this single proposed recipe.

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful but sub-target policy. `checkpoint-100352` is selected as the working and best-known lineage for continued development.

**Observed behavior:** Training success rose from zero to high values after approximately 75,000 steps. `checkpoint-86016` reached 93.125% on 160 research episodes and 94.0% on the fixed task-reference panel. `checkpoint-100352` reached 94.375% on the first 160-episode research panel, 95.0% on the disjoint 200-episode panel, and 98.0% on the fixed task-reference panel. `checkpoint-120832` reached 94.375%, 93.5%, and 97.0% on those corresponding panels. The fixed task-reference panel was used for candidate selection and is not independent confirmation.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Supported for establishing a strong baseline, but the evidence does not support claiming that the baseline satisfies the human objective: the best disjoint research result is 95.0%, below 98%. The measurements also indicate that continuing from `checkpoint-100352` to the final checkpoint was not beneficial under these conditions, although this is a checkpoint comparison rather than a causal attribution of late-training degradation.

**Interpretation:** `checkpoint-100352` is the best-supported saved policy because it generalizes better than the final checkpoint on the disjoint panel and wins the pooled paired comparison, while retaining the strongest task-reference score. It should anchor a subsequent training experiment aimed at the residual failures; no final benchmark is requested because development evidence does not justify expecting the objective-reaching verdict.

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Experiment 2

**Result:** The hold-progress forfeiture intervention weakened the trained challengers. The saved baseline remains the working and best-known lineage, and the experiment recipe should be reverted.

**Observed behavior:** On the new 200-episode research panel, the unchanged saved parent reached 189/200 successes (94.5%). `checkpoint-105472` reached 163/200 (81.5%) and `checkpoint-120832` reached 162/200 (81.0%). The paired comparisons favored the parent by 27-to-1 and 28-to-1 discordant episodes. The challengers frequently either failed to reach tolerance or entered tolerance and lost it after only 1-8 held steps; the final checkpoint also contained long interrupted-hold episodes.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted for the trained checkpoints tested. Setting `HOLD_EXIT_FORFEIT_FRACTION` to 0.5 did not improve complete uninterrupted success or preserve the baseline's behavior under continued training. This conclusion is limited to this intervention, training run, and research panel; it does not show that every hold-focused reward change will fail.

**Interpretation:** The intervention is not a useful successor to the baseline and should not be retained as a training parent. The baseline's 94.5% on a panel disjoint from its selection evidence is meaningful independent development evidence, but it remains below the 98% human objective, so no final assessment is warranted yet. Future training remains an ordinary next experiment after closure.

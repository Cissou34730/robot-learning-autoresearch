# Research postmortems

## c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 196 successes out of 200 official episodes on the unchanged full-range reach-and-hold task. The strongest saved policy, checkpoint-100352 (working and best-known), succeeds on 1945/2000 = 97.25% of two disjoint 1000-episode full-range panels (97.2% seed 6000, 97.3% seed 8000), with 99.5% on the reused seed-5000 selection panel and 98.0% on the reused task-reference panel. Later working controls measured 98.6% and 97.0% on seeds 11000 and 12000, so the larger development evidence remains below the objective. The deficit is not radial: every 6-20 cm radius band sits at 96.8-97.6%, while angles from -120 to +180 degrees are 98-100% and the lower-left sector carries nearly all failures, including 72.9% in [-150,-120) and 96.6% in [-180,-150). Of 55 failures, 28 never enter the 1 cm tolerance and 27 enter but hold fewer than 50 of the required 100 steps. The deficit is therefore in-distribution and angularly localized. The 50% lower-left curriculum and full hold-progress forfeiture interventions both failed to produce a durable measured improvement and were reverted. The lower-left joint-limit/IK-branch explanation remains a code-inspection hypothesis, not a measured mechanism.

**Lessons and limits:** The 14-20 cm training annulus was not the binding gap: near targets (6-14 cm) generalize at about 97.6%, no worse than the trained bands. Extended training of the same recipe gave no measured gain: checkpoint-100352 beats checkpoint-120832 on a shared 1000-episode panel (97.2% versus 96.2%) and every paired comparison (12-1 over 1200 episodes); checkpoints 115712 and 105472 also remain behind. Training proxies plateaued while measured failures stayed angularly structured, and proxy peaks for both challengers did not predict task success. The reward challenger briefly reduced interruptions but pooled at 97.75% versus 97.8% for working, then degraded to 81.4% at its final checkpoint. The baseline already oversampled -165 to -115 degrees at 25%; increasing that probability to 50% did not repair the sector and disturbed other sectors, so coverage alone is not established as the binding remedy. The current policy uses an 11-value observation containing joint state, end-effector error, and both analytic IK branches, with a direct two-action mapping; changing that representation would require a new semantic contract, while unchanged-recipe fresh training is semantically comparable. The large panels are development evidence with selection context, the task-reference panel is reused, and the angular decomposition is descriptive. Experiment 2 is invalid and unmeasured; its attempted curriculum change is neither a positive nor negative result.

**Open questions:** Whether a fresh run of the unchanged recipe can exceed the objective or reproduces the near-97% lower-left residual affects the decision between treating baseline performance as process variance and investing in a changed recipe. Whether never-enter failures arise from optimization, the analytic IK branches, or joint-limit geometry affects the decision between a training intervention and a representation change. Whether hold-boundary oscillation is a stable failure mode remains unresolved after the non-durable reward signal. These are uncertainties with different decision value, not a prescribed experiment queue.

## c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The automatic fresh PPO baseline trained to 120,832 steps. Its best measured checkpoint, checkpoint-100352, reaches 97.2-97.3% on two disjoint 1000-episode full-range panels and 98.0% on the reused task-reference panel, below the 98% objective. checkpoint-100352 is selected as the working policy and designated best-known; the baseline recipe is kept and the campaign continues.

**Observed behavior:** Training-time proxy from research/training_logs/c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461/experiment-1-attempt-1.log: success_rate rises from 0.06 at 75,776 steps to 0.71 at 90,112, crosses 0.9 by 94,208, then plateaus at 0.93-0.95 through 120,832; ep_rew_mean peaks near 168 at ~88,064 and falls to ~112 by 120,832 as successful episodes shorten (ep_len_mean ~500 to ~150). Measured results: checkpoint-100352 99.5% (200 ep seed 5000), 97.2% (1000 ep seed 6000), 97.3% (1000 ep seed 8000), 98.0% (task reference, 200 ep); checkpoint-120832 99.0% (200 ep seed 5000) and 96.2% (1000 ep seed 6000); checkpoint-95232 99.0% (200 ep seed 5000); checkpoint-115712 96.7% and checkpoint-105472 96.7% (1000 ep seed 8000). Paired comparisons all favor checkpoint-100352: vs checkpoint-120832 12 vs 1 over 1200 episodes, vs checkpoint-115712 7 vs 1, vs checkpoint-105472 6 vs 0 over 1000 episodes. Pooling checkpoint-100352 over the two 1000-episode panels (2000 distinct episodes): 1945 successes. By target angle, success is 100% for every 30-degree bin from -120 to +180 except [-180,-150) at 96.6% and [-150,-120) at 72.9%; by radius every 6-20 cm band is 96.8-97.6%. Failure structure: 28/55 never reached tolerance, 27/55 entered and lost it, all hold failures with max_held_steps below 50. Checkpoint-120832 and -115712 add hold-boundary oscillation failures at lateral/positive angles (e.g. seed 5013, many interruptions), and checkpoint-105472 shows four catastrophic low-radius misses with final distance 16.9-21.4 cm.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The automatic baseline carries no Researcher hypothesis, so this assesses the measurement-round questions that framed the evidence. The round-1 concern that the 14-20 cm training annulus would fail to generalize to the full 6-20 cm official distribution is weakened: the 6-10 and 10-14 cm bands succeed at ~97.6%, no worse than the trained 14-20 cm bands. The round-1/round-2 expectation that checkpoint-100352 is the strongest available policy on the full distribution is supported: it leads both disjoint 1000-episode panels and every paired comparison. The round-3 expectation that the unmeasured plateau checkpoints checkpoint-115712 or checkpoint-105472 would reduce the shared negative-angle residual is contradicted: both score 96.7% on the same panel, below checkpoint-100352, and exhibit the same negative-angle deficit. Overall the investigation outcome is partially supported to inconclusive at the objective level: the baseline is the best measured policy so far, but it remains ~0.7 points below the 98% objective and the originally hypothesized near-target generalization gap is not the binding deficit. Limits: the full-range estimates derive from panels that also ranked checkpoints, and the task-reference result is selection-contaminated; the angular attribution is descriptive.

**Interpretation:** The baseline learned a near-complete reach-and-hold solution whose remaining ~2.7% error is localized to the -120 to -160 degree target sector within the trained radius range, and appears as marginal reach misses (just outside the 1 cm tolerance) or fleeting tolerance contacts that never build a hold. Because the deficit sits inside the trained distribution and outside the near-target region, more training of the same recipe is unlikely to remove it: the training proxy plateaued with no improving trend after ~94,208 steps while the measured failures stayed angularly structured. The 98.0% task-reference result is higher than the ~97.3% of the larger panels, consistent with small-panel noise and panel reuse rather than independent confirmation. checkpoint-100352 is a sound working and best-known baseline, and reaching the 98% objective appears to require a next experiment that explicitly targets the negative-angle sector, not merely extended training.

## c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461 / Experiment 3

**Result:** The 50% lower-left curriculum intervention did not improve the measured policy. The transferred working policy remains the best-known lineage, and the changed recipe is reverted.

**Observed behavior:** The experiment-3 candidate inventory reports a strong early training proxy for checkpoint-5120 (0.96875 training success, 117.45 mean training reward), followed by lower proxy values at later checkpoints; checkpoint-120832 reports 0.85 training success and 99.27 mean training reward. These are training-time proxies, not task measurements. On the same disjoint research-evaluation panel of 1,000 episodes with seed 10000, working succeeded on 972/1000 (97.2%), checkpoint-5120 on 964/1000 (96.4%), and checkpoint-120832 on 918/1000 (91.8%). Paired comparisons favored working by 0 to 8 discordant wins against checkpoint-5120 and by 1 to 55 against checkpoint-120832. The diagnostic panel showed no lower-left repair: working versus checkpoint-5120 versus checkpoint-120832 succeeded in the [-180,-150) degree bin at 96.3%, 90.2%, and 81.7%, and in [-150,-120) at 71.1%, 68.7%, and 63.9%. The final challenger also introduced unexpected failures outside the target sector, including 69.7% success in [150,180) degrees and 81.7% and 82.7% success in the 6-8 cm and 8-10 cm radius bands, compared with working's 100%, 97.2%, and 95.0% in those bins. The early proxy peak therefore did not translate into task success; hold interruptions and near-target failures were orthogonal warning signals in the saved-policy diagnostics.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The hypothesis is weakened under this transferred recipe and training budget. The expected observation of improved lower-left success with preserved broad-distribution performance was not observed: both measured challengers were below working on the shared panel, and the final checkpoint regressed in positive-angle and near-target sectors. This does not establish that coverage can never help or prove the joint-limit/IK explanation; it shows that increasing this unchanged angular curriculum probability from 0.25 to 0.50 did not produce a useful policy in the tested run. The early training proxy is not evidence of progress toward the human objective. The 97.2% working result is also below the 98% objective, so this closure selects the best measured development policy rather than declaring the objective reached; the reused task-reference panel is not used as independent confirmation.

**Interpretation:** Stronger sampling of the already targeted angular range was insufficient to repair the residual lower-left behavior and was associated with broader degradation by the final checkpoint. The shared panel is directly comparable for the three policies and provides measured evidence against retaining either experiment-3 challenger, while the single transferred recipe does not identify whether the remaining deficit is geometric, representational, reward-related, or optimization-related. Reverting preserves the measured 97.2-97.3% working baseline and leaves future work free to test a less disruptive explanation.

## c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461 / Experiment 4

**Result:** Full forfeiture of accumulated hold-progress reward did not produce a durable measured improvement. The working lineage remains the best-known policy, the changed reward recipe is reverted, and development continues below the official objective.

**Observed behavior:** The altered-reward run reached a training proxy of 1.0 success and 103.8 mean reward at checkpoint-5120, then declined to 0.92 and 94.7 by checkpoint-120832. On the seed-11000 research panel, checkpoint-5120 succeeded on 987/1000 episodes (98.7%) versus working's 986/1000 (98.6%), with 250 versus 489 total hold interruptions and 5 versus 8 interruption-bearing failures. The later checkpoint-120832 achieved only 814/1000 (81.4%) and had 3,292 total interruptions and 177 interruption-bearing failures. On the disjoint seed-12000 panel, checkpoint-5120 succeeded on 968/1000 (96.8%) versus working's 970/1000 (97.0%); it had 260 versus 17 total interruptions and 20 versus 19 never-enter failures. In the lower-left [-150,-120) degree sector, it achieved 67.1% versus working's 69.5%. Across the two new panels, checkpoint-5120 therefore achieved 1955/2000 (97.75%) versus working's 1956/2000 (97.8%), with the paired comparison favoring working by 2 to 1 discordant wins. These are development measurements; the official panel was not run, and the other 22 experiment-4 checkpoints remain unmeasured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The confirmatory hypothesis is weakened. Its expected observation appeared on the first panel as a one-success advantage with fewer interruptions, but the disjoint panel did not reproduce either a success advantage or the interruption reduction. The pooled result is essentially tied and slightly favors working, while the lower-left residual and never-enter failures were not repaired. This weakens the claim that full hold-progress forfeiture is a useful route under this transferred recipe and training run. It does not establish that reward shaping cannot help, nor does it distinguish the remaining reach, IK-geometry, representation, and optimization alternatives.

**Interpretation:** The intervention changed the failure mode in a panel-dependent way rather than improving complete reach-and-hold behavior. The first-panel interruption reduction was a partial signal, but the disjoint panel exposed its lack of stability and the early proxy peak was not a reliable policy-quality indicator. Continuing from the altered recipe is not justified by these measurements; reverting preserves the stronger working baseline while leaving the residual lower-left mechanism unresolved.

## c92ccfe2-1135-4ec4-bc5a-eded729fc461 / Experiment 5

**Result:** The fresh unchanged PPO recipe did not reproduce the near-97% working policy. The measured fresh checkpoints achieved 15.1% and 17.4% on a new 1,000-episode panel, while the working control achieved 96.1%. The objective is not reached; the working lineage remains the evidence-backed policy and the unchanged recipe is kept for the next experiment.

**Observed behavior:** The experiment-5 training log shows `ep_rew_mean` increasing from -3.57 at 1,024 steps to 31.7 at 120,832 steps, while `success_rate` remained 0 and `ep_len_mean` remained 500. On the disjoint seed-13000 panel, checkpoint-100352 succeeded on 151/1000 episodes and checkpoint-120832 on 174/1000; the same-panel working control succeeded on 961/1000. The final fresh checkpoint had 787 never-enter failures, 39 episodes that entered tolerance but failed the hold, and 2,261 hold interruptions; the mid-run checkpoint had 804, 45, and 2,006 respectively, versus 16, 23, and 501 for working. The fresh final checkpoint had no successes in the 6-14 cm radius bins and failures across many angle bins, rather than only the working policy's lower-left residual. These are development measurements on one disjoint panel; the 22 other fresh checkpoints remain unmeasured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The confirmatory hypothesis is contradicted under this fresh initialization and training budget. The expected near-97% success and concentrated lower-left residual did not appear at either measured checkpoint; the late checkpoint's rising training proxy also did not translate into task success. The alternative that fresh initialization can produce materially different behavior is supported by this run, but one replication does not quantify initialization variance or show that no fresh run can reach the objective. The working control on the same panel makes a panel-wide explanation for the fresh run's low scores implausible, without establishing a causal mechanism for the learning failure.

**Interpretation:** The saved fresh policies are not useful lineage replacements and do not justify measuring the remaining unmeasured checkpoints before closure: the same-step fresh checkpoint, the highest-proxy final checkpoint, and a directly measured working control already answer the replication question for this run. The result supports prioritizing a changed scientific intervention over another unchanged fresh baseline, while leaving the mechanism of the broad fresh-run failure unresolved. The measured working policy remains below the official 98% objective, so this closure is a development decision rather than terminal assessment.

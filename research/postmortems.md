# Research postmortems

## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the unchanged official two-joint-arm task. Experiment 2 transferred the retained PPO policy while sampling the full official radius range. Its checkpoint-100352 and endpoint achieved 97.0% on the researcher panel and 98.5% on the protected task-reference panel, a small fixed-panel improvement over the prior retained policy, but neither established robust objective attainment across development panels. Checkpoint-100352 is now the strongest measured development policy, with terminal readiness unresolved.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, rather than logged reward or training success, is the policy-progress signal. The experiment-2 training proxy and reward declined from 0.99 and 114 at checkpoint-100352 to 0.94 and 111 at the endpoint, while measured success stayed unchanged on the researcher panel and unchanged on the task-reference panel after checkpoint-100352. On the fixed task-reference panel, full-radius training reduced the retained policy's four failures to three and raised success from 98.0% to 98.5%, but two short-radius failures around -123 to -128 degrees and one 18.2 cm failure remained. The researcher panel still had six failures at seeds 11, 25, 111, 167, 171, and 188, all reaching 500 steps. These results partially support missing radius coverage as a contributor, without separating its effect from transfer, optimization, or angular-control effects. The two 200-episode development panels are distinct from the official final assessment.

**Open questions:** The residual negative-angle failures, including short-radius cases, remain unresolved. It is unknown whether a method focused on angular control or optimization can improve the researcher panel without sacrificing the task-reference result, and whether the fixed-panel gain generalizes to the official final assessment.

## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline learned a useful near-objective policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as the working and best-known policy; the scientific recipe is kept, and terminal assessment is deferred.

**Observed behavior:** The run completed 120,832 steps against a requested 120,000. The training log's proxy success was 0% through 70,656 steps, rose to 98% at 99,328 steps, was 97% at 100,352, and was 95% at the endpoint; logged episode reward likewise peaked earlier and was lower at the endpoint. Research evaluation measured checkpoint-86016 at 93.5%, checkpoint-100352 at 97.0% (194/200), and checkpoint-120832 at 96.5% (193/200). The protected task-reference panel measured the same checkpoints at 94.0% (188/200), 98.0% (196/200), and 97.0% (194/200). On that shared protected panel, the endpoint lost both discordant episodes against checkpoint-100352 and had no wins. The four checkpoint-100352 protected-panel failures all had target radii below 10 cm and angles between -116 and -128 degrees; all four episodes ran to 500 steps, with final distances from 0.986 to 1.325 cm. Research-evaluation failures for checkpoint-100352 also ran to 500 steps, and at least one failed endpoint episode retained a high positive reward, showing a proxy/task mismatch.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline established strong learned task behavior and identified a measured late checkpoint near the objective, but it did not demonstrate reliable attainment of the human objective: one distinct development panel was 2 percentage points below 98%, and the endpoint regressed on both panels. The baseline result supports checkpoint selection and further development, while the limited panels do not support a terminal readiness claim.

**Interpretation:** Training improved the learned policy substantially, but continuing to the saved endpoint was not beneficial for measured task success. The agreement between the two panels that checkpoint-100352 is better than the endpoint supports selecting 100352, while the panel disagreement about whether it reaches 98% leaves meaningful uncertainty. The concentrated protected-panel failures suggest a useful next investigation around short-radius targets in the negative-angle region, without establishing that this is the sole cause of all failures.

## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Experiment 2

**Result:** Partially supported. Full-radius transfer training produced a useful candidate and a modest fixed task-reference improvement, but did not resolve the researcher-panel gap or establish terminal readiness. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as the working and best-known policy; the expanded-radius recipe is kept and terminal assessment is deferred.

**Observed behavior:** The run completed 120,832 steps against a requested 120,000. The raw training log's proxy success and episode reward were 0.99 and 114 at checkpoint-100352, then 0.94 and 111 at the endpoint. Research evaluation measured checkpoint-25600, checkpoint-100352, and checkpoint-120832 at 97.0% (194/200) each. Their six researcher-panel failures were the same episode seeds (11, 25, 111, 167, 171, and 188), and each ran to 500 steps. The task-reference panel measured checkpoint-25600 at 98.0% (196/200) and both checkpoint-100352 and checkpoint-120832 at 98.5% (197/200). Relative to the experiment-1 retained checkpoint's four task-reference failures, the later experiment-2 checkpoints succeeded on the 6.7 cm, -116.4 degree case and the 7.2 cm, -125.4 degree case, but still failed at 9.4 cm/-127.9 degrees, 9.9 cm/-122.9 degrees, and 18.2 cm/-154.8 degrees.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The predicted reduction in short-radius failures occurred on the protected development panel and broad success was preserved or modestly improved there. However, the researcher-panel success did not improve, two short-radius negative-angle failures remained, and the endpoint did not improve on the selected checkpoint. This supports radius coverage as a useful but incomplete contributor under this transferred run; it does not establish that radius coverage caused the improvement or that the result generalizes beyond the development panels.

**Interpretation:** The measured policy remains near the human objective but is not robustly demonstrated at 98% across development evidence. The fixed task-reference gain justifies retaining the full-radius training recipe and selecting checkpoint-100352, while the unchanged researcher-panel result and concentrated negative-angle failures leave angular control or optimization as live explanations. Since later training degraded logged dynamics without changing measured success, the earlier checkpoint is preferable to the endpoint. No additional measurement is needed to resolve the lineage decision, and terminal assessment is not requested.

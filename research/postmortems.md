## 10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb / Scientific strategy

**Direction:** The stability-reward investigation is resolved: transferring the retained baseline with a bounded, angle-independent bonus did not repair the negative-angle robustness gap and produced broad reach regressions. The next direction is a transfer-compatible observation or representation change aimed at the no-reach component of the gap while preserving the baseline's hold reliability.

**Lessons and limits:** The baseline checkpoint-100352 achieved 97.75% pooled research success across two matched panels, with nine failures concentrated around -121 to -150 degrees, establishing a concrete robustness gap (`research/brief.md`; `research/evaluations/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb`). On the compatible seed-2000 panel, it had 5 failures, all in the focus sector. Experiment 2 rejected focused exposure after 8 focus-sector and 3 outside-sector failures at 15,360 steps and 9 focus-sector and 9 outside-sector failures at 120,832 steps (`research/evaluations/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb`). Experiment 3 established that fresh PPO realization variance can produce a globally failing policy, so transfer remains appropriate when testing a mechanism (`research/postmortems.md`; `research/training_logs/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb/experiment-3-attempt-1.log`). In experiment 4, the stability bonus reached 92.5% at 35,840 steps, then 81.5% at both 100,352 and 120,832 steps on the matched panel. Failures grew from 15 to 37, and no-reach failures from 4 to 21 and 28; the later reduction in hold interruptions did not recover the lost reach reliability (`research/evaluations/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb`; `research/training_logs/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb/experiment-4-attempt-1.log`). These results contradict the expected reduction in negative-angle failures without collateral loss and reject this reward shaping for the current recipe. All measurements are development evidence, not the official objective verdict.

**Open questions:** It remains uncertain whether a representation that makes target geometry or angular structure easier to use can remove the recurring no-reach failures without disturbing hold control. The campaign also has insufficient evidence to characterize the seed-to-seed frequency of successful interventions, but that question should be reopened only when seed robustness changes a concrete model decision.

**Conditional next steps:** Use the retained baseline as the parent for a transfer-compatible observation or representation intervention, keeping the fixed target distribution and matched research evaluation. If no-reach failures in the negative-angle sector fall while hold interruptions and full-range success remain near baseline, pursue that representation; if reach remains unchanged, investigate policy capacity or optimization rather than another hold-only reward; if failures broaden again, reject the representation and reconsider whether the remaining gap is primarily stochastic. Do not repeat the exposure-only sampler or this stability bonus without a materially different mechanism question.

## 10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb / Experiment 2

**Result:** The focused-angle sampling intervention is rejected. The baseline checkpoint-100352 remains the working and best-known lineage, and the campaign should continue without requesting the terminal benchmark.

**Observed behavior:** The intervention's training success was 0.93 at 5,120 steps, declined to 0.77-0.85 through most checkpoints, and ended at 0.80. On the matched seed-2000 research panel, checkpoint-15360 reached 94.5% with 8 failures in the -150 to -120 degree focus sector and 3 failures outside it. Most intervention failures were reach failures rather than failures after reaching the hold region.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The recorded expectation was fewer negative-angle reach and hold failures while maintaining or improving reliability elsewhere. The recorded contradicting observation was similar focus-sector failures or a material decline outside the sector. Both contradicting outcomes occurred: focus-sector failures increased from 5 to 8 and 9, while new outside-sector failures appeared and grew from 3 to 9. The hypothesis is contradicted for this transferred run and recipe. The fixed-distribution evidence resolves the exposure-only investigation, although it cannot by itself distinguish a systematic limitation from PPO training variance.

**Interpretation:** Focused exposure did not repair the diagnosed geometric gap and progressively damaged full-range reach-and-hold behavior. The model decision is therefore to restore the established baseline recipe and retain its checkpoint-100352 lineage. The next scientific direction changes to a mechanism-focused observation or reward intervention; a fresh replication is justified only if the next decision specifically requires separating mechanism limits from stochastic training variation.

The baseline remains the strongest measured policy, while the next useful direction changes to a representation or observation intervention that directly targets no-reach failures. A fresh replication is not needed before that intervention because experiment 3 already established variance as a competing explanation and transfer isolates the next mechanism more directly; replication should be reserved for a later decision that specifically requires seed-robustness evidence.

**Lessons and limits:** Task success, rather than training reward or training success, supports policy selection. Candidate-c3c84d52 had the highest observed training reward but 94% task-reference success; candidate-f68b1643 was the final saved checkpoint and scored 97%; candidate-d918a3be scored 98% on both measured panels. The available development evidence does not establish whether the concentrated negative-angle failures recur under the official draw or whether further training would improve or degrade them.

## 05b467a1-a612-4916-b6dc-29cc31e0d3f4 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline learned a policy that met the 98% development objective on the measured 100352-step checkpoint, and that checkpoint is ready for official assessment.

**Observed behavior:** Training completed 120832 steps with PPO and saved 24 checkpoints. The selected candidate-d918a3be at 100352 steps had training_success 0.97 and ep_rew_mean 117.32. On task-reference-v1 it achieved 196/200 successes (98.0%); its four failures were all 500-step episodes with target angles -116.41, -125.40, -122.90, and -127.91 degrees and target radii between 6.73 and 9.91 cm. On the distinct-seed research evaluation (seed 9000, 200 episodes) it also achieved 98.0% (196/200). The highest logged reward was candidate-c3c84d52 at 86016 steps (ep_rew_mean 163.85), but its task-reference result was 94.0%; the final candidate-f68b1643 at 120832 steps scored 97.0% on task-reference-v1. Training logs show success_rate rising to 0.98 around 99328 steps and ending at 0.95, while ep_rew_mean declined from about 168 around 87040-88064 steps to 112 at 120832.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Supported for baseline establishment: the unchanged fresh recipe produced a policy with measured task performance at the campaign objective on two development evaluation settings. The result does not establish official success, generalization beyond those panels, or a causal explanation for the different checkpoint outcomes. Training proxies were only partially informative because their peaks did not coincide with the strongest measured task behavior, and unmeasured checkpoints remain unmeasured rather than failed.

**Interpretation:** The baseline demonstrates substantial learned task competence and provides a defensible candidate for terminal assessment. The agreement between the protected task-reference panel and the distinct-seed research evaluation increases confidence in the saved artifact, while the four clustered task-reference failures and the later proxy/task decline preserve meaningful uncertainty. No intervention was tested, so the evidence supports selecting this artifact, not attributing its performance to a particular mechanism.

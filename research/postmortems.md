# Research postmortems

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The retained 64x64 PPO checkpoint-100352 remains the strongest supported policy, with 1122/1160 successes across six disjoint research panels and 197/200 on the latest panel. The 98.0% task-reference result used for selection is not independent confirmation, and development panels never establish the official result. Hold-forfeiture and full-radius transfer changes produced no gain, while a fresh 128x128 policy and a fresh unchanged-recipe run were substantially worse. The policy is capable but variable, and the available evidence does not establish the 98% objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies identify the learned transition but do not establish task success. The tested reward, target-coverage, capacity, and fresh-replication interventions did not improve the saved policy; the width change lost 105-to-1 and 108-to-1 paired comparisons, the full-radius challengers tied the control at 197/200 with no discordant outcomes, and the replication lost to the control by 117-to-0 and 94-to-0 discordant outcomes. The replication result weakens claims that the unchanged recipe reliably reproduces the selected policy, but one fresh seed does not isolate all sources of training variance. Recent control diagnostics contain both never-reached-tolerance failures and interrupted holds, while the comparison of checkpoint-100352 with checkpoint-120832 indicates that ordinary continued training can degrade behavior. Development panels remain non-final, and the fixed task-reference panel is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether conservative continued optimization can improve the strong 64x64 policy without repeating late-training degradation, and whether such an improvement can make success reliably at least 98% across panels. The source of the remaining reach and hold failures, and the extent to which the poor replication reflects ordinary fresh-training variance versus a limitation of the unchanged recipe, also remain unresolved.

The baseline's 94.5% on a panel disjoint from its selection evidence is meaningful independent development evidence, but it remains below the 98% human objective, so no final assessment is warranted yet.

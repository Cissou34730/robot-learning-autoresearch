# Research postmortems

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The baseline learned a useful but sub-objective reach-and-hold controller. Checkpoint-100352 remains the strongest measured lineage, with 927/960 successes across five disjoint research panels, including 199/200 on the latest panel. The full-target-radius intervention achieved 118/200 at both measured checkpoints, and the branch-margin observation intervention achieved 44/200 and 48/200. Baseline failures recur in a narrow lower-left target-angle region and include both failure to enter tolerance and brief entry followed by exit, while the experiment-3 challengers show much broader approach and hold failures. The evidence remains consistent with coupled geometry, posture selection, approach, settling, and hold behavior without isolating one cause.

**Lessons and limits:** Training reward and training success tracked the emergence of competence but did not establish the human objective. The experiment-2 proxy rose from 0.49 at checkpoint-100352 to 0.60 at checkpoint-120832, while both complete-task measurements stayed at 59.0%; each lost all 79 discordant paired episodes to the baseline reference at 98.5%. In experiment 3, reward rose from about 23.7 to 73.2 and training success from 0.0 to 0.01, but complete success remained 22.0% and 24.0%; the matched challenger lost 156 of 157 discordant episodes to the baseline and the final challenger lost 152 of 153. The branch-margin hypothesis is therefore contradicted under its tested fresh initialization, recipe, checkpoints, and panel, but the result does not disprove branch-related causes generally or identify whether the broad failures arose from representation, learning variance, trajectory, settling, or hold regulation. The current instrumentation does not establish inverse-kinematic branch choice, joint-limit margin, velocity, or braking causality, and development evidence remains insufficient to declare the 98% objective.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved how target radius and angle, the two joint-limit-constrained inverse-kinematic postures, direct-torque trajectory and velocity, settling, and sustained local regulation interact in the residual failures. It is also unresolved whether the recurring angle band reflects posture feasibility, learned representation, trajectory generation, or their interaction, and whether complete-success changes would come from fewer tolerance-entry failures, fewer hold interruptions, or a trade between them. The measured experiment-3 failures do not distinguish these behavioral alternatives or establish how the baseline would perform beyond the recorded development panels.

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a useful learned policy but did not demonstrate the human objective. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as the working and best-known lineage for this closure.

**Observed behavior:** Checkpoint-100352 achieved 94.375%, 94.5%, and 95.5% on three disjoint research panels, totaling 531/560 successes. It achieved 98.0% on the reused fixed task-reference panel. Checkpoint-120832 achieved 94.375% and 93.5% on two disjoint panels, while checkpoint-110592 achieved 94.5% on the third panel and lost the paired comparison to checkpoint-100352 by two discordant episodes. The late checkpoints therefore remained below the 98% objective on fresh research panels despite high training-time proxy values.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline established substantial reach-and-hold competence and produced a measurable candidate for future work, but it did not establish a policy satisfying the 98% objective. The conclusion is limited to this training recipe, these saved checkpoints, and the recorded development panels; it does not explain the residual failures or rule out future improvements.

**Interpretation:** The policy commonly reaches and holds the target, but the complete behavior remains unreliable across the full target distribution. The failure diagnostics show both failure to enter the tolerance region and failure to sustain an entered tolerance region, consistent with the Scientific model's coupled approach, settling, and hold constraints. Since the additional late checkpoint did not improve the measured leader, the available evidence supports resolving lineage around checkpoint-100352 without treating its favorable fixed-panel score as independent confirmation.

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Experiment 2

**Result:** The full official target-radius training distribution did not improve complete reach-and-hold success and is closed without selecting either challenger.

**Observed behavior:** On the fresh research panel covering episode seeds 4800-4999, checkpoint-100352 and checkpoint-120832 each achieved 118/200 successes (59.0%). The retained baseline achieved 197/200 (98.5%) on the same panel; each challenger lost all 79 discordant paired episodes to that reference, and the challengers were tied on every paired outcome. Training proxies increased from 0.49 to 0.60 success between the measured checkpoints, but this did not correspond to improved complete-task behavior. The detailed challenger traces contain both episodes that never enter tolerance and episodes that enter without completing the uninterrupted hold.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted under the tested fresh initialization, training recipe, checkpoints, and panel. Training on the full official radius interval did not produce the predicted overall complete-success improvement, and the available result does not show an inner-radius benefit; both measured challengers were 39.5 percentage points below the baseline reference. The conclusion is bounded by the single fresh evaluation panel and does not identify which coupled physical behavior caused the regression or rule out a different intervention.

**Interpretation:** The unchanged baseline remains the useful working and best-known lineage, while the experiment-2 radius change is not supported as a path toward the human objective. The unchanged result between matched and final challenger checkpoints indicates that the higher training proxy and additional late training did not recover the measured policy behavior. Because success couples approach, tolerance entry, settling, and sustained regulation, the 59% outcome should not be assigned to radius coverage alone as a single isolated stage failure.

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Experiment 3

**Result:** The branch-margin observation intervention was contradicted on the fresh disjoint panel and is closed without selecting or retaining either challenger. The retained baseline remains the working and best-known lineage.

**Observed behavior:** On episode seeds 5000-5199, the matched experiment-3 checkpoint-100352 achieved 44/200 successes (22.0%) and the final checkpoint-120832 achieved 48/200 (24.0%), while the retained baseline achieved 199/200 (99.5%). The baseline won 156 of 157 discordant episodes against checkpoint-100352 and 152 of 153 against checkpoint-120832. The challengers' increasing training reward and small training-success proxy did not correspond to complete reach-and-hold competence. Their diagnostics include many episodes that never entered tolerance, especially in difficult angle regions, and episodes that entered but repeatedly lost the uninterrupted hold.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted under the tested fresh initialization, scientific recipe, checkpoints, and disjoint research panel. Explicit signed branch joint-limit margins did not improve complete task success and instead produced a large regression relative to the retained baseline. The result weakens the explanation that missing explicit branch-feasibility information was the principal actionable cause of the recurring failures, but it does not isolate the cause of the challenger regression or rule out other branch, trajectory, settling, or hold mechanisms.

**Interpretation:** The 13-component observation did not preserve the baseline's coupled approach, tolerance entry, settling, and sustained 100-step hold behavior in this run. The late challenger was only slightly better than the matched challenger on the measured panel, so higher reward and additional training did not recover the intervention's task performance. Restoring the baseline recipe preserves the only lineage with substantial multi-panel competence; its 99.5% result on this panel is development evidence, not independent confirmation of the 98% official objective.

# Research postmortems

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The human objective is at least 98% official episode success. The fresh PPO baseline learned substantial reach-and-hold behavior, but checkpoint-100352 reached only 96% on each of two disjoint 200-episode research panels; its 98% task-reference result is selection-contaminated. It remains the strongest measured lineage. The retained late checkpoints did not improve that result: checkpoint-110592 tied it at 96% on the preparation panel, while checkpoint-115712 reached 95%. The measured policy is therefore useful but not terminally validated. Independent-panel failures repeatedly concentrate in a negative-angle sector, and several are at radii below the current 14 cm training lower bound, leaving a plausible mismatch between training support and the official 6-20 cm task.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward rose sharply late in the run, but reward peaked around 86016-90112 steps while task success continued improving, and later continuation did not improve the measured policy. The task-reference panel is selection-contaminated; the seed-7500 and seed-7700 research panels are disjoint and directly comparable. On the seed-7500 panel, checkpoint-100352 had eight failures and checkpoint-120832 had ten; on seed 7700, the working and 110592-step policies each had eight failures and 115712 had ten. The preparation comparison found no discordant wins for 110592 versus the working policy and two wins for the working policy versus 115712. The current training environment samples radii 14-20 cm while the protected task samples 6-20 cm; this is compatible with the same target-conditioned 11-element observation and two-action representation, but the benefit of adding inner-radius support is untested. These measurements describe saved policies under the recorded evaluator and do not establish a causal explanation for the angular failure concentration or any intervention.

**Open questions:** Whether expanding training support to the official inner radii improves generalization, especially within the repeatedly difficult angular sector, remains unresolved. It is also unknown whether the residual failures are primarily caused by radial support, angular/control behavior, or hold stability. The standing policy's official readiness remains uncertain because development measurements are below 98% and the official benchmark has not been run. The late proxy-selected alternatives are not currently supported as better parents by task outcomes.

## e88f7b9e-b5cb-4f9b-86c6-4115ca3a0f2b / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a strong but not yet objective-satisfying learned policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as working and best-known; checkpoint-110592 and checkpoint-115712 are retained as uncertain late-run alternatives. Terminal assessment is deferred.

**Observed behavior:** The run completed 120832 steps with no researcher-owned code or parameter changes. Training success was 0 through 70656 steps, rose to 0.71 at 90112, reached 0.97 at 97280-100352, then varied between 0.93 and 0.96 through the end. The reward proxy peaked at 163.85 at 86016 and was 160.85 at 90112, then was 117.32 at 100352 and 112.02 at 120832. On the reused task-reference-v1 panel, checkpoint-90112, checkpoint-100352, and checkpoint-120832 achieved 93%, 98%, and 97%. On the disjoint research-evaluation panel covering episodes 7500-7699, checkpoint-100352 achieved 192/200 (96%) and checkpoint-120832 achieved 190/200 (95%). The fresh-panel failure sets were not nested: checkpoint-100352 failed one episode that checkpoint-120832 passed, while checkpoint-120832 failed three episodes that checkpoint-100352 passed; seven failures were shared.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The baseline establishment objective is supported: the run supplies a reproducible saved-policy reference and measured task progress. The measurements also partially support the practical expectation that late training would produce useful task behavior, but they weaken any stronger inference from training proxies to the human objective. The reused 98% result is not independent confirmation, and the disjoint 96% result is below the 98% objective. Because this was a baseline rather than an intervention test, no causal claim about why checkpoint-100352 is better is warranted.

**Interpretation:** The policy has learned the core reach-and-hold behavior and is close to the objective on development measurements, but the independent panel shows residual failures and does not justify terminal assessment. Checkpoint-100352 is the best-supported aggregate choice among the measured candidates and should remain available for the next experiment. The two retained late alternatives preserve unresolved checkpoint-selection uncertainty without treating unmeasured candidates as failed.

# Research postmortems

## 4b17531f-ff44-4b08-997f-e703fc28eb8e / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The PPO baseline learned competent reach-and-hold behavior, but the best characterized checkpoint remains below the 98% human objective on development panels. The working checkpoint-100352 remains strongest, with 152/160 on the latest disjoint panel and 609/640 pooled across four disjoint research panels. The full-coverage experiment's matched-step, proxy-peak, and final candidates each reached only 151/160 on that panel, while the parent won the only discordant episode. The working policy's latest failures still include inner-radius no-reach cases, and the fixed task-reference panel's four failures were also at inner radii from 6.73 to 9.91 cm. The reduced-learning-rate continuation reached only 151/160 at its proxy peak and 147/160 at its final checkpoint.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward identify useful late-training regions but do not establish the required task success. Broadening target sampling from 14-20 cm to the full 6-20 cm range did not improve measured behavior in this transfer run: the intervention candidates were 0.625 percentage points below the parent on the disjoint panel, with no candidate win in paired comparisons. This weakens the coverage-only explanation for the residual failures, but does not disprove that a different curriculum or optimization recipe could help. The lower learning rate also did not preserve the parent behavior: the parent won 4-0 discordant episodes against checkpoint-95232 and 8-0 against checkpoint-120832. Development measurements are not the official 200-episode assessment, and unmeasured checkpoints provide no additional evidence.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether a different intervention can reduce the residual inner-radius failures while preserving the parent's learned behavior. It is also unresolved whether the late decline reflects update magnitude, stochastic variation, or a plateau. The current measurements do not establish a recipe for crossing the objective.

# Research postmortems

## a4e64322-8d31-4479-9b0f-c04211eaf8b4 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The retained baseline remains the strongest established campaign role, with 196 of 200 successes on task-reference-v1. On the disjoint experiment-2 panel, the branch-aware reward-peak checkpoint achieved 196/200 (98%) versus 195/200 (97.5%) for the paired baseline, but the later disjoint reproducibility panel reversed that difference: the same active lineage achieved 195/200 versus 197/200 for working. Experiment 3 changed hold-credit accounting and outside-band punishment, but its early and late checkpoints both achieved 197/200 on a new paired panel, exactly matching the working baseline. Experiment 4 then produced a global fresh-start reach collapse: all three measured checkpoints achieved 0/200, with no tolerance entry or hold. Complete reach-and-hold behavior, rather than training reward or proxy success, remains the governing criterion; no development checkpoint establishes official objective completion.

**Lessons and limits:** Experiment 2 combined circular branch-error features with full-radius training, so the paired panel cannot attribute the small gain to either component separately. Its apparent gain also did not reproduce: on seed 20800, active failed five episodes while working failed three, with two shared failures, one working-only failure, and three active-only failures. This weakens a reproducible task advantage and shows that the active-only errors are not a stable copy of the earlier negative-angle cluster. Experiment 3's early checkpoint failed at target angles -157.4, -128.9, and -148.7 degrees, with no tolerance entry in all three cases; the late checkpoint had the same three failures, with only a one-step entry for the -128.9 degree target. The working baseline entered only one of those three cases and held it for one step. Hold interruptions were not reduced: the early refined checkpoint had one separate episode with 39 exits, while the late refined checkpoint had three interrupted episodes. Experiment 4's three checkpoints had no entry in any of 600 episodes and mean minimum distances of 16.93, 17.73, and 13.84 cm, but the fresh initialization and changed observation dimension prevent attributing that collapse to the branch-limit values alone. The seed-20800 diagnostics still show clean completion for almost all episodes and no global collapse, but they do not expose joint trajectories, velocity at entry, or actuator saturation. These are 200-episode development panels, not the held-out official benchmark.

**Competing explanations:** The recurring difficult sector remains consistent with a branch/limit or torque-path difficulty, but the seed-20800 comparison shows that policy-specific variation or checkpoint/seed variance can move failures between policies without changing the task. The circular representation and radius coverage may still help some geometries, but their earlier one-episode gain is not reproducible evidence of a method advantage. Experiment 3 weakens the explanation that hold-credit forfeiture and stronger outside-band punishment are the dominant missing mechanism: it did not change the failed-seed set and did not suppress interrupted holds. Experiment 4 adds a separate explanation: the fresh 23-value representation may have disrupted PPO learning through feature conditioning or optimization rather than testing branch admissibility in isolation. Dynamic command path, residual velocity, and policy-learning variance therefore remain live and cannot be separated by outcome summaries alone.

**Decision frontier:** The unresolved distinction is whether the remaining failures are primarily caused by dynamically abrupt command paths and marginal settling, or by policy-specific branch selection and learning variance. The next evidence must test this while preserving the active lineage's 19-value observation contract and broad reach behavior, then compare tolerance entry, minimum distance, hold interruptions, and late settling against the paired active and working references. A result that improves entry and hold without a global reach loss would support the dynamic-control explanation; unchanged or redistributed failures would redirect the inquiry toward policy variance or a more explicit trajectory-level instrument. Another reward-only interpretation is not supported by experiment 3.

## a4e64322-8d31-4479-9b0f-c04211eaf8b4 / Experiment 2

**Result:** The experiment-2 reward-peak checkpoint completed 196/200 episodes (98%) on the disjoint research-evaluation panel, compared with 195/200 (97.5%) for the retained baseline. The late checkpoint completed 192/200 (96%). The method produced a small paired improvement at the earlier checkpoint but did not sustain it through late training.

**Observed behavior:** The reward-peak checkpoint failed four episodes, all with target angles between approximately -160 and -125 degrees. Two never entered the 1 cm tolerance; the other two entered at steps 17-18 and held for only 1-4 steps. The paired baseline failed five episodes on the same seeds, also concentrated in the negative-angle sector; the challenger fixed one baseline failure near -117 degrees but retained the other four. The late checkpoint failed eight episodes: two never entered tolerance, while the remaining failures included repeated tolerance exits and maximum holds ranging from 3 to 98 steps. Its additional failures occurred outside the original sector as well as within it.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The combined branch-representation and full-radius-coverage hypothesis is weakly supported for the selected early checkpoint but not confirmed. The persistent negative-angle failures contradict the claim that the intervention eliminates the approach limitation, and the late-checkpoint regression contradicts a claim of stable improvement through training. The evidence does not isolate representation from coverage or establish generalization beyond the shared 200-episode panel.

**Interpretation:** The reward-peak candidate is a scientifically useful but incomplete improvement, so the method should be refined rather than matured, abandoned, or promoted. The current evidence shifts the problem from baseline diagnosis to preserving a useful early policy while resolving residual branch/torque-path failures and late hold instability.

## a4e64322-8d31-4479-9b0f-c04211eaf8b4 / Experiment 3

**Result:** The early and late experiment-3 checkpoints each completed 197/200 episodes (98.5%) on the shared research-evaluation panel, exactly matching the paired working baseline. All three models failed the same seeds: 20473, 20481 and 20533. The hold-credit forfeiture and stronger outside-band penalty did not produce a paired task improvement.

**Observed behavior:** The three shared failures corresponded to targets at approximately (-157.4 degrees, 19.75 cm), (-128.9 degrees, 11.54 cm), and (-148.7 degrees, 17.59 cm). The early refined checkpoint never entered tolerance on any of them. The late refined checkpoint also never entered the first and third targets and entered the second for only one step. The working baseline entered the first target for one step and never entered the other two. Outside this shared failure set, the early refined checkpoint had one episode with 39 hold interruptions, and the late refined checkpoint had three interrupted episodes, including the one-step failure and two successful episodes with one interruption each.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The hold-reward refinement is contradicted as a sufficient explanation for the remaining failures on this panel. It neither changed the failed-seed identity nor reduced interruption evidence, and it left the negative-angle no-entry cases intact. The evidence does not prove that branch feasibility or torque authority is the cause: the diagnostics expose outcome geometry and hold timing, not joint trajectories, actions, saturation, or branch selection. The panel is also not an official benchmark.

**Interpretation:** Experiment 3 is a useful negative result. It redirects the method away from treating accumulated hold credit as the primary bottleneck and back toward the coupled approach problem: a target-sector-specific controller must find and execute a dynamically viable branch/path before hold shaping can matter. The branch-aware method remains actionable because experiment 2 showed a small paired gain, but experiment 3 does not justify maturity, promotion, or selecting its checkpoint as the method lineage. The method should be refined while preserving the experiment-2 active lineage and the current recipe for the next deliberate redesign.

## a4e64322-8d31-4479-9b0f-c04211eaf8b4 / Experiment 4

**Result:** All three measured experiment-4 checkpoints completed 0/200 episodes on the disjoint research-evaluation panel. Every episode truncated at 500 steps, with no tolerance entry, no hold, and no successful episode at the early, intermediate, or final checkpoint.

**Observed behavior:** The early, intermediate, and final checkpoints had mean minimum endpoint distances of approximately 16.93, 17.73, and 13.84 cm, respectively; their best episodes still remained 2.84, 2.90, and 2.87 cm from the target. The targets covered the full angular range rather than only the previously recurring negative-angle sector. Training success was 0.0 at all recorded experiment-4 checkpoints, and the evaluation reward means were -1.40, -0.51, and 0.75. The failure signature is therefore a global reach collapse, not a change in the prior no-entry versus post-entry failure balance.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The explicit branch-limit-margin hypothesis is contradicted as a behavior-preserving intervention under this fresh-start training setup. The predicted improvement in the recurring sector did not occur, and the policy lost reach behavior across all sectors before hold control became relevant. The result does not establish that branch-limit information is physically irrelevant: fresh initialization and the 19-to-23 observation change confound feature usefulness with optimization and representation conditioning. It also does not distinguish feature scaling, policy-learning failure, or an implementation interaction from the underlying torque-path explanation.

**Interpretation:** Experiment 4 is a severe negative result and does not provide a usable candidate lineage. The branch-aware method remains actionable because experiment 2 supplied a small paired improvement, but the experiment-4 recipe should not be retained as the current scientific surface. Restore the experiment-2 active lineage and refine the method later with evidence that separates preserving learned reach behavior from adding geometric information; no maturity, promotion, or retention claim is supported.

## a4e64322-8d31-4479-9b0f-c04211eaf8b4 / Inquiry measurement after experiment 4

**Result:** The restored experiment-2 active lineage did not reproduce its earlier paired advantage. It completed 195/200 episodes on the disjoint seed-20800 panel, versus 197/200 for the working baseline.

**Observed behavior:** The active lineage and working baseline shared failures at seeds 20833 and 20945. The active lineage additionally failed at 20952, 20963 and 20995, while working additionally failed at 20810. The active failures included both no-entry and short-entry/hold behavior, but successful episodes generally completed cleanly. The panel therefore shows a modest adverse difference without a global reach collapse.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The result weakens the claim that the experiment-2 representation and coverage recipe provides a reproducible task advantage. It does not establish that branch-aware observations are harmful, because the comparison is one additional 200-episode panel and does not identify branch choice, velocity, torque saturation or trajectory shape.

**Interpretation:** The active method remains in development, but its next iteration should not be justified as another branch-feature gain. A transfer intervention that leaves the observation representation unchanged can test whether command-path smoothing improves the coupled approach-and-hold behavior without repeating the experiment-4 fresh-start confound.

## 768a395f-05a2-43c3-b1ef-449056fbf8ed / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the distinct official 200-episode panel, and it remains unresolved after the frozen final assessment was rejected. The PPO policy is useful but not reliably characterized: experiment 1 reached 98% on task-reference-v1, experiment 2 transferred the policy to the official 6-20 cm radius range and reached 98% on task-reference-v1 plus 99% on the matched researcher panel, experiment 3's negative-angle oversampling regressed both panels, experiment 4's fresh unchanged-recipe run reached only 96.5% and 96%, experiment 5's lower learning rate preserved but did not improve the experiment-2 results, experiment 6's sharper near-threshold shaping preserved the researcher score but did not improve task-reference success, experiment 7's fresh unchanged-recipe run came close to experiment 2 but reached only 97.5% and 98.5% on the two development panels, and experiment 8's periodic angle-error representation reached only 97.5% and 98.5% at its selected checkpoint and 96.5% and 98% at its endpoint. The experiment-2 checkpoint remains the strongest measured working candidate. The periodic representation direction is weakened under this fresh run: recurring negative-angle and just-outside-threshold failures remained, while the endpoint's higher checkpoint proxy success coincided with lower measured task success. Representation and control explanations remain confounded with fresh initialization, optimizer dynamics, reward design, and run-to-run effects. This synthesis is provisional campaign memory and prescribes no next action.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxy success and reward peaked before the end of relevant runs and are not task-acceptance evidence. Experiment 1's failures clustered at small radii and negative angles. Experiment 6's early checkpoint tied the parent at 98% task-reference success, but later checkpoints fell to 97%; its negative-angle failures ended 1.01-1.21 cm from targets and the matched researcher failures remained episodes 42 and 174. Fixed panels, fresh replications, and transferred interventions leave directional control, representation, inherited weights, reward shaping, optimizer dynamics, and run-to-run variation confounded.

# Research postmortems

## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on one frozen 200-episode official panel under the unchanged reach-and-hold task. The transferred radius-coverage run in experiment 4 is now the strongest measured candidate: `checkpoint-100352` reached 196/200 (98.0%) on the researcher panel and 197/200 (98.5%) on the protected task-reference panel, improving the prior parent's 196/200 task-reference result while preserving all non-negative-angle successes. The earlier lower-learning-rate transfer in experiment 2 degraded to 182/200 on the task-reference panel, and the unchanged fresh seed-1 replication in experiment 3 remained far below the parent. Experiment 4 therefore supports moving the working and best-known designation to its comparable checkpoint, but only the distinct official assessment can establish the human objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Experiment 4's logged training success reached 1.00 at 102,400-105,472 steps and fell to 0.94 at 120,832, while measured researcher success stayed 196/200 at all three measured checkpoints and task-reference success was 197/200, 196/200, and 197/200. At the comparable task-reference panel, the intervention resolved the parent's two failures at 6.7-7.2 cm and -116 to -125 degrees, but added one failure at 18.2 cm and -154.8 degrees; the remaining two failures were short-radius targets at 9.4-9.9 cm and -123 to -128 degrees. All four researcher-panel failures at `checkpoint-100352` were negative-angle cases, with three hold/reach failures at radii 17.1-19.0 cm and one at 7.4 cm. The endpoint's lower proxy did not reduce measured success, so proxies remain diagnostic rather than acceptance evidence. The experiment-4 task-reference comparison is on the same fixed panel and has one parent-only versus two challenger-only outcomes; the researcher records use a changed evaluation context, so their 97.0% versus 98.0% scores are supportive but not a clean causal comparison. Evidence remains bounded to one transferred seed and fixed development panels; robustness across seeds and the residual failure mechanism are unknown.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the observed short-radius improvement is robust across transferred seeds and whether the remaining negative-angle failures require further intervention. The official panel is distinct from the development panels, so terminal readiness is a request for the human-owned verdict rather than a claim that development measurements are final validation.

## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a learned policy with clear task progress; `checkpoint-100352` is selected as both working and best-known, while terminal assessment is not requested.

**Observed behavior:** Training logs show training success rising from 0 through 70,656 steps to 0.97 at 97,280 and 100,352 steps, then falling to 0.95 at 120,832; `ep_rew_mean` likewise fell from 117.32 at 100,352 to 112.02 at 120,832. On the task-reference panel, `checkpoint-100352` achieved 196/200 (98.0%) and the endpoint achieved 194/200 (97.0%). The four task-reference failures for `checkpoint-100352` had target angles from about -116 to -128 degrees; researcher-panel failures similarly clustered around negative angles from about -117 to -150 degrees, with both missed reaches and hold interruptions. On the shared researcher episodes, the two checkpoints changed one outcome in each direction rather than showing a uniform endpoint improvement.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The automatic baseline hypothesis was to establish the initial baseline, and it established substantial learned task performance near the objective. The measured task-reference result reaches 98.0% for the selected checkpoint, but the independent researcher panels remain at 97.0-97.5%, so the evidence does not support claiming that the human objective has been established. The baseline had no type-specific causal prediction; the observed training peak and endpoint regression are useful diagnostic signals but do not by themselves identify a mechanism.

**Interpretation:** `checkpoint-100352` is more useful than the completed endpoint because it has the best measured task performance and avoids the endpoint's two additional fixed-panel failures. Keeping the baseline recipe preserves a valid reference, while selecting this checkpoint focuses the next investigation on the localized negative-angle reach-and-hold failures. Repeated measurement of the same candidates would not resolve the observed cross-panel discrepancy enough to justify another round before closure.

## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Experiment 2

**Result:** Conservative PPO fine-tuning did not improve the measured policy. The parent `checkpoint-100352` remains working and best-known; the experiment is closed with the scientific recipe reverted to the parent.

**Observed behavior:** The experiment changed only `ppo.learning_rate` from 0.0003 to 0.0001 and transferred from the working parent. Training logs reported success rates of 0.98 at local step 100,352 and 0.97 at 120,832, with mean episode reward declining to 111.92 and 110.11. On the same-seed researcher evaluation, the parent scored 194/200 (97.0%), while fine-tuned checkpoints 100,352 and 120,832 scored 188/200 (94.0%) and 186/200 (93.0%). Paired comparisons had zero fine-tuned wins and six parent wins for checkpoint 100,352, and zero fine-tuned wins and eight parent wins for checkpoint 120,832. On the fixed task-reference panel, the parent scored 196/200 (98.0%) and both fine-tuned checkpoints scored 182/200 (91.0%). The parent research-evaluation failures were localized to negative angles around -122 to -148 degrees; the fine-tuned checkpoints retained such failures but also showed additional failures at other negative and non-negative angles, including both no-reach cases and hold interruptions.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The exploratory question's useful-refinement branch was not observed: neither measured fine-tuned checkpoint improved the residual region or preserved broad task performance relative to the parent. This conclusion is limited to the transferred run, its learning-rate change, and the measured panels and seed. The unmeasured checkpoints remain unmeasured, and the evidence does not isolate learning rate as the causal source of regression.

**Interpretation:** The measured task behavior favors the unchanged parent over both measured fine-tuned candidates, despite positive training proxies. This training/evaluation discrepancy makes the conservative recipe unsuitable as the current lineage and supports restoring the parent's complete scientific recipe. The result weakens this specific refinement route without disproving other ways to address the residual negative-angle failures.

## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Experiment 3

**Result:** The fresh seed-1 replication did not reproduce the baseline's near-threshold task performance. The experiment is closed with `working` and `best_known` unchanged at the retained experiment-1 `checkpoint-100352`; no terminal assessment is requested.

**Observed behavior:** This was an unchanged PPO baseline trained from fresh initialization for 120,832 completed steps. The measured `checkpoint-100352` achieved 69/200 (34.5%) on the researcher evaluation and 64/200 (32.0%) on the protected task-reference panel. The endpoint achieved 95/200 (47.5%) and 99/200 (49.5%), respectively. On the shared task-reference panel, the parent at the comparable checkpoint had 196/200 (98.0%) successes and the replica had 64/200; the parent-only and replica-only outcome counts were 134 and 2. At the endpoint the corresponding parent result was 194/200 (97.0%), with parent-only and replica-only counts of 96 and 1. The replica's task-reference success covered 58/110 negative-angle and 6/90 non-negative-angle targets at 100,352 steps, then 78/110 and 21/90 at the endpoint, so failures were broad rather than confined to the previously observed negative-angle region. The recorded training proxies increased from `ep_rew_mean` 84.60 and training success 0.04 to 107.41 and 0.13; 22 other checkpoints were not measured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted. The proposed fresh seed-1 run was expected to reproduce near-threshold performance near the experiment-1 selection point with similarly concentrated negative-angle failures. Neither measured replication checkpoint was near threshold, and the shared-panel geometry included extensive non-negative-angle failures. The alternative that the near-threshold result depends on seed-specific optimization or representation formation is supported in this tested seed contrast. This does not identify the causal source of the difference or estimate variance across the broader seed space, and unmeasured checkpoints remain unmeasured.

**Interpretation:** Measured task behavior strongly favors the retained experiment-1 parent over both replication checkpoints. The fresh run is not a useful working or best-known candidate, and additional measurement of its unmeasured checkpoints is unlikely to change the replication conclusion because both measured points are far below the parent on the same panels. The unchanged baseline recipe remains the appropriate current lineage reference, while the seed contrast argues against terminal readiness and motivates a distinct future intervention after closure.

## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Experiment 4

**Result:** Expanded training-radius coverage produced the strongest measured candidate so far. `checkpoint-100352` is selected as working and best-known, and terminal assessment is requested.

**Observed behavior:** The transferred run changed training target radii from 14-20 cm to 6-20 cm and completed 120,832 steps. Logged training success reached 1.00 at 102,400 and 105,472 steps, then declined to 0.94 at the endpoint; `ep_rew_mean` was 113.72, 110.18, and 110.99 at the three measured checkpoints. Researcher evaluation with seed 1000 was 196/200 (98.0%) at checkpoints 100,352, 105,472, and 120,832. On the fixed task-reference panel the same checkpoints scored 197/200 (98.5%), 196/200 (98.0%), and 197/200 (98.5%). At 100,352 steps, the parent had four task-reference failures, all negative-angle 500-step reach failures at radii 6.7-9.9 cm; the challenger had three failures, resolving the parent's two failures at 6.7 and 7.2 cm, retaining two short-radius failures at 9.4-9.9 cm, and adding one failure at 18.2 cm and -154.8 degrees. All challenger task-reference and researcher-panel failures were at negative angles, with no observed loss of non-negative-angle competence. The endpoint matched the 100,352-step measured success rates despite its lower training proxy.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The exploratory question asked whether full-radius transfer could improve short-radius negative-angle failures while preserving broad competence. The protected panel shows a net improvement from 196/200 to 197/200 at the comparable checkpoint, with two prior short-radius failures resolved and all non-negative-angle successes retained. The result is incomplete because two short-radius failures remain, one long-radius negative-angle failure appears, and only one transferred seed and fixed development panels were measured. The researcher-panel improvement from 97.0% for the parent to 98.0% is not a clean causal comparison because its evaluation context changed.

**Interpretation:** Measured task behavior supports selecting `checkpoint-100352` over the prior parent and the later experiment-4 checkpoints: it ties the endpoint on the researcher panel, leads the intermediate checkpoint on the task-reference panel, and is the directly comparable training position. The radius intervention is a useful measured improvement rather than proof of a mechanism or robustness. The candidate reaches the development objective on both measured panel types, so the appropriate next decision is the isolated official assessment; the distinct official panel remains the only basis for declaring the human objective reached.

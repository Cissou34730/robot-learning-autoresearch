## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The objective is at least 98% success, or 196 of 200 episodes, on the unchanged official two-joint-arm reach-and-hold task. Across twenty experiments and 120 development measurements, the fresh baseline reached 97.0% on the researcher panel and 98.0% on the protected task-reference panel at checkpoint-100352; full-radius transfer in experiment 2 reached 97.0% and 98.5%; and the transferred 0.1 action deadband in experiment 16 reached 97.0% and 99.0%, correcting one parent task-reference residual without adding another. The 0.05, 0.15, and continuous-dead-zone variants corrected no further residual; experiment 20 stayed at 97.0% on the researcher panel, while its task-reference score moved from 99.0% early to 97.5% mid-run and 98.0% at the endpoint. Joint shaping, targeted sampling, continuation, hold-credit forfeiture, lower-rate transfer, sharper closeness shaping, longer PPO rollouts, temporal smoothing, entropy removal, higher discounting, and a fresh 128x128 policy did not produce robust cross-panel improvement. The conditional joint-velocity penalty in experiment 19 degraded from 97.0%/98.0% early to 93.5%/94.5% at its endpoint. The campaign therefore has one useful transferred near-objective lineage but no robust cross-panel attainment: retained-policy failures cluster near the 1 cm boundary, fresh trajectories fail broadly, and training reward or proxy success often diverges from measured complete task success. The experiment-16 result is evidence for a useful transferred artifact, not yet for a reproducible hard-deadband learning recipe.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured complete task success, not logged reward or training proxy success, is the policy-progress signal, and checkpoint choice matters. The retained experiment-16 endpoint fails the two recurring task-reference cases near 9.4 cm/-127.9 degrees and 18.2 cm/-154.8 degrees at 500 steps and just beyond the 1 cm boundary, while the researcher panel retains six failures. The observation representation contains joint positions and velocities, Cartesian error, both inverse-kinematics branch errors, and saved normalization; this supports transfer because those quantities retain their meanings, not merely because tensor dimensions match. The 0.1 hard deadband is a useful but single transferred action-level signal: threshold changes, stronger deadband, continuous damping, and low-pass smoothing did not improve its residual set, and no result establishes that jitter caused the failures. In experiment 20, the early soft-deadband candidate preserved the parent's task-reference score and residual identities, while later candidates added boundary-near failures without changing the researcher-panel score. The velocity penalty implementation was confined to the success band, but its strongly negative reward and declining proxy success coincided with broad measured regression; this does not isolate coefficient scale, reward interaction, or optimization trajectory. Experiments 5 and 6 showed broad fresh-training failure for the earlier full-radius recipe, but they did not reproduce the exact hard-deadband recipe, so transfer history and seed-dependent optimization remain confounded. The fixed 200-episode panels are development evidence distinct from the official assessment, and no panel is known to predict that verdict.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the recurring near-boundary failures reflect command discontinuity or jitter, insufficient in-band velocity control, another action-level limitation, policy representation, or optimization variance after transfer. The unchanged or regressed experiment-20 measurements across the continuous dead-zone trajectory do not identify the useful local action transform. It is also unresolved whether the experiment-16 hard-deadband outcome can be learned from fresh initialization or depends on transferred weights and trajectory. Experiment 19 weakens one conditional velocity-penalty coefficient and trajectory without ruling out other stability objectives. The 100-step hold, reward scale, initialization contrast, representation capacity, and optimization variance are not independently separated. The development panels remain finite and distinct from the official assessment, and their disagreement does not establish terminal readiness.

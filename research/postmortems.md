These observations characterize this baseline and its measured artifacts, not the causal effect of training duration or the official result.

The official benchmark is the appropriate next measurement for the selected candidate.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Supported within the baseline question: the unchanged method learned substantial task behavior and produced a candidate at the campaign objective level on a development panel. The result does not establish the official objective because both panels are development measurements, the training run used one seed, and the residual failure region is not fully resolved. The late reward and reference-panel decrease are an unexpected signal against treating continued baseline training as an automatic improvement, but they do not prove that additional training caused the decrease.

**Interpretation:** Measured task performance, rather than reward or training proxy success, identifies checkpoint-100352 as the strongest saved policy. Its concentrated negative-angle failures indicate residual coverage risk, while the matched research-evaluation outcomes and one-point reference-panel drop show no measured benefit from the later checkpoint. This is sufficient to freeze checkpoint-100352 for the official assessment, not to claim that the learned policy has already passed the human objective.

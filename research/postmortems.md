**Interpretation:** The new disjoint control independently supports the retained working policy's near-objective behavior, but it does not establish the official result. The replication candidates add no useful lineage or retention value, so closure should preserve the existing policy and baseline recipe. Further development, if chosen later, should be a separate experiment rather than continuation from either weak replication candidate.

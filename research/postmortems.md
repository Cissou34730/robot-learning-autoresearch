# Research postmortems

## df7588aa-bb83-4dcf-a7c3-300afb63ee97 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the complete two-second reach-and-hold task. The fresh PPO baseline learned broad competence, with checkpoint-100352 reaching 94.5% on each of two disjoint 200-episode research panels and the unchanged control reaching 95.5% on the experiment-2 matched panel. Baseline failures are concentrated in the -180 to -90 degree target-angle sector, while the other three coarse angle sectors were fully successful in the inspected panels; most failures did not establish a sustained hold. The transferred hold-stability reward change did not improve complete-task success: its measured checkpoints reached 93.5% and 92.5%. The reused task-reference result of 98.0% remains relevant but selection-contaminated and does not establish readiness for terminal assessment.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies are informative but not acceptance evidence: the unchanged run's task-proxy success peaked around 97% at checkpoint-100352 and fell to 95% by checkpoint-120832, while the modified run reached 0.99 training success and 112.056 reward at checkpoint-95232 but produced lower task success than its unchanged control. On the shared seed-4600 panel, checkpoint-95232 lost four control successes with no reciprocal wins, and checkpoint-120832 lost six with no reciprocal wins; all failures ran to the 500-step limit. The angle concentration is a diagnostic association, not proof that target-angle exposure is causal, and the current training radius range also differs from the official range. The reward result is evidence against the tested transferred recipe, not a universal claim about every hold penalty or random seed. The task-reference failures were four episodes in a different, reused panel and clustered at target angles from -127.912 to -116.409 degrees. The evidence describes saved policies under the current evaluator and does not establish training-process reproducibility or official benchmark performance.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether concentrated failures in the negative-angle sector can be reduced by changing training exposure without regressing the already competent sectors, or whether they reflect a broader control or representation limitation. The effect of the training-radius mismatch, the source of the discrepancy between the reused task-reference panel and disjoint research panels, learning-process reproducibility, and official benchmark performance are also unresolved. These uncertainties are recorded for reassessment and do not prescribe a required next action.

## df7588aa-bb83-4dcf-a7c3-300afb63ee97 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful but not yet objective-satisfying policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as both the working and best-known lineage; the recipe is kept for the next experiment, and no final benchmark is requested.

**Observed behavior:** The candidate inventory records 120,832 completed steps. Training success was 0.97 at checkpoint-100352, compared with 0.42 at checkpoint-86016 and 0.95 at checkpoint-120832; training reward was highest at checkpoint-86016 (163.854) and lower at checkpoint-100352 (117.320) and checkpoint-120832 (112.017). On research panel seed 4200, checkpoint-100352 achieved 189/200 (94.5%), checkpoint-120832 188/200 (94.0%), and checkpoint-86016 187/200 (93.5%); paired comparisons favored checkpoint-100352 by 1 and 2 discordant wins respectively. A disjoint research panel with seed 4400 again measured checkpoint-100352 at 189/200 (94.5%). The fixed task-reference panel measured the same checkpoint at 196/200 (98.0%), with four failures, all truncated at 500 steps and targeting angles from -127.912 to -116.409 degrees. Research-panel failures for the measured candidates also truncated at 500 steps.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The measurement question was whether late training-proxy improvements transferred to the complete reach-and-hold task and which checkpoint was useful for closure. Checkpoint-100352 was better than the two comparison checkpoints on the shared research panel and remained stable on a disjoint panel, supporting transfer of substantial task competence and selecting it as the most useful saved policy. The transfer did not reach 98% on either independent research panel, and continued training to checkpoint-120832 did not improve measured success. The 98.0% task-reference result is a promising but selection-contaminated and non-independent signal, so it strengthens evidence of progress without resolving the independent-panel shortfall. These observations do not identify which training component caused the result.

**Interpretation:** The baseline made meaningful progress toward the human objective, but the measured policy is not supported as meeting the objective across independent development panels. Checkpoint-100352 is the appropriate current reference for further development because it has the best measured independent performance and stable behavior across disjoint panels; the lower measured alternatives do not justify retention as competing lineages. Keeping the recipe preserves the tested baseline for the next ordinary experiment, while deferring terminal assessment avoids treating the task-reference panel as independent confirmation.

## df7588aa-bb83-4dcf-a7c3-300afb63ee97 / Experiment 2

**Result:** The transferred hold-stability reward change did not improve complete-task success. The unchanged working lineage remains the working and best-known policy; the modified reward recipe is not retained.

**Observed behavior:** The experiment-2 training log reached 0.99 training success and 112.056 mean training reward at checkpoint-95232, then declined to 0.95 and 98.577 at checkpoint-120832. On the same fresh research panel of 200 episodes (seed 4600), checkpoint-95232 achieved 187/200 (93.5%), the unchanged working policy achieved 191/200 (95.5%), and checkpoint-120832 achieved 185/200 (92.5%). Paired comparisons recorded zero candidate wins against four working-policy wins for checkpoint-95232 and zero candidate wins against six working-policy wins for checkpoint-120832. Every failure in these three artifacts truncated at 500 steps. The unmeasured checkpoint-105472 remains unmeasured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The expected improvement over the working policy and reduction in timeouts were not observed; both measured modified-reward checkpoints were worse on the matched panel, and the later checkpoint degraded further alongside its training proxies. The hold-stability proposition is weakened under this transferred recipe and training run, but the evidence does not establish that all possible hold penalties or random seeds would fail.

**Interpretation:** The shared-panel control and paired outcomes make panel difficulty an insufficient explanation for the observed shortfall. The measured saved policies therefore do not support keeping the modified reward as the campaign recipe. Because only one transferred training run was tested and the development panels are not the official assessment, the result supports a lineage reversal rather than a universal causal conclusion or an official objective verdict. Further training can start from the unchanged working lineage with its original recipe.

## 614b8ee6-6a63-4ee9-b948-d4fb9064d6be / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The experiment-11 continuation remains the strongest current lineage: it achieved 776/800 successes across four disjoint development panels, and both experiment-13 checkpoints tied the current working lineage at 194/200 on a further disjoint panel. Experiment 13 produced the same six failing episode identities for the asymmetric negative-edge sampler and the reference, so it did not demonstrate a complete-task gain. Residual failures remain a coupled direct-torque behavior spanning the singular reset, posture choice, approach, settling, and sustained regulation.

**Lessons and limits:** The experiment-11 sampler improved complete success over its predecessor without establishing why; the experiment-12 hold-credit consequence did not improve the shared edge failures; and experiment 13's asymmetric allocation of hard-target exposure did not improve success, alter the failing episode set, or yield a paired win. On episodes 8300-8499, the shared failures included one tolerance non-entry and five brief entries followed by hold interruption, with near-continuous saturated control in those failures; this describes the coupled behavior but does not identify whether geometry, posture, braking, settling, or local regulation is responsible. Earlier representation, effort, boundary, action-mapping, and focused-exposure changes also did not preserve broad competence under their tested conditions. Development evidence remains below the 98% objective.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved how target radius and angle, the two inverse-kinematic branches, the reset singularity, direct-torque trajectory and velocity, settling, and sustained local regulation combine in the residual behavior. It is also unresolved whether the persistent non-entry and interrupted-hold outcomes share a limitation, whether the observed angular asymmetry generalizes beyond the measured panels, and whether any improvement can preserve complete-task behavior across the full official distribution.

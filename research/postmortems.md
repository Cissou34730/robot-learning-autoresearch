# Research postmortems

## 229ee49c-81d2-4d70-b9d1-83d2c786555a / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The unchanged PPO baseline learned a strong but uneven reach-and-hold policy. The selected `checkpoint-100352` lineage achieved 465/480 successes (96.875%) across three distinct research panels, while the full-range target-radius continuation matched its parent at 155/160 with zero paired discordance. Late training and broader radius sampling therefore produced no measured task improvement; the remaining failures include both failure to reach tolerance and interrupted holds.

**Lessons and limits:** Training reward and training success locate candidates but are not task scores: experiment-2 training success was 1.0 at 105472 steps and 0.94 at 120832, while both checkpoints remained 155/160. The original research panels and fixed task-reference panel remain development evidence, not independent confirmation or an official result. On the 6000–6159 panel, the saved parent had three failures that never reached tolerance and two that entered tolerance but held for only 1 and 1 steps; before the proposed intervention, the reward assigned no forfeiture to an interrupted hold. These results support a useful working lineage, not attainment of the objective.

**Open questions:** It remains unknown whether explicitly valuing uninterrupted hold completion can reduce hold failures without sacrificing reach, whether a different intervention is needed for missed-tolerance failures, and whether the selected policy meets the objective on the separate official 200-episode assessment. The fixed task-reference panel remains reused development evidence rather than independent confirmation.

## 229ee49c-81d2-4d70-b9d1-83d2c786555a / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful policy, with `checkpoint-100352` selected as the working and best-known lineage; no final benchmark is requested.

**Observed behavior:** The run completed 120,832 steps. The proxy peak at 100,352 steps had training success 0.97 and reward 117.32; the final checkpoint had training success 0.95 and reward 112.02. On the original research panel both achieved 151/160 (94.375%). On the disjoint panel, `checkpoint-100352` achieved 159/160 (99.375%) and `checkpoint-120832` achieved 158/160 (98.75%); the paired comparison favored 100352 by one net win over 320 episodes. The fixed task-reference panel scored 98% and 97%, respectively, but is reused rather than independent. Failure diagnostics include both missed tolerance and interrupted holds; the final checkpoint's disjoint failure reached tolerance but held for at most 5 steps with 244 interruptions.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The fresh baseline established substantial learned reach-and-hold capability, but the available development evidence does not establish robust attainment of the 98% objective. The disjoint panel distinguishes the earlier proxy peak from the final checkpoint, while the pooled distinct research-panel result for the selected checkpoint remains 310/320 (96.875%) and the official panel has not been run.

**Interpretation:** The earlier checkpoint is the most defensible saved policy because it is independently better on the disjoint comparison and late training slightly degraded measured behavior. The experiment made no researcher-owned code changes, so the scientific recipe should be kept. The selected policy should remain available for future development, but terminal assessment should not be used to resolve the remaining uncertainty; further training is the appropriate next experiment after closure.

## 229ee49c-81d2-4d70-b9d1-83d2c786555a / Experiment 2

**Result:** The full-range target-radius continuation did not improve measured complete reach-and-hold success. The saved parent remains the working lineage and the experiment-only recipe change is reverted.

**Observed behavior:** On the new research panel at seed 6000, the saved parent, checkpoint `105472`, and checkpoint `120832` each achieved `155/160` (`96.875%`). Each continuation had zero discordant episode outcomes against the parent. Checkpoint `105472` had the highest training-success proxy (`1.0`); the final checkpoint's proxy declined to `0.94`. The official 98% objective requires at least 196/200 successes and was not assessed by the available measurements.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened under the tested continuation and panel. Expanding training-radius sampling from 14–20 cm to 6–20 cm did not produce the predicted increase in complete success, and there was no episode-level evidence of a change relative to the parent. This does not disprove the intervention under other training budgets or methods, but it does not justify selecting either measured continuation checkpoint over the established parent.

**Interpretation:** The intervention did not establish progress toward the human objective, and the training proxies did not identify a task-superior checkpoint. The parent remains the most defensible reusable policy because experiment 1 selected it on disjoint evidence; that selection evidence and the fixed task-reference panel are not independent confirmation of the current result. No final assessment is requested because development evidence remains below the objective and further training is the appropriate separate next experiment.

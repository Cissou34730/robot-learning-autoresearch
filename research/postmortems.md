# Research postmortems

## 4b17531f-ff44-4b08-997f-e703fc28eb8e / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The unchanged PPO baseline learned competent reach-and-hold behavior, but the best characterized checkpoint remains below the 98% human objective on disjoint research panels. The original working checkpoint-100352 remains strongest: it achieved 155/160 on the latest disjoint panel and 457/480 pooled across three research panels. The reduced-learning-rate continuation reached 151/160 at its proxy peak and 147/160 at its final checkpoint. The fixed task-reference result of 98% for checkpoint-100352 is useful transfer evidence, but not independent confirmation because that panel was reused for selection.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward identify useful late-training regions but do not establish the required task success. The lower learning rate did not preserve the parent behavior in this continuation: the parent won 4-0 discordant episodes against checkpoint-95232 and 8-0 against checkpoint-120832 on the new shared panel. These development measurements are not the official 200-episode assessment, the single continuation does not establish a general causal limit for learning-rate changes, and unmeasured checkpoints provide no additional evidence.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether a different training intervention can reduce the residual failures and whether the late decline reflects update magnitude, stochastic variation, or a plateau. The current measurements do not identify the cause or establish a recipe for crossing the objective.

## 4b17531f-ff44-4b08-997f-e703fc28eb8e / Experiment 2

**Result:** The reduced-learning-rate continuation did not improve the selected policy. The existing working and best-known lineage remains the strongest measured policy, and the parent recipe is restored for future decisions.

**Observed behavior:** On the new disjoint research panel, the parent `working` checkpoint-100352 achieved 155/160 successes (96.875%), checkpoint-95232 achieved 151/160 (94.375%), and final checkpoint-120832 achieved 147/160 (91.875%). Paired comparisons favored `working` by 4-0 discordant wins over checkpoint-95232 and 8-0 over checkpoint-120832; checkpoint-95232 beat the final checkpoint by 4-0. The parent therefore has 457/480 pooled research successes across the three disjoint panels. All are development measurements, not the official result.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted for this tested continuation. Lowering the PPO learning rate from 0.0003 to 0.0001 did not improve or preserve the parent's measured behavior: even the proxy-peak checkpoint was 2.5 percentage points below the parent on the shared panel, and the final checkpoint was 5.0 points below. This is evidence against this particular continuation recipe and run, not a general causal conclusion about all learning-rate changes.

**Interpretation:** The measurement question is resolved without retaining a challenger. The parent lineage is useful but remains below the 98% objective, so the campaign still has a meaningful development gap; any further training should be a separately prepared experiment after this closure rather than a continuation of the rejected reduced-rate recipe.

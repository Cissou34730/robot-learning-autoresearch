Research and task-reference measurements are development evidence, not the official 200-episode verdict; the fixed task-reference panel is reused and is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** Whether the current near-threshold policy reaches at least 196/200 on the official panel remains unresolved. Further training or a changed scientific recipe could improve the residual failures, but the current experiment does not identify their cause.

This conclusion is limited to the single trained recipe and measured checkpoints.

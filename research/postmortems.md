## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The fresh PPO baseline remains the strongest measured policy. The transferred conservative fine-tuning run regressed on both the researcher panel and the protected task-reference panel at its measured checkpoints, so the parent `checkpoint-100352` remains working and best-known. It is a strong development candidate but the official objective remains unestablished; development measurements remain distinct from the official result.

**Lessons and limits:** The experiment-2 training proxy stayed high at selected checkpoints (`training_success` 0.98 at local step 100,352 and 0.97 at 120,832), but `ep_rew_mean` declined from 122.83 at local step 5,120 to 111.92 and 110.11 at those checkpoints. On the same-seed researcher panel, the parent scored 97.0%, while fine-tuned checkpoints 100,352 and 120,832 scored 94.0% and 93.0%; paired outcomes gave the parent six and eight wins respectively and no losses. On the protected task-reference panel, the parent scored 98.0% while both fine-tuned checkpoints scored 91.0%. The fine-tuned diagnostics added failures outside the parent's concentrated negative-angle region and included both missed reaches and interrupted holds. These are bounded measurements of this transferred run and do not establish that learning rate alone caused the regression; training proxies are not task acceptance criteria, and the 22 unmeasured checkpoints remain unmeasured.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether a different intervention can improve the negative-angle reach-and-hold region without broad regression, whether reach and hold failures need separate treatment, and how the cross-panel discrepancy relates to generalization. The parent candidate's 98.0% task-reference result is encouraging but is not confirmed by the researcher panels, so terminal readiness remains uncertain.

**Interpretation:** `checkpoint-100352` is more useful than the completed endpoint because it has the best measured task performance and avoids the endpoint's two additional fixed-panel failures. Keeping the baseline recipe preserves a valid reference, while selecting this checkpoint focuses the next investigation on the localized negative-angle reach-and-hold failures. ## 05a911a8-774f-412d-a86a-fd033fa1300a / Experiment 2

**Result:** Conservative PPO fine-tuning did not improve the measured policy. The parent `checkpoint-100352` remains working and best-known; the experiment is closed with the scientific recipe reverted to the parent.

**Observed behavior:** The experiment changed only `ppo.learning_rate` from 0.0003 to 0.0001 and transferred from the working parent. Training logs reported success rates of 0.98 at local step 100,352 and 0.97 at 120,832, with mean episode reward declining to 111.92 and 110.11. On the same-seed researcher evaluation, the parent scored 194/200 (97.0%), while fine-tuned checkpoints 100,352 and 120,832 scored 188/200 (94.0%) and 186/200 (93.0%). Paired comparisons had zero fine-tuned wins and six parent wins for checkpoint 100,352, and zero fine-tuned wins and eight parent wins for checkpoint 120,832. On the fixed task-reference panel, the parent scored 196/200 (98.0%) and both fine-tuned checkpoints scored 182/200 (91.0%). The parent research-evaluation failures were localized to negative angles around -122 to -148 degrees; the fine-tuned checkpoints retained such failures but also showed additional failures at other negative and non-negative angles, including both no-reach cases and hold interruptions.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The exploratory question's useful-refinement branch was not observed: neither measured fine-tuned checkpoint improved the residual region or preserved broad task performance relative to the parent. This conclusion is limited to the transferred run, its learning-rate change, and the measured panels and seed. The unmeasured checkpoints remain unmeasured, and the evidence does not isolate learning rate as the causal source of regression.

**Interpretation:** The measured task behavior favors the unchanged parent over both measured fine-tuned candidates, despite positive training proxies. This training/evaluation discrepancy makes the conservative recipe unsuitable as the current lineage and supports restoring the parent's complete scientific recipe. The result weakens this specific refinement route without disproving other ways to address the residual negative-angle failures.

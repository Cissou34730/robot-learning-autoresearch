# Research postmortems

## 768a395f-05a2-43c3-b1ef-449056fbf8ed / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The fresh PPO baseline learned a near-objective reach-and-hold policy. Measured task success improved from 93% at checkpoint-90112 to 98% at checkpoint-100352 on the protected task-reference panel, then fell to 97% at checkpoint-120832. Independent research evaluation measured 98% pooled success for checkpoint-100352 across two 200-episode panels and 99% for checkpoint-120832 on the later panel, with identical success outcomes for the two checkpoints on their shared episodes. The best-supported current policy is therefore checkpoint-100352, while the official 98% objective remains unresolved until the final benchmark.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxy success and reward rose substantially during learning but are not policy-acceptance evidence; the late proxy decline is useful for checkpoint selection only because task-level measurements also favored the earlier checkpoint on the protected panel. Failures on checkpoint-100352 were concentrated in four small-radius targets at angles from about -116 to -128 degrees on the task-reference panel, and research diagnostics showed a broader negative-angle failure pattern on its independent panels. These observations identify residual behavior but do not establish a causal mechanism or guarantee performance on the distinct final panel. Sources: `research/results.jsonl`, the six detailed artifacts under `research/evaluations/768a395f-05a2-43c3-b1ef-449056fbf8ed/`, and `research/query_training_log.py` output for experiment 1 steps 70656-120832.

**Open questions:** Whether the selected policy reaches at least 98% on the fixed official panel is unresolved. The cause and generality of the negative-angle and small-radius failures are also unresolved; the available development panels are not final validation.

## 768a395f-05a2-43c3-b1ef-449056fbf8ed / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful near-objective policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected for closure because it achieved 98% on the protected task-reference panel; the final benchmark is requested to determine the official result.

**Observed behavior:** Training logs show proxy success increasing from 0.00 at step 70656 to 0.98 at step 99328, then declining to 0.95 at step 120832; mean reward similarly declined from 168 at step 87040 to 117 at step 100352 and 112 at step 120832. These are training signals, not task-acceptance measurements. On task-reference-v1, checkpoint-90112 scored 93% (186/200), checkpoint-100352 scored 98% (196/200), and checkpoint-120832 scored 97% (194/200). On researcher evaluation, checkpoint-100352 scored 97% on seeds 0-199 and 99% on seeds 200-399; checkpoint-120832 scored 99% on seeds 200-399. The paired comparison on seeds 200-399 found no discordant outcomes and no wins for either checkpoint. The four checkpoint-100352 task-reference failures were all small-radius targets (6.7-9.9 cm) at angles from -116.4 to -127.9 degrees; the endpoint retained those failures and added two failures, including one at +169.1 degrees. Twenty-two of the twenty-four saved checkpoints remain unmeasured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Supported for the baseline's intended purpose of establishing a strong initial reference: the learned policy substantially improved measured task success and reached 98% on one independent development panel. This does not establish the official objective, and the endpoint's research-panel result does not overturn the protected-panel regression because the paired research outcomes were identical and the panels are distinct. The assessment is limited to this single training run and the measured checkpoints.

**Interpretation:** The saved policy demonstrates measured progress toward the human objective and is ready for the official frozen-policy assessment. Checkpoint-100352 is preferable to the endpoint on the protected development panel and has comparable independent research performance. The failure concentration suggests a residual directional/near-radius weakness, but the evidence does not justify attributing it to a particular reward, observation, or training mechanism.

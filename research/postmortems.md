# Research postmortems

## 6dab7993-582d-4243-b0a2-f935e51c2728 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** PPO learned the reach-and-hold behavior, and working checkpoint-100352 achieved 766/800 pooled successes across five disjoint research panels, including 157/160 on the latest panel. The experiment-4 hold-continuity reward tied working at its local peak but fell to 130/160 at the endpoint; the pooled development evidence therefore does not establish the 98% objective. Residual failures span radii and repeatedly cluster around negative angles from -90 to -180 degrees, with both reach and hold-instability patterns present.

**Lessons and limits:** Late training proxies do not establish task success, and the experiment-4 reward penalty did not produce a measured advantage: its local peak had zero discordant outcomes against working, while its endpoint lost 27 paired episodes. Working remains best supported; experiment-2, experiment-3, and experiment-4 challengers produced no paired wins on their respective new panels. The task-reference panel is reused development evidence, not independent confirmation. The radius, angle-sector, and tested hold-continuity recipes are weakened as sufficient explanations without ruling out other representation, reward, or optimization changes.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the residual negative-angle failures are primarily reachability/generalization errors or failures to sustain tolerance, and whether another intervention can improve the unchanged official task. Generalization across the full official distribution remains unestablished, and the official 200-episode assessment has not been requested.

## 6dab7993-582d-4243-b0a2-f935e51c2728 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful late-run policy, with checkpoint-100352 selected as the working and best-known lineage, but the policy is not yet sufficiently supported as meeting the human objective.

**Observed behavior:** Checkpoint-100352 succeeded on 151/160 episodes (94.375%) on both research panels, for 302/320 pooled research episodes. The matched comparison over 320 episodes gave checkpoint-100352 two wins and checkpoint-120832 zero wins, with two discordant episodes. Training proxies rose late in the run, but the final checkpoint's measured research success was lower than checkpoint-100352's.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline established that the unchanged method can learn the task and that late checkpoints substantially outperform early training, but it did not establish the 98% objective on independent development evidence. The apparent 98% task-reference result cannot serve as independent confirmation because the protected panel is permanently reused; the conclusion is limited to this trained artifact, evaluation semantics, and observed panels.

**Interpretation:** Checkpoint-100352 is the best-supported policy from this experiment and is worth carrying forward. The stable 94.375% result on disjoint research panels indicates real progress but leaves a meaningful residual failure rate, so further development is warranted before requesting the irreversible official assessment. The current scientific recipe is retained because this baseline has demonstrated useful learning and no measured evidence yet justifies changing it.

## 6dab7993-582d-4243-b0a2-f935e51c2728 / Experiment 2

**Result:** Full-radius transfer training did not improve measured task performance. The existing working lineage remains the strongest policy, and the full-radius final checkpoint is retained as a reusable alternative.

**Observed behavior:** On the disjoint research panel covering episodes 4520-4679, the working control, checkpoint-105472, and checkpoint-120832 each achieved 155/160 (96.875%). Both paired comparisons had zero candidate wins, zero reference wins, and zero discordant episodes, indicating identical success outcomes on this panel. The five shared failures occurred at episode seeds 4556, 4565, 4583, 4593, and 4625, with target radii approximately 6.29, 16.72, 7.30, 19.93, and 10.69 cm and target angles approximately -117.0, -139.5, -118.6, -161.6, and -126.0 degrees. The 105472 checkpoint reached a training-success proxy of 1.0, but that proxy did not translate into measured improvement.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The prediction that uniform training over 6-20 cm would reduce the residual failures and improve independent success was not observed: both measured transfer checkpoints tied the parent below the 98% objective, and the shared failures included covered long radii as well as short radii and were concentrated in a directional sector. This is evidence against radius coverage as a sufficient explanation under this transfer run, not proof that radius coverage can never help.

**Interpretation:** The intervention preserved the learned reach-and-hold behavior but did not address the dominant residual failure pattern. The parent working lineage should remain active, while the current full-radius source change should be reverted before a different intervention is prepared. Retaining the full-radius endpoint preserves a measured transfer trajectory for later comparison without promoting it.

## 6dab7993-582d-4243-b0a2-f935e51c2728 / Experiment 3

**Result:** Angle-sector oversampling did not improve the learned policy. The existing working lineage remains the best-supported policy, and the experiment-3 training change should be reverted.

**Observed behavior:** On the disjoint research panel covering episodes 4680-4839, working achieved 152/160 (95.0%), the checkpoint-5120 proxy peak achieved 151/160 (94.375%), and checkpoint-120832 achieved 148/160 (92.5%). The paired comparisons recorded zero challenger wins: working led by one discordant episode against checkpoint-5120 and by four against checkpoint-120832. The proxy peak therefore did not reach the 157/160 success level corresponding to 98% on a 160-episode panel, and continued angle-sector training degraded measured success at the endpoint.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The prediction that oversampling the repeatedly failing -180 to -90 degree sector would reduce directional failures and improve independent success was not observed: both measured experiment-3 checkpoints were below the working control and below the objective proxy threshold. This weakens angle-sector coverage as a sufficient explanation under this transfer recipe, but does not establish that other changes to representation, reward, optimization, or training conditions cannot address the residual failures.

**Interpretation:** The intervention preserved enough reach-and-hold behavior to remain useful as a diagnostic, but it did not correct the residual directional failures and late training reduced measured performance. The working lineage should remain active and best known, while the experiment-3 source change should be reverted so a future ordinary experiment can test a different route toward the human objective. The experiment-3 candidates are not retained because neither measured challenger improved on working and neither supplies a reusable policy advantage.

## 6dab7993-582d-4243-b0a2-f935e51c2728 / Experiment 4

**Result:** The hold-continuity reward intervention did not improve the learned policy. The local proxy peak tied working on the disjoint measurement panel, while continued training degraded the endpoint substantially.

**Observed behavior:** On episodes 4840-4999, working and checkpoint-35840 each achieved 157/160 (98.125%) with zero discordant episodes. Checkpoint-120832 achieved 130/160 (81.25%); working won all 27 discordant paired episodes. The peak's three failures were shared with working, while the endpoint showed repeated failed holds and long truncated episodes. Across five disjoint research panels, working now has 766/800 successes (95.75%), so the latest panel is encouraging but pooled development evidence remains below the human objective.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. Penalizing exits from tolerance did not reduce the shared residual failures or produce a paired success advantage over working, and the endpoint degraded under continued training. This weakens the tested hold-continuity reward formulation as a sufficient remedy under this transfer recipe, but does not rule out other reward, representation, optimization, or training interventions.

**Interpretation:** Working remains the best-supported and useful training parent. The experiment-4 source change should be reverted because it supplied no measured advantage and harmed the endpoint. Retain checkpoint-35840 as a reusable, independently measured local-peak alternative for future comparison; do not promote it over working or request the irreversible official assessment from development evidence alone.

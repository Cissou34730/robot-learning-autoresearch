# Research postmortems

## 90890200-b313-4f38-b010-de1eaaeb3d98 / Scientific strategy

**Direction:** Determine whether the baseline's localized negative-angle blind spot is a control or representation limitation while separating method behavior from initialization variance. Experiments 4 and 5 both failed to repair the wedge, and experiment 5 produced a broad regression after replacing the IK errors with selected-branch errors and validity flags. Experiment 6 indicates that temporal action smoothing can preserve the baseline success rate and improve late hold stability, but it does not repair the wedge. If research continues, use the smoothed lineage as the control-stable reference while investigating a control, dynamics, or less disruptive representation change with a stronger rationale.

**Lessons and limits:** Experiment 1 selected `checkpoint-100352` at 97.0%, with all six failures in the -122 to -145 degree sector; five never reached tolerance and one reached it only briefly. Experiment 2 retained the same six non-reaching episodes at 10,240, 20,480 and 90,112 steps despite 50% oversampling of the surrounding negative-angle sector, while success fell to 94.5% and 92.5% and hold interruptions increased from 38 to 278 and 312. Experiment 3 used the unchanged method from fresh seed 2 and scored 75.5% at the matched checkpoint, supporting substantial initialization variance but not explaining the control/representation limitation. Experiment 4's target-coordinate representation scored 96.5% at checkpoint-100352 with seven failures: all six baseline wedge failures remained and one additional negative-angle non-reach appeared; it had zero hold interruptions but no reach improvement. At checkpoint-120832 it fell to 94.0%, with 12 failures and 216 hold interruptions, including the same wedge plus broader reach and hold failures. Experiment 5's feasible-IK observation scored 10.5% at both checkpoint-100352 and checkpoint-120832: 167 and 156 failed episodes never reached tolerance, and total hold interruptions were 612 and 418 respectively. The training log reported zero training successes through 120,832 steps. Experiment 6 measured 97.0% at checkpoints 40,960, 100,352 and 120,832; each had the same six non-reaching episodes in the wedge, while hold interruptions decreased from 2 to 1 to 0. Relative to the baseline's 96.5% and 245 hold interruptions at checkpoint-120832, smoothing improved late stability without broadening failures, but the evidence is still one transfer run and one development panel. These results reject both tested representation changes as sufficient fixes; experiment 5 also shows that the selected-branch representation can severely destabilize learning, while experiment 6 isolates a useful stability effect without resolving the reach limitation.

**Open questions:** Which control, dynamics, or observation design property causes the persistent negative-angle reach failures, and whether it can be changed without sacrificing hold stability. It also remains unknown how frequently the unchanged method reaches the retained baseline's performance across fresh initializations, and whether experiment 6's late hold improvement reproduces across seeds and panels. Experiment 5 does not isolate whether the regression came from branch selection, validity flags, their scaling, or the resulting optimization dynamics.

**Conditional next steps:** If research continues, start from the smoothed `checkpoint-120832` and test a control- or dynamics-focused intervention or a less disruptive representation change with the same matched development evaluation and explicit hold-stability diagnostics; a fresh unchanged-method replication remains useful for estimating initialization variance. Do not further narrow angular sampling, repeat the selected-branch/validity encoding without a new stability rationale, or treat either failed representation as evidence that all representation changes are ineffective.

Keeping the smoother is justified as a more stable working recipe for a subsequent intervention, not as evidence that the objective has been reached. The comparison remains limited to one transferred training realization, one 200-episode research panel and a single evaluation seed.

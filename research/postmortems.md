## 7a8a9e3a-e0cc-4940-a747-cb7529b2da8c / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The unchanged PPO baseline remains the strongest saved policy, with 778/800 successes across four distinct research panels and 196/200 on the latest disjoint panel. The three measured reward-shaping continuations all regressed, while the current training distribution covers only 14–20 cm of the official 6–20 cm range; development evidence therefore shows a strong but non-terminal policy.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward identify useful checkpoints but do not replace task-success measurement. Full and partial hold-exit forfeiture and convex hold-progress shaping regressed in the measured continued-training checkpoints, although those findings are limited to their tested recipes and trajectories. Incumbent failures include both episodes that never reach tolerance and episodes that interrupt a hold; the research panels and reused task-reference panel remain development evidence rather than official confirmation.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether training on the full target-radius range can improve performance on the official distribution without disrupting the learned outer-range behavior. The official result for the selected policy is also unresolved; development panels do not declare the objective reached.

**Observed behavior:** On the new disjoint research panel covering episodes 2200-2399, the parent `working` policy achieved 196/200 successes (98.0%). The measured intervention checkpoints achieved 177/200 (88.5%) at `checkpoint-105472` and 167/200 (83.5%) at `checkpoint-120832`. Paired comparisons favored `working` over those checkpoints by 19 and 29 discordant episodes, respectively; the earlier parent-selection panel is not counted as independent confirmation of this new result. The measured candidates therefore fail the 98% development threshold by 19 and 29 episodes, while 22 other experiment-2 candidates remain unmeasured.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The hypothesis that setting `HOLD_EXIT_FORFEIT_FRACTION` to 1.0 would improve hold completion and persist through continued training is contradicted under the tested continuation and checkpoints. This conclusion is limited to the tested intervention, training trajectory, and evaluation panel; it does not disprove every possible hold-robustness method. The parent result is useful saved-policy evidence, but development measurements do not declare the human objective reached.

**Interpretation:** The intervention should not replace the parent lineage. The independent 196/200 parent result justifies retaining the unchanged recipe as the current working and best-known lineage, while the large regression at both measured intervention checkpoints makes another measurement of the same experiment unlikely to change the lineage decision. No terminal benchmark is requested in this closure because the development evidence is at the threshold on one new panel but remains 391/400 (97.8%) across the two research panels, so a goal-reached verdict is not yet expected with sufficient confidence.

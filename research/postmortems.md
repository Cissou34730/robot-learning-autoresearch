**Current synthesis:** The saved `working`/`best_known` lineage (`checkpoint-100352`) remains the strongest policy: it reached 197/200 (98.5%) on the new disjoint experiment-3 panel, after 94.5% on the disjoint experiment-2 panel and 530/560 on the earlier pooled research panels. The 98.0% task-reference result used for selection is not independent confirmation, and development panels never establish the official result. Both experiment-2 hold-progress forfeiture challengers were substantially worse, while both experiment-3 full-radius challengers tied the saved policy at 197/200. The baseline was trained on target radii 14-20 cm even though the official distribution spans 6-20 cm.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies identify the learned transition but do not establish task success. Adding `HOLD_EXIT_FORFEIT_FRACTION=0.5` did not improve the trained policy: the measured checkpoints were 26.5 and 27.0 percentage points below the saved parent, and paired comparisons favored the parent by 27-to-1 and 28-to-1 discordant episodes. Expanding training target coverage to the full 6-20 cm range produced no observed gain: the control, training-success peak, and final checkpoint all scored 197/200 with zero discordant paired outcomes. The baseline's failures include both missed reaches and interrupted holds, while the failed reward intervention also produced severe hold instability; this does not show that every hold or coverage intervention fails. Development panels remain non-final, and the fixed task-reference panel is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** The learned policy still has not been established against the official terminal assessment despite one disjoint development panel reaching 98.5%. The contribution of target coverage versus continued transfer training is unresolved beyond this tied experiment, and further training would be a new ordinary experiment rather than part of experiment 3 closure.

**Interpretation:** The intervention is not a useful successor to the baseline and should not be retained as a training parent. The baseline's 94.5% on a panel disjoint from its selection evidence is meaningful independent development evidence, but it remains below the 98% human objective, so no final assessment is warranted yet. Future training remains an ordinary next experiment after closure.

Preserve the existing best-known designation and restore the parent's scientific recipe. Although the parent reached 98.5% on this disjoint development panel, the broader pooled research evidence remains below 98% and development measurements cannot declare the objective reached, so no final assessment is requested. Further training, if pursued, must be a separate ordinary experiment after closure.

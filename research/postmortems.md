**Direction:** The exposure-only investigation is resolved: oversampling the recurring -150 to -120 degree failure sector did not improve robustness and caused broad collateral loss on the fixed distribution. The next direction is mechanism-focused, distinguishing observation/representation or reward limitations from stochastic training variation rather than repeating aggressive angle reweighting.

**Lessons and limits:** The baseline checkpoint-100352 achieved 97.75% pooled research success across two matched panels, with nine failures concentrated around -121 to -150 degrees, establishing a concrete robustness gap (`research/brief.md`; `research/evaluations/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb`). On the compatible seed-2000 panel, the baseline had 5 failures, all in the focus sector; experiment 2 had 8 focus-sector and 3 outside-sector failures at 15,360 steps (94.5%), then 9 focus-sector and 9 outside-sector failures at 120,832 steps (91.0%). Its transferred training trajectory also fell from 0.93 success at 5,120 steps to 0.77-0.85 through most checkpoints and 0.80 at the endpoint (`research/training_logs/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb/experiment-2-attempt-1.log`; `research/checkpoints/challengers/10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb/experiment-2/inventory.json`). These fixed-distribution results directly contradict the expected improvement and establish that this exposure-only recipe should not replace the baseline. The evidence is still development evidence, not the official objective verdict, and one transferred run cannot separate the intervention effect from PPO training variance.

**Open questions:** The remaining distinction is whether the persistent negative-angle failures are primarily caused by policy observation/representation or reward limitations, or whether a different PPO realization could address them. It is also unknown which mechanism-focused change can improve the sector without sacrificing full-range reach and hold reliability.

**Conditional next steps:** Use the retained baseline as the parent for a mechanism-focused observation or reward experiment, with the same fixed-distribution development evaluation. If the mechanism improves the negative-angle sector while preserving broad success, pursue that mechanism; if it fails without broad regression, test a fresh replication to quantify training variance before attributing the limitation to the mechanism. Do not repeat the same aggressive exposure-only sampler, because both measured checkpoints already reject its stated prediction.

## 10469e2d-31ee-4afe-a37b-5a24da0d14bb / Experiment 2

**Result:** The focused-angle sampling intervention is rejected. The baseline checkpoint-100352 remains the working and best-known lineage, and the campaign should continue without requesting the terminal benchmark.

**Observed behavior:** The intervention's training success was 0.93 at 5,120 steps, declined to 0.77-0.85 through most checkpoints, and ended at 0.80. On the matched seed-2000 research panel, checkpoint-15360 reached 94.5% with 8 failures in the -150 to -120 degree focus sector and 3 failures outside it. Checkpoint-120832 reached 91.0% with 9 focus-sector failures and 9 outside-sector failures. The baseline checkpoint-100352 reached 97.5% on the same panel with 5 failures, all in the focus sector. Most intervention failures were reach failures rather than failures after reaching the hold region.

**Hypothesis assessment:** The recorded expectation was fewer negative-angle reach and hold failures while maintaining or improving reliability elsewhere. The recorded contradicting observation was similar focus-sector failures or a material decline outside the sector. Both contradicting outcomes occurred: focus-sector failures increased from 5 to 8 and 9, while new outside-sector failures appeared and grew from 3 to 9. The hypothesis is contradicted for this transferred run and recipe. The fixed-distribution evidence resolves the exposure-only investigation, although it cannot by itself distinguish a systematic limitation from PPO training variance.

**Interpretation:** Focused exposure did not repair the diagnosed geometric gap and progressively damaged full-range reach-and-hold behavior. The model decision is therefore to restore the established baseline recipe and retain its checkpoint-100352 lineage. The next scientific direction changes to a mechanism-focused observation or reward intervention; a fresh replication is justified only if the next decision specifically requires separating mechanism limits from stochastic training variation.

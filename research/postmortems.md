# Research postmortems

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The saved `working`/`best_known` lineage (`checkpoint-100352`) remains the strongest supported policy, with 925/960 successes across five disjoint research panels, including 197/200 and 198/200 on the two latest panels. The 98.0% task-reference result used for selection is not independent confirmation, and development panels never establish the official result. The tested hold-forfeiture and full-radius continuation changes did not improve it, while a fresh 128x128 policy was substantially worse. The policy is therefore strong but variable, and the available evidence does not establish the 98% objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies identify the learned transition but do not establish task success. The tested reward, target-coverage, and capacity interventions did not improve the saved policy; the width change lost 105-to-1 and 108-to-1 paired comparisons, and the full-radius challengers tied the control at 197/200 with no discordant outcomes. The evidence includes both missed reaches and interrupted holds, but does not separate recipe limitations from ordinary fresh-training variation. Development panels remain non-final, and the fixed task-reference panel is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the strong 64x64 baseline behavior is reproducible from fresh initialization and whether an unchanged run can produce a policy that is both reliably at least 98% successful and robust to the observed panel variation. The source of the remaining reach and hold failures also remains unresolved.

## 8635b3d2-8904-405a-a15e-d72fb56435d8 / Experiment 2

**Result:** The hold-progress forfeiture intervention weakened the trained challengers. The saved baseline remains the working and best-known lineage, and the experiment recipe should be reverted.

**Observed behavior:** On the new 200-episode research panel, the unchanged saved parent reached 189/200 successes (94.5%). `checkpoint-105472` reached 163/200 (81.5%) and `checkpoint-120832` reached 162/200 (81.0%). The paired comparisons favored the parent by 27-to-1 and 28-to-1 discordant episodes. The challengers frequently either failed to reach tolerance or entered tolerance and lost it after only 1-8 held steps; the final checkpoint also contained long interrupted-hold episodes.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted for the trained checkpoints tested. Setting `HOLD_EXIT_FORFEIT_FRACTION` to 0.5 did not improve complete uninterrupted success or preserve the baseline's behavior under continued training. This conclusion is limited to this intervention, training run, and research panel; it does not show that every hold-focused reward change will fail.

**Interpretation:** The intervention is not a useful successor to the baseline and should not be retained as a training parent. The baseline's 94.5% on a panel disjoint from its selection evidence is meaningful independent development evidence, but it remains below the 98% human objective, so no final assessment is warranted yet. Future training remains an ordinary next experiment after closure.

Although the parent reached 98.5% on this disjoint development panel, the broader pooled research evidence remains below 98% and development measurements cannot declare the objective reached, so no final assessment is requested. **Observed behavior:** On the new disjoint research panel covering episodes 4960-5159, the saved working control achieved 198/200 successes (99.0%). The conclusion is limited to this architecture, seed, training run, and measured late checkpoints; 22 other candidates remain unmeasured and the result does not rule out every capacity or training intervention.

**Interpretation:** The disjoint control result independently supports preserving the existing lineage for this closure, while the large paired losses reject widening the policy under the tested recipe. The pooled control evidence is 925/960 (96.35%), so the campaign has not established the 98% objective and should not request the official assessment from this closure. Future training, if pursued, is a separate ordinary experiment after closure.

## ce64c0ca-01b8-4d79-ab15-1ff5a6d582aa / Experiment 2

**Result:** The zero-entropy continuation did not improve the selected parent on a new disjoint research panel. The parent scored 194/200 (97.0%), while the final continuation checkpoint scored 193/200 (96.5%).

**Observed behavior:** Checkpoint-100352 at the continuation boundary scored 172/200 (86.0%), and checkpoint-105472 scored 166/200 (83.0%), despite training success proxies of 0.99 and 0.98. The final checkpoint recovered most of the lost performance but remained below the parent, with failures represented by incomplete episodes that reached the 500-step limit.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted for the tested zero-entropy intervention and measured checkpoints. Removing PPO entropy regularization did not improve complete reach-and-hold success: the early continuation checkpoints regressed substantially and the final checkpoint remained one success below the parent on the new panel. This conclusion is limited to this continuation recipe, its measured checkpoints, and the 200-episode panel; it does not reject other interventions or future training.

**Interpretation:** The experiment provides no basis for replacing the parent recipe or selecting an experiment-2 candidate. The existing checkpoint-100352 lineage remains the best-supported working and best-known policy, and the existing retained checkpoint-120832 remains a useful comparison. The experiment is closed without requesting another measurement round.

## ce64c0ca-01b8-4d79-ab15-1ff5a6d582aa / Experiment 3

**Result:** Continuing the working policy with the full official target-radius range did not improve complete reach-and-hold success. The parent remains the best-supported working policy.

**Observed behavior:** On the new disjoint research panel, the working parent achieved 193/200 (96.5%), while checkpoint-100352, checkpoint-105472, and checkpoint-120832 from the full-radius continuation each achieved 192/200 (96.0%). Each continuation candidate lost one paired episode to the parent, and the final checkpoint did not recover to the parent level. Training proxies therefore did not translate into improved measured task success; the proxy-peak checkpoint reached a training-success value of 1.0 but still scored 192/200.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Contradicted for the tested full-radius continuation and measured checkpoints. Exposing continued training to the official 6–20 cm radius range did not improve the parent on the independent panel and provided no evidence of better inner-target or hold behavior. This conclusion is limited to this changed training range, the transferred lineage, the measured checkpoints, and the 200-episode panel; unmeasured checkpoints and other interventions remain unresolved.

**Interpretation:** The intervention should not replace the parent policy or recipe. The parent lineage remains useful and should continue as both working and best-known; the experiment's changed training-environment recipe should be reverted. Further training, if pursued, is a separate next experiment rather than part of this closure.

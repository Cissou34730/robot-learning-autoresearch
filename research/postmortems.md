# Research postmortems

## 3f8899d3-51f3-4f34-8ace-9209912451c8 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** Checkpoint-100352 remains the strongest measured lineage: it achieved 393/400 successes across the two disjoint baseline panels, while the saved working policy and both measured experiment-2 checkpoints each achieved 194/200 on the fresh experiment-2 panel. Broadening training-radius coverage from 14-20 cm to 6-20 cm therefore produced no observed complete-task improvement; the measured evidence remains just below the 98% objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Training success and reward were proxies; direct measurements were needed to distinguish useful policies. On episodes 10400-10599, all three measured policies failed on the same six episodes, with three failures showing no tolerance reach and the remainder reaching it for only 1-3 samples. The reward recipe used for experiments 1-2 gave hold-progress credit but zero hold-exit forfeiture, so that term did not distinguish a transient hold from a broken accumulated hold. The scientific model establishes that success requires 100 consecutive 0.020-second in-tolerance samples; the observed transient crossings are therefore incomplete holds. These results identify a mixed reach/stability deficit but do not establish its causes across target angle, inverse-kinematic branch, or approach dynamics, and development measurements are not the official benchmark.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether making accumulated hold progress costly to lose will improve complete-task success without harming the already successful reach behavior. The contributions of target angle, inverse-kinematic branch, approach dynamics, and late hold behavior are also not separated by the current evidence.

## 3f8899d3-51f3-4f34-8ace-9209912451c8 / Experiment 1

**Result:** The baseline produced a useful but not yet objective-level policy. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as both the working and best-known lineage for continued development; the unchanged scientific recipe is kept and no alternative candidate is retained.

**Observed behavior:** The 120,832-step baseline had a late training-success plateau, with measured candidates at 95.5% (checkpoint-120832), 97% (checkpoint-100352), and 93% (checkpoint-95232) training success. On research episodes 10000-10199, the candidates achieved 198/200, 199/200, and 197/200 successes respectively; on the disjoint episodes 10200-10399 they achieved 191/200, 194/200, and 194/200 respectively for checkpoint-95232, checkpoint-100352, and checkpoint-120832. The pooled disjoint research evidence therefore favors checkpoint-100352 at 393/400 (98.25%), versus 389/400 and 391/400 for the alternatives. The fixed task-reference panel recorded 96%, 98%, and 97% for checkpoint-95232, checkpoint-100352, and checkpoint-120832, respectively, but it is not independent confirmation. For checkpoint-100352, the seven research failures across both panels included five episodes that reached tolerance but failed to complete the 100-step hold and two that never reached tolerance; the disjoint-panel failures specifically included four maximum hold streaks of 10 or 1 step and two zero-step reaches.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline clearly learned the reach-and-hold task and produced a candidate near the 98% objective, while direct task measurements supported checkpoint-100352 over the sampled alternatives. However, its 97% result on the disjoint panel and residual reach/hold failures do not support concluding that the policy reliably satisfies the human objective. The result establishes useful progress and a defensible lineage choice, not official success or causal claims about the PPO recipe.

**Interpretation:** The policy can generally move the planar two-link arm into the small target region and complete the required hold, but performance varies across target episodes and remains sensitive to final stabilization. The measured ranking is sufficiently consistent across the two research panels to preserve checkpoint-100352, while continued training or a changed method should be treated as a new experiment aimed at the remaining generalization and hold-stability failures. No additional measurement is needed for the current lineage decision because all three sampled late candidates have already been compared on a disjoint panel.

## 3f8899d3-51f3-4f34-8ace-9209912451c8 / Experiment 2

**Result:** The full-radius continuation did not improve the learned policy on its fresh disjoint panel. The saved `working` control, checkpoint-105472, and checkpoint-120832 each achieved 194/200 successes (97%), so `working` remains the selected lineage and no experiment-2 candidate is retained.

**Observed behavior:** Experiment 2 changed only training target-radius sampling, from 14-20 cm to the official 6-20 cm interval; PPO and policy parameters were unchanged. The checkpoint inventory recorded a proxy peak of 1.00 at checkpoint-105472 and 0.94 at the final checkpoint, but direct measurements on episodes 10400-10599 found no complete-task improvement. Both challengers had zero wins and zero discordant episodes against `working`. All three policies failed on the same six episode identities. For `working`, three failures never reached tolerance and three reached it only briefly, with maximum holds of 1-2 samples; the challengers showed the same binary failures and only 1-3 held samples. Since success requires 100 consecutive samples, none of these transient holds completed the task. The six failures split evenly between radii below 14 cm and radii at or above 14 cm, so the intervention did not improve the hypothesized near-radius gap while preserving the already covered region.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Not supported by this measurement. Broadening the training distribution to the full official radius interval did not change complete-task outcomes, near-radius failures, or the paired ranking on the measured panel. This is evidence against selecting either experiment-2 challenger, not proof that radius coverage has no effect under other training conditions or panels.

**Interpretation:** The policy generally performs the planar reach-and-hold task, but the remaining failures combine non-reach and severe hold-instability modes. The identical paired outcomes make another measurement unnecessary for the current lineage choice: no candidate has shown an advantage that a further comparison would clarify. The closure restores the parent scientific recipe and retains checkpoint-100352 as a strong development lineage, without requesting the official final benchmark because the independent development result remains 97%, below the 98% objective.

# Research postmortems

## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The objective is at least 98% success, or 196 of 200 episodes, on the unchanged official two-joint-arm reach-and-hold task. Across fourteen experiments and 84 development measurements, the fresh baseline reached 97.0% on the researcher panel and 98.0% on the protected task-reference panel at checkpoint-100352, while full-radius transfer in experiment 2 reached 97.0% and 98.5% and remains the strongest measured artifact. Joint shaping, targeted sampling, unchanged continuation, hold-credit forfeiture, a lower transfer learning rate, sharper closeness shaping, doubled PPO rollout length, temporal command smoothing, a fresh 128x128 policy, and removing entropy regularization did not improve that artifact; the two fresh 64x64 replications and the fresh 128x128 run were broadly weak. The campaign therefore contains one useful transferred trajectory, poor fresh trajectories, and no robust cross-panel attainment. The retained policy's failures cluster near the 1 cm boundary, while fresh failures are broad; logged reward and proxy success repeatedly diverge from measured task success. Reward curvature, target exposure, rollout aggregation, the tested low-pass execution change, the tested larger policy class, and entropy-free settling are weakened explanations under their tested recipes, while action-level control, long-horizon credit, and optimization variance remain unresolved.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, not logged reward or training success, is the policy-progress signal. Checkpoint and trajectory choice matter: several experiments showed proxy or late-training gains without task improvement. Full-radius transfer recovered two short-radius negative-angle failures from experiment 1, but experiment 2 still failed at roughly 9.4-9.9 cm and -123 to -128 degrees plus an 18.2 cm/-154.8 degree case. Detailed researcher-evaluation artifacts show that most retained-policy failures have maximum hold counts of zero or two steps and minimum distances close to the 1 cm boundary, whereas fresh failures are broad and often never approach the target. The current 11-value observation contains joint state, Cartesian error, both inverse-kinematics branch errors, direct two-joint actions, and saved normalization, so transfer is semantically compatible, not merely dimension-compatible. The lower learning rate preserved behavior but did not reduce residual count. Experiments 10-14 repeatedly showed that high reward or proxy success can coexist with unchanged or worse complete reach-and-hold success; experiment 14's entropy-free transfer retained every named task-reference failure at its early measured checkpoint and regressed later. Experiment 12's smoothing candidates likewise preserved the parent's residual set, and experiment 13's fresh 128x128 candidates corrected some named cases only while adding broad failures. The 100-control-step hold makes temporal credit a distinct unresolved consideration, but no measured result isolates it from action control, representation, or optimization trajectory. Fixed 200-episode panels are development measurements rather than the official final assessment. A transferred policy remains semantically compatible when its observation features and action coordinates are preserved; changing temporal command execution is nevertheless a behavioral intervention, not proof from tensor shape alone. Fresh initialization is not a useful proxy for the retained transfer trajectory here because experiments 5, 6, and 13 were broadly weak, but that contrast does not establish that transfer itself caused the difference.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the retained policy's near-boundary failures arise from action-level control, insufficient temporal credit for the 100-step hold, the learned policy class, or optimization variance after transfer. The smoothing result is limited to one stateful 0.5 blend, and the larger-policy result to one fresh 128x128 seed; neither separates all representation, initialization, and optimization effects. Entropy-free transfer did not improve the residual cases, but this does not rule out other optimization schedules or credit-assignment settings. The three poor fresh runs leave transfer history and seed-dependent learning confounded, and neither development panel is known to predict the official final assessment. These uncertainties describe the limits of current knowledge and do not prescribe a required next action.

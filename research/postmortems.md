# Research postmortems

## b62e8ccc-3c2e-4a3f-9477-2aa04cacd0d2 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** PPO has learned strong but variable reach-and-hold behavior: the retained experiment-2 policy `checkpoint-105472` achieved 191/200, 196/200, 198/200, 194/200, and 195/200 on disjoint research panels, while the fresh experiment-5 replication reached only 25/200 and 15/200 against a 194/200 working control. The experiment-6 short-radius-focused continuation reached 193/200 at its measured intermediate checkpoint and 181/200 at its final checkpoint, both below the 195/200 working control on the same new panel. Reward forfeiture, lower learning rate, and short-radius oversampling therefore weakened transferred performance, leaving residual reach and hold failures and no official demonstration of the 98% objective.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies do not rank task behavior reliably, and continued optimization has degraded behavior after stronger intermediate checkpoints. The experiment-5 fresh run had zero recorded training success at all checkpoints despite reward increasing late, and its measured checkpoints lost to the working control by 169-0 and 179-0 discordant paired outcomes. On the experiment-3 panel, the unchanged parent achieved 196/200 versus 189/200 and 194/200; on the experiment-4 panel it achieved 198/200 versus 186/200 and 182/200; on the experiment-6 panel it achieved 195/200 versus 193/200 and 181/200, with paired comparisons favoring the parent 2-0 and 14-0. The selected working lineage totals 974/1000 across five disjoint research panels, including the new panel that was not used to select it, but this remains development evidence below a reliable 98% claim. The fixed task-reference panel is reused, and all development measurements remain distinct from the official 200-episode verdict.

**Open questions:** The causes of the retained policy's residual reach and interrupted-hold failures remain unresolved, as does whether another training intervention can improve them without the late-training degradation seen in multiple continuations. Fresh learning under the tested full-radius recipe and the tested short-radius-focused continuation were poor, and the official objective has not been demonstrated.

# Research postmortems

## d862f6d9-2222-4dd9-9cb5-63921a7b5c7f / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the unchanged 6–20 cm official target distribution. Experiment 2 provides measured progress after expanding transfer training to the full radius range: `checkpoint-120832` reached 98.5% (197/200) on the protected task-reference panel and 98.0% (196/200) on the researcher evaluation. Its remaining task-reference failures are still concentrated at negative angles, including two short targets and one outer target. This candidate is the strongest measured policy and is selected for terminal assessment, while the development panels remain distinct from the official panel.

**Lessons and limits:** Direct task measurements, rather than training proxy success or reward, support the experiment-2 progress claim. Relative to experiment 1 `checkpoint-100352`, the endpoint reduced short-radius task-reference failures below 10 cm from four to two, kept the 10–15 cm group at 59/59, and retained 83/84 success in the 15–20 cm group; the net task-reference result improved from 98.0% to 98.5%. Under the compatible experiment-2 research-evaluation semantics, both measured experiment-2 checkpoints scored 98.0% versus 97.0% for the current working policy, with two candidate wins and no reference wins in the paired summaries. The training proxy nevertheless reached 1.00 near `checkpoint-105472` and declined to 0.94 at the endpoint, so it did not predict the endpoint's direct task result. The task-reference panel is fixed development evidence, the research evaluation is one 200-episode sample, and the single transfer run does not establish causal attribution or learning-process reproducibility. All observed task-reference failures truncated at 500 steps and remained at negative angles.

**Open questions:** The official 200-episode panel may differ from both development measurements, and the variability of a fresh learning process remains uncharacterized. The residual negative-angle failures could reflect radius-angle interaction, control or optimization limits, or sampling variability; the current measurements do not distinguish these explanations.

## d862f6d9-2222-4dd9-9cb5-63921a7b5c7f / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh baseline produced a useful near-objective policy. `checkpoint-100352` is selected as both the working and best-known lineage, with terminal assessment deferred.

**Observed behavior:** Raw training success rose from 0 through 70,656 steps to 0.98 at 99,328 steps, then was 0.97 at 100,352, 0.95 at 115,712, and 0.95 at 120,832. Research evaluation measured 97.0% (6 failures), 96.5% (7 failures), and 97.0% (6 failures) at checkpoints 100352, 115712, and 120832 respectively; every research-evaluation failure truncated at 500 steps. The protected task-reference panel measured 98.0% (196/200), 96.5% (193/200), and 97.0% (194/200) at those same checkpoints. For `checkpoint-100352`, the four task-reference failures were all short targets from 6.73-9.91 cm at negative angles from -116.4 to -127.9 degrees, with final distances 0.986-1.325 cm. The 10-15 cm task-reference group succeeded 100% for all three checkpoints; the 15-20 cm group succeeded 100%, 100%, and 98.81%.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The baseline established substantial learned task competence and reached the objective percentage on the protected development panel at `checkpoint-100352`, but later checkpoints did not preserve or improve that result and the independent researcher evaluation remained at 97.0% or below. These measurements support selecting the earlier checkpoint, not a claim that the policy reliably satisfies the official objective.

**Interpretation:** The direct task measurements identify `checkpoint-100352` as the strongest available candidate. The agreement between the 98.0% task-reference result and the 97.0% research result supports meaningful progress, while their difference and the repeated short negative-angle failures limit confidence in terminal readiness. The paired comparisons also provide no evidence that the later checkpoints improve over `checkpoint-100352` (100352 versus 115712: -0.5 percentage points; 100352 versus 120832: 0.0 points on shared episodes). No researcher-owned code or parameters changed in this baseline, so no intervention effect is attributable.

## d862f6d9-2222-4dd9-9cb5-63921a7b5c7f / Experiment 2

**Result:** The full-radius transfer intervention was partially supported by direct task behavior. `checkpoint-120832` is selected as the working and best-known lineage, the changed training recipe is kept, and the candidate is requested for terminal official assessment.

**Observed behavior:** Training completed 120,832 local steps. The training proxy reached 1.00 at `checkpoint-105472` and was 0.94 at `checkpoint-120832`; the corresponding mean rewards were 110.18 and 110.99. Research evaluation scored both measured checkpoints at 98.0% (196/200), while the compatible current working policy scored 97.0% (194/200). The paired research comparisons recorded two candidate wins and no reference wins for each checkpoint. On the fixed task-reference panel, `checkpoint-105472` scored 98.0% (196/200) and `checkpoint-120832` scored 98.5% (197/200). At the endpoint, the 6–10 cm group was 55/57, the 10–15 cm group was 59/59, and the 15–20 cm group was 83/84. Its three failures were two short negative-angle targets (9.915 cm at -122.902 degrees and 9.355 cm at -127.912 degrees) and one 18.240 cm target at -154.787 degrees; all truncated at 500 steps.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The expected short-target improvement occurred: endpoint failures below 10 cm fell from four for experiment 1 `checkpoint-100352` to two, while the 10–15 cm range remained perfect and overall task-reference success increased to 98.5%. The 15–20 cm group retained one failure rather than the baseline's zero, and negative-angle failures persisted. The compatible research evaluation also improved from 97.0% for the current working policy to 98.0%, but the paired evidence contains only two discordant episodes. These observations support the usefulness of the tested intervention under the measured conditions, not a causal claim that radius coverage alone produced the improvement.

**Interpretation:** Expanding target-radius exposure is a useful recipe change for this campaign: the endpoint is directly better than the measured intermediate checkpoint and is the strongest measured policy on both development instruments. The lower late training proxy is an orthogonal warning against selecting by training metrics, but it does not outweigh the endpoint's direct task measurements. Two 200-episode development measurements at or above 98% justify terminal assessment of the frozen candidate, while the distinct official panel and residual angle concentration remain uncertainty about the final verdict.

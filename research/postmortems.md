## dd6d401e-1c3b-46d0-8ecf-3b9304fbff2a / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The baseline learned strong reach-and-hold behavior, making `checkpoint-100352` the current working and best-known candidate, but the human objective remains unestablished until the official panel. Across six distinct research panels, working is 1177/1200 (98.1%), including 198/200 on the newest disjoint panel. The full hold-credit-forfeiture intervention produced no improvement over working at its proxy peak, while its final checkpoint regressed to 155/200 with broad missed-reach and hold-instability failures. The earlier target-exposure and branch-feasibility interventions likewise did not produce a supported replacement.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, rather than training reward or training success, supports the lineage choice. On episodes 7000-7199, the experiment-5 proxy peak tied working at 198/200 with zero discordant wins, sharing both failure episodes; working reached tolerance but held only one step on those cases, whereas the candidate did not reach tolerance. The final checkpoint had 45 failures and 43 working wins, showing that late training under the changed reward damaged both reach and hold behavior. The development aggregate is now slightly above 98%, but all such measurements remain non-official and the fixed task-reference panel is not independent confirmation.

**Open questions:** The causal source of the remaining failures—trajectory, settling, posture, or control margin—remains unresolved, and the reward intervention did not isolate it. The independent development evidence is strong enough to submit the unchanged working lineage for the official 98% assessment, but only that terminal panel can determine whether the human objective is met.

The shared failure geometry and the occasional short holds indicate that the remaining deficit may involve posture selection, settling, or hold-margin control in addition to reach coverage; the available measurements do not identify the mechanism. The experiment-2 recipe should therefore be reverted while preserving the previously selected policy.

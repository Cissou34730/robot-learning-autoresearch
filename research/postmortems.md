# Research postmortems

## d1897119-e054-4e2a-a2fd-06958f8ce0bd / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the official 6–20 cm reach-and-hold distribution. The unchanged fresh PPO baseline reached 51.0% on the task-reference panel, full-radius training raised a transferred checkpoint to 82.5%, and unchanged continuation reached 83.0% on the repeated task-reference panel and 87.5% on a fresh 400-episode research panel. The experiment-4 three-band sampler, transferred from that learned policy, produced the strongest measured artifact at 92.5% on task-reference-v1 and 93.5% on the fresh research panel. A fresh seed-1 replication of the sampler instead reached 23.0% and 25.5% on those respective panels, so the strong transferred result is not established as reproducible from fresh initialization. The campaign has made substantial measured progress but remains below 98% with no official confirmation.

**Lessons and limits:** The original 14–20 cm training distribution missed the short-radius portion of the official task. Full-radius training recovered short and mid radii but traded away long-radius successes during later optimization; experiment 3 ended at 42/57, 50/59, and 74/84 by radius versus 50/57, 54/59, and 61/84 for its parent. Experiment 4 improved those bands to 52/57, 58/59, and 75/84, with 37 fresh-panel successes unique to its endpoint versus 13 unique to the prior working policy. Its 65536-step proxy/reward peak instead reached 82/84 long-radius successes while scoring lower overall, so reward and proxy metrics do not reliably select task policies. Experiment 5 reinforces that limit: its proxy was 0.94 and endpoint reward 173.15, yet it achieved only 46/200 reference and 102/400 fresh-panel successes; on the shared reference panel, experiment 4 had 142 successes not shared by experiment 5 versus 3 in the reverse direction. This establishes process variability under the tested fresh seed, not its cause. Across these interventions, the protected evaluator, task mechanics, observation layout, action mapping, and policy I/O remained unchanged.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the best-known learned policy has useful headroom under the unchanged training dynamics, or whether further optimization produces a plateau or shifts the short-, mid-, and long-radius tradeoff. It is also unresolved how much of the experiment-5 replication gap is due to fresh-initialization variance, training trajectory, checkpoint selection, or interaction with the sampler; the available measurements do not separate those explanations.

All three experiment-2 measurements used task-reference-v1 with seed 7300; this is development evidence, not the official final assessment.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The result supports the exploratory expectation that full-radius training can improve the short-radius deficit and materially raise aggregate protected-task success: the selected checkpoint's 85 parent-exclusive successes versus 22 parent-exclusive successes are consistent with meaningful behavioral progress. The preservation clause is not fully supported because the horizon-matched checkpoint lost six successes in the 15-20 cm band, and the endpoint's 80.5% result does not show monotonic late improvement. The intervention was transferred from the parent and trained further rather than compared with a contemporaneous unchanged control, so these measurements do not isolate radius coverage as the sole cause. The high training proxy also remains an orthogonal, non-acceptance signal.

**Interpretation:** Matching the training radius support to the official support is a promising route for the dominant short-radius failure mode, and candidate-32a2902d is a materially better measured policy than the prior working lineage. The evidence does not support official assessment yet: the best development result is 31.5 percentage points below the objective, uses one repeated development panel, and leaves a long-radius tradeoff unresolved. The selected horizon-matched checkpoint should become working and best-known; the endpoint is worth retaining as a complementary late-training alternative because it has one more short-radius success despite lower aggregate performance.

## d1897119-e054-4e2a-a2fd-06958f8ce0bd / Experiment 4

**Result:** Emphasizing 6-10 cm targets during transfer training produced the strongest measured policy of the campaign, but not a policy ready for the official objective. Candidate-4d0018a6 reached 92.5% (185/200) on the repeated task-reference panel and 93.5% (374/400) on the fresh research panel; the 98% objective remains unmet.

**Observed behavior:** Training completed 120832 steps and produced 24 checkpoints, of which 3 were measured. The training log's proxy success reached 0.99 at several early and mid-run points, including the measured 65536-step and 100352-step candidates, then fell to 0.96 at the endpoint; mean episode reward similarly fell from 192.42 at 65536 steps to 184.31 at 120832 steps. On task-reference-v1, candidate-daae19fb achieved 184/200 with radius results 46/57, 56/59, and 82/84; candidate-171ef7b2 achieved 182/200 with 48/57, 58/59, and 76/84; and endpoint candidate-4d0018a6 achieved 185/200 with 52/57, 58/59, and 75/84 for 6-10, 10-15, and 15-20 cm. On the fresh 400-episode panel, the same candidates achieved 92.0%, 93.0%, and 93.5%, respectively. Against the experiment-3 working policy on shared episodes, the endpoint had 28 task-reference successes not shared by the parent versus 9 parent-only successes, and 37 fresh-panel successes not shared versus 13 parent-only successes. No official benchmark was run.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Partially supported. The exploratory question is supported in the tested transfer run: the endpoint recovered 10 short-radius and 8 mid-radius successes relative to the experiment-3 endpoint on the repeated panel, while long-radius success increased by one, and aggregate success improved on both development panels. This is not evidence that the sampler alone caused the improvement because the comparison is not a contemporaneous unchanged control and uses one training run. The preservation claim is also limited: the endpoint retained the parent's panel long-radius level, but the reward/proxy peak reached 82/84 long-radius successes while giving up short-radius performance. The contrasting checkpoint behavior therefore indicates a remaining tradeoff rather than uniform bandwise improvement.

**Interpretation:** Measured task behavior, rather than the training proxy, identifies candidate-4d0018a6 as the useful policy: it is the highest-scoring experiment-4 candidate on both measured panels and materially exceeds the prior working policy under shared episode identities. The endpoint's lower proxy and reward than the 65536-step checkpoint are an unexpected orthogonal signal that late training metrics are not a reliable acceptance or checkpoint-selection criterion here. Development evidence is still 4.5 percentage points below the objective on the fresh panel, uses one repeated panel and one fresh seed, and does not justify official assessment.

## d1897119-e054-4e2a-a2fd-06958f8ce0bd / Experiment 5

**Result:** The fresh-initialization replication of the experiment-4 sampler did not reproduce its measured task performance. Candidate-65013bc7 reached 23.0% (46/200) on task-reference-v1 and 25.5% (102/400) on a new research panel, so it is not a useful replacement for the experiment-4 working lineage.

**Observed behavior:** Training completed 120832 steps and produced 24 checkpoints. The endpoint had inventory training success 0.94 and mean episode reward 173.15, but the task-reference endpoint succeeded in only 11/57 short-radius, 12/59 mid-radius, and 23/84 long-radius episodes; 154/200 episodes truncated at the 500-step limit. On the same task-reference panel, experiment-4 candidate-4d0018a6 had 142 successes not shared by experiment 5, while experiment 5 had 3 successes not shared by experiment 4. The fresh research panel contained 102/400 successes, with 298 truncated episodes. No official benchmark was run.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Weakened. The expected fresh-run reproduction of experiment 4's materially improved policy was not observed: both aggregate panels were far below experiment 4's 92.5% reference and 93.5% fresh-panel results, and the short-, mid-, and long-radius bands all regressed on the shared reference panel. This supports the stated alternative that the earlier result depended on learning-process conditions such as transfer or trajectory, but the replication does not identify which factor caused the gap and does not disprove the sampler's usefulness under other conditions. The endpoint's 0.94 training proxy alongside 25.5% fresh-panel task success is an orthogonal discrepancy, not evidence that the proxy measures the human objective.

**Interpretation:** Measured task behavior supports retaining experiment 4 as working and best-known, while the fresh seed-1 candidate should not be promoted or retained as a reusable policy. The result reduces confidence in generalizing the experiment-4 transfer outcome to fresh initialization; it does not warrant the official benchmark because the selected policy remains below the 98% development target and experiment 5 is substantially worse. Further training is a separate future experiment, not part of this closure.

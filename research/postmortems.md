# Research postmortems

## 9280ffcf-9e77-494f-9cff-e2a75c9e97ea / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The unchanged PPO baseline learned a strong reach-and-hold policy after roughly 86,000 steps. Checkpoint-100352 is the best-supported candidate: it achieved 151/160 on the first research panel and 199/200 on the later disjoint panel. Checkpoint-120832 was effectively tied on the first panel and achieved 198/200 on the same disjoint panel, but was one success behind on the fresh comparison and one percentage point below checkpoint-100352 on the reused task-reference panel. The disjoint result supports expecting the objective-level behavior to generalize, but the official objective remains unverified until the final assessment.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, rather than training reward, selected the useful checkpoint: the reward-peak checkpoint-86016 was weaker than both later candidates. The fixed task-reference score for checkpoint-100352 was used in selection and is therefore not independent confirmation; the seed-5000 research panel supplies the independent confirmation. Residual failures in the measured panels concentrate around negative angles near -120 to -151 degrees, with occasional failures at farther radii, so the policy is strong but not uniformly characterized. Development panels are not the official 200-episode verdict.

**Open questions:** Whether checkpoint-100352 reaches at least 196/200 on the official final panel remains unresolved. If development continues after a non-reaching verdict, the angular failure cluster is the clearest measured target for a subsequent experiment; no causal claim about its source has been established.

## 9280ffcf-9e77-494f-9cff-e2a75c9e97ea / Experiment 1

**Result:** The fresh PPO baseline produced a policy that is close to, and on the independent panel above, the 98% campaign objective. Checkpoint-100352 is selected as both the working and best-known lineage; checkpoint-120832 is retained as a measured alternative, and the unchanged scientific recipe is kept.

**Observed behavior:** Training proxies rose sharply after 75,776 steps, but the reward peak at checkpoint-86016 was not the strongest measured policy. On the first research panel, checkpoint-100352 and checkpoint-120832 each succeeded on 151/160 episodes (94.375%), while checkpoint-86016 succeeded on 149/160. On a new disjoint research panel, checkpoint-100352 succeeded on 199/200 (99.5%) and checkpoint-120832 on 198/200 (99.0%); their paired comparison had one discordant win for checkpoint-100352 and none for checkpoint-120832. The fixed task-reference panel reported 98% for checkpoint-100352, 97% for checkpoint-120832, and 94% for checkpoint-86016, but it was used for selection and is permanently reused. The four checkpoint-100352 failures on that panel all had target angles between -116 and -128 degrees; the independent-panel failures were also concentrated in the negative-angle region.

**Hypothesis assessment:** Supported as a baseline establishment: the run produced a learned policy with strong measured task behavior and a clear candidate for lineage selection. The evidence supports checkpoint-100352 over the reward-peak and final checkpoints under the measured conditions, but it does not itself establish the official 98% result or explain the residual angular failures.

**Interpretation:** Checkpoint-100352 is the strongest available policy because it has the best independent-panel result and the highest task-reference result, with the dependence of the latter explicitly discounted. Keeping the current recipe is appropriate because no scientific code or parameter intervention was tested. Retaining checkpoint-120832 preserves a nearly equivalent later policy for future comparison or continuation without treating its later training position as inherently superior. The independent 99.5% panel supports requesting the official final assessment rather than spending another measurement round before closure.

## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the unchanged official two-joint-arm task. Experiment 1's fresh PPO baseline reached 97.0% on the researcher panel and 98.0% on the protected task-reference panel at checkpoint-100352, with endpoint regression on both panels. Experiment 2 transferred that policy and trained over the official 6-20 cm radius range; its selected checkpoint reached 97.0% and 98.5% on the two panels. The transferred shaping intervention in experiment 3 preserved the mid-run result and regressed at the endpoint, while experiment 4's targeted curriculum fell below the unchanged policy and did not repair the persistent protected-panel failures. Experiment 5 used the unchanged full-radius recipe from a fresh seed, but reached only 63.5-67.5% on the researcher panel and 64.5-66.5% on task-reference. Thus experiment 2 checkpoint-100352 remains the strongest measured development policy, but robust cross-panel attainment has not been established and the fresh result does not establish whether its gap is caused by initialization history or stochastic optimization. This is provisional memory for reassessment and does not prescribe a next action.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, rather than logged reward or training success, is the policy-progress signal. Checkpoint choice and training trajectory matter: experiments 1-3 all selected earlier or intermediate candidates over endpoints, and experiment 4 again showed proxy improvement without task improvement. Full-radius transfer in experiment 2 recovered two of experiment 1's four short-radius negative-angle failures, but left failures at 9.4-9.9 cm near -123 to -128 degrees and at 18.2 cm/-154.8 degrees. The shaping and focused-sampling interventions did not remove those cases. Experiment 5's fresh-seed-1 checkpoints at 100352, 110592, and 120832 steps scored 63.5%, 67.5%, and 64.5% on the researcher panel and 64.5%, 65.5%, and 66.5% on task-reference; its fresh failures were broad rather than concentrated only on experiment 2's residual cases. Its reward rose from about -20 to 95 while logged success reached only 1%, reinforcing the proxy/task mismatch. The one fresh replication therefore separates from experiment 2 but cannot causally distinguish initialization history from seed-dependent PPO optimization. The learned input remains the 11-value state containing both inverse-kinematics branch errors, and the physical output remains the two joint controls; this representation and mapping are shared by training and inference. The fixed 200-episode panels are development evidence, not the official final assessment.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the selected policy's residual failures reflect target coverage, PPO optimization dynamics, or a policy limitation. It also remains unresolved whether experiment 2's near-objective behavior is attainable from fresh initialization with substantial probability, or whether transfer history materially changes the learned trajectory; experiment 5 provides only one fresh draw. It remains unknown whether any policy improvement on residual regions preserves broad success and whether development-panel measurements predict the official final assessment.

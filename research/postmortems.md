# Research postmortems

## df7588aa-bb83-4dcf-a7c3-300afb63ee97 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the complete two-second reach-and-hold task under the unchanged official distribution. The selected fresh PPO baseline checkpoint reached 94.5% on each of two disjoint research panels and 98.0% on the reused task-reference panel, while unchanged controls later reached 98.5% and 99.5% on fresh panels and then 96.5% on another. This mixed history shows useful competence but does not establish objective-level performance or justify terminal assessment. The hold-stability reward, angle-oversampling, and full-radius transfer interventions supplied no stable improvement over the unchanged lineage. Failures were often concentrated in the negative-angle sector and ended at the episode limit, but that association does not identify a causal control or representation defect.

**Lessons and limits:** Training proxies are informative but not acceptance evidence: the unchanged run's task-proxy success peaked around 97% at checkpoint-100352 and fell to 95% by checkpoint-120832, while the modified reward run reached 0.99 training success and 112.056 reward at checkpoint-95232 but produced lower task success than its unchanged control. The angle and radius interventions likewise had proxy or early-panel signals that did not replicate against unchanged controls. These observations weaken the tested transferred recipes without disproving every reward, exposure schedule, or training seed. A fresh-run question changes the decision of whether the unchanged recipe itself can reliably yield a policy suitable for selection or terminal assessment, and therefore has more direct objective value than continuation after repeated late proxy declines. A negative-angle diagnostic could instead change the design of a future control or representation intervention, but the current evidence is only associative. Alternate radius curricula and further continuation have similarly weaker immediate decision value after the failed transfer tests. The task-reference panel is reused and selection-contaminated; all development evidence remains distinct from the official benchmark and does not establish training-process reproducibility.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved how variable the unchanged PPO recipe is from fresh initialization, whether a different radius curriculum or fresh recipe can help, whether negative-angle failures reflect a broader control or representation limitation, why the reused task-reference panel differs from earlier disjoint panels, and what the official benchmark result would be. These uncertainties are recorded for reassessment; they are not a priority list or a prescribed next action.

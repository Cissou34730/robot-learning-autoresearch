## abd93958-a0b2-4d26-b212-96ebeb401d22 / Scientific strategy

**Current synthesis:** The campaign objective is at least 98% success on the unchanged official two-joint-arm task. Experiment 1's fresh PPO baseline reached 97.0% on the researcher panel and 98.0% on the protected task-reference panel at checkpoint-100352, with endpoint regression on both panels. Experiment 2 transferred that policy and trained over the official 6-20 cm radius range; its checkpoint-100352 reached 97.0% and 98.5% on the two development panels and remains the strongest measured artifact. The transferred shaping intervention in experiment 3 preserved the mid-run result but did not change the residual failures, while experiment 4's targeted curriculum reduced broad task success. Experiments 5 and 6 repeated the unchanged full-radius recipe from fresh seeds and reached only 63.5-67.5% and 62.5-67.0% on the researcher panel, with task-reference ranges of 64.5-66.5% and 63.5-66.5%. The evidence therefore separates the retained transferred trajectory from two poor fresh trajectories, but does not identify transfer history as causal or establish robust cross-panel attainment. This is provisional memory for reassessment and does not prescribe a next action.

**Lessons and limits:** Measured task success, rather than logged reward or training success, is the policy-progress signal. Checkpoint choice and training trajectory matter: experiments 1-3 selected earlier or intermediate candidates over endpoints, and experiments 4 and 6 showed proxy improvement without task improvement. Full-radius transfer in experiment 2 recovered two of experiment 1's four short-radius negative-angle failures, but left failures at 9.4-9.9 cm near -123 to -128 degrees and at 18.2 cm/-154.8 degrees. The shaping and focused-sampling interventions did not remove those cases. The fresh runs had broad target-geometry failures, unlike the retained policy's narrow residual pattern. In experiment 6, reward rose from -7.6 at 5,120 steps to 109.1 at 100,352 before falling to 99.8 at the endpoint, while logged success rose only to 22-26%; measured task success stayed low. The retained artifact and the current training method use the same 11-value observation containing both inverse-kinematics branch errors, direct two-joint controls, and saved observation normalization, so transfer is semantically compatible rather than merely dimension-compatible. These observations reinforce the proxy/task mismatch and leave seed variance, transfer history, and policy limitation confounded. The fixed 200-episode panels are development evidence, not the official final assessment.

**Open questions:** It remains unresolved whether the selected policy's residual failures reflect target coverage, PPO optimization dynamics, or a policy limitation. It is unknown whether further unchanged updates from the retained transferred policy can improve those residual regions without eroding broad behavior, and whether that trajectory differs from the poor fresh runs for reasons beyond seed-dependent optimization. It also remains unknown whether development-panel measurements predict the official final assessment.

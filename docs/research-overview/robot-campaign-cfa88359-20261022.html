2. What the arm had to do

A simulated two-joint arm had to reach targets 6 to 20 cm from its base, at any bearing. Its hand had to stay within 1 cm of the target for 100 uninterrupted control steps, or two seconds. Each episode allowed 500 control steps. The official goal was at least 196 successes in 200 episodes.

The PI was the AI principal investigator. It chose questions, changes and the final assessment. Runner executed operations, enforced boundaries and recorded results. Coder and reviewer agents implemented and checked scientific changes.

A checkpoint is a model saved during training. A panel is a set of test episodes. Research evaluation used the PI-owned evaluator. The protected task-reference panel supported development. Neither was the separate official assessment that ended the campaign.

Reward and training-success displays were useful clues, not substitutes for complete-hold evaluation. The early run's reward rose while its training-success proxy stayed at zero.

3. The change: teach one side, reflect the other

The physical task is symmetric across the horizontal axis. A target above that axis has a matching target below it. The first learned policy, T1, behaved very differently on those two sides.

T2 used a common coordinate frame for the learned policy, called canonical coordinates. When the target had positive y, the runtime reflected the observation into the y <= 0 half-plane. After the neural policy chose an action, the runtime negated both controls to reflect that action back. Targets already on the lower side needed no reflection.

Physical target and arm state: choose whether reflection is needed.

Present the same kind of coordinates to the learned policy

Target at y <= 0

Use the observation and learned action without reflection.

Target at y > 0

Reflect the observation, get the learned action, then negate both controls.

Apply the learned command in the physical world

This was a structured representation, not an analytic-controller rescue. The learned PPO policy still generated the actions. The final runtime had no inverse-kinematics or PD controller on its action path. The separate M1 analytic controller was a feasibility diagnostic only.

The only scientific source change for T2 training was robot_learning/scenario/policy_io.py. The network, reward and 6–20 cm training exposure were retained. Both T1 and T2 trained from scratch with seed 23; T2 did not continue T1's weights. Each requested 120,000 steps and completed 120,832 actual steps. These are requested and actual counts, not evidence of a training-allocation violation. [State and provenance]

A trained policy need not learn physical symmetry by itself. T1's asymmetry was a learned-policy symmetry weakness, not proof of a software bug.

6. What improved, and what remains uncertain

Useful scientific behavior

  • The PI moved from a broad training-coverage change to a specific, testable symmetry weakness.
  • A large paired gain followed a targeted representation change: 89 gains and zero losses against T1 on M4, not merely a small pooled-score increase.
  • M6 checked the intended mechanism. In one fully checked mirrored pair, residuals were zero for raw scenario observations, canonical inputs, raw actor equality, applied controls, joint states and hand positions.
  • The PI corrected M5's diagnostic assumptions instead of treating its old labels as valid.
  • Goal review obtained independent M7 evidence after the selected perfect M4. It did not require an unnecessary third inquiry to do so.
  • It separated candidate selection, protected validation and official assessment, and reported uncertainty before committing.

Limits that the pass does not remove

  • Both fresh training runs used seed 23. There is no cross-seed reproducibility result.
  • Three T2 checkpoints were screened. The selected checkpoint's general superiority is unproved.
  • The symmetry tests and large gains support the tested method, not a claim that symmetry explains every T1 failure or that all learned policies need this mapping.
  • M5 was a genuine diagnostic-assumption error that required correction.
  • Evidence precision and context growth remain concerns. One clean finish does not close them.
  • One campaign cannot isolate the effects of individual harness changes or establish how often this process succeeds.

Judgment: this was better scientific behavior, not only a fortunate final score. The mechanism check, strong paired gain, new research panel and protected validation support that judgment. Keep the balanced goal-review wording and uncertainty display. This report does not automatically close any outstanding issue.

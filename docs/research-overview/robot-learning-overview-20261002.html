02 / THE CAMPAIGN LOOP

Current runtime and scientific-session boundaries

A campaign can contain several inquiries. An inquiry can contain several training runs and measurements.

Startup does not require training

The PI can build tools, change its method, or measure before training.

A startup training request may be shorter than the maintainer's allocation. It cannot be larger.

Inquiry training still uses the full allocation. Completed steps can round up at a rollout boundary.

Current goal-review behavior

Closing an inquiry does not mean the campaign has passed.

A development score above the goal does not automatically justify assessment.

Consider remaining failures, regressions, and uncertainty before choosing assessment.

Goal review can measure. Its permissions do not depend on the inquiry count. It does not train.

The temporary inquiry cap does not establish scientific exhaustion.

An official failure ends the campaign. It does not automatically return to another inquiry.

Current runtime phase behavior

PhasePurpose and permitted workHow it ends
Preliminary scientific modelRead the robot and task contracts. Write established facts, physical consequences, and unknowns before campaign evidence exists.The model document is accepted and published. It then remains fixed for this campaign.
StartupEstablish the first research direction. Build tools, change PI-owned code, measure, train, assign roles, or restore a recipe.A PI checkpoint records the direction and evidence. A fresh goal-review session follows.

Goal, campaign, inquiry, session, operation, and AI invocation are different units
Human goal
The fixed task and success criterion. Lower-level milestones do not replace it.
Campaign
One attempt to reach the goal. It starts with independent records and a new scientific model.
Inquiry
A temporary question that can change the route to the goal. It has a question, goal connection, and closure condition.
Scientific session
Work on one coherent objective. It can include several operations. A checkpoint ends the session.
Operation
One accepted request, such as measurement, training, or checkpoint publication.
PI invocation
One call to the AI backend. Several invocations can belong to the same scientific session.

A completed training run is not automatically a completed session, inquiry, or campaign.

Continuation, reframing, closure, and the inquiry limit

Continue: keep the inquiry open and save a checkpoint. A fresh session continues the same question.

Reframe: change the active question and its connection to the goal. Checkpoint before further work.

Close: record the outcome and reason. After checkpointing, return to goal review.

Inquiry closure may follow a useful answer, a changed direction, or rejection of that inquiry's route. It does not establish campaign exhaustion.

The current 15-inquiry cap is a temporary execution limit, not a scientific decision signal.

After the final permitted inquiry closes and checkpoints, the launcher pauses without a terminal state. Active work finishes normally.

The maintainer can resume by explicitly raising -MaxInquiries at that paused boundary. Measurements and model-role permissions do not change. Assessment still requires an explicit best-known candidate.

The maintainer intends to remove the cap when the scientist is ready. Cap removal is not implemented.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY AND OWNERSHIP

The PI decides the science. The Runner executes the requests.

Maintainer

Defines the goal and protected task.

Changes the harness and training allocation.

Starts, stops, and resets campaigns.

Principal Investigator / PI

Chooses questions, methods, and tools.

Changes PI-owned research code.

Interprets completed evidence.

Decides whether to request assessment.

Runner

Checks contracts and file ownership.

Executes accepted operations.

Records outcomes and artifacts.

Does not judge scientific readiness.

The two environment files are both PI-owned

The split separates shared research mechanics from training-specific code. It is not a human/PI ownership split.

PI freedom remains inside the scientific boundary

The PI can replace its methods, tools, reward, observations, and training implementation.

It cannot change the official robot, task, success rule, harness, tests, or dependency manifests.

All Python execution uses uv run. The PI uses Runner requests for training and protected evaluation.

04 / THE OPERATION LOOP

From a PI request to a recorded result

The launcher resumes pending work before asking for another action. The Runner executes requests; the PI interprets their results.

StepActionRecord or boundary
1. PrepareThe PI chooses an action and states its scientific reason. It may change PI-owned code or build a tool.Changes must remain within the owned surface.
2. SubmitThe PI writes one operation to research\operation_request.json.The request has one top-level operation kind.
3. ValidateThe Runner checks shape, phase permissions, ownership, allocation, and instrument-specific conditions.A structurally valid request is not a scientific endorsement.
4. AcceptThe Runner saves the accepted request as a pending operation and assigns its identity.Accepted request identity is fixed during execution. M# means measurement, T# training, and E# another event.
5. ExecuteThe Runner executes the instrument or requested state transition.Training produces candidates. Measurement produces factual outputs and detailed artifacts.
6. PublishThe Runner saves outcomes, provenance, artifacts, and operation history.Only completed operations can be cited as scientific evidence.
7. ReturnThe launcher regenerates the brief and returns feedback to the PI.The PI inspects evidence and decides the next action in the same session.
What happens when the operation does not complete normally
SituationProcess responseScientific meaning
Invalid requestReturn the validation reason. The launcher permits one correction retry, then stops if the request remains invalid.No accepted scientific result exists.
Scientific-code execution errorPreserve pending state and return the error to the same PI session. The PI can repair the implementation or explicitly replace the failed operation.The exception is execution history, not a measurement against the method.
Publication failure after executionPreserve the result and pending transaction. Stop for publication repair. Resume publication rather than retraining.A produced result must not be confused with a new training request.
Maintainer stopEnd the launcher activity while preserving durable state. The maintainer decides whether to resume or reset.A stop is not an official pass, official fail, or scientific conclusion.

A completed method can perform poorly and still provide useful evidence. A code exception is a different situation.

Startup allocation and inquiry allocation

The maintainer supplies a per-run training allocation. The default is 120,000 requested steps.

During startup, the PI can request any positive step count up to that allocation.

During an inquiry, a training request must use the full allocation.

Neither phase requires training. Neither the PI nor the Runner may raise the maintainer's allocation.

Completed steps can round up to a learning rollout boundary. The ceiling controls the request, not strict executed-step stopping.

05 / EVIDENCE, CONTEXT, AND CANDIDATES

Evidence records and model roles

RecordPurposeLimit

research\research_state.jsonCurrent inquiry, session, pending operation, candidates, roles, and checkpoint.A pending operation is not completed evidence.

research\evaluations\Detailed measurement artifacts referenced by completed operations.Find and inspect the data needed for the decision. A summary is not the full artifact.

Candidate and recipe are different

Standard candidate publication is training-centered. Training produces registered learned-policy candidates.

A controller artifact created during measurement is not automatically a registered candidate.

A recipe is the scientific code and configuration associated with that candidate.

Recipe restoration changes the PI-owned recipe. It does not assign a model role.

Training statistics can identify candidates worth inspecting. They do not establish development or official performance.

Model roles require explicit assignment

Working: the candidate selected for current work.

Best-known: the strongest available candidate supported by completed evidence.

Retained: a candidate archived under an explicit label.

Training does not assign these roles automatically. Best-known assignment does not require official assessment.

What a checkpoint must preserve

The checkpoint records the connection to the human goal and the remaining gap.

Its synthesis distinguishes measurements from explanations. It retains supporting and contradictory evidence.

Its decision frontier states the unresolved distinction and the evidence that could resolve or redirect it.

Its next direction states the selected action or closure and the reason.

It also records completed operations, candidate roles, evidence references, and cumulative resource use.

A new session receives this durable context. It does not depend only on the previous conversation.

Development measurement and official assessment

Development measurements inform the PI's judgment. They do not declare the official goal reached.

Matched comparisons use the same episode seeds for the compared candidates.

The protected official panel is separate. Do not combine its result with development scores.

Official assessment requires goal review, no active inquiry, and an explicitly assigned best-known candidate.

The PI decides whether to request it. The Runner checks the operational contract and executes it.

Either official pass or official fail ends the campaign.

09 / TERMS AND SOURCES

Model and checkpoint terminology

Scientific model
A document about the robot and task. It separates established facts, physical consequences, and unknowns.
Learned policy
The controller produced by learning. It chooses actions from observations.
Training checkpoint
A saved learning artifact. It is not a scientific conclusion.
PI checkpoint
A written record of evidence, interpretation, remaining questions, and next direction.
Best-known policy
The strongest available candidate supported by completed evidence. It is not automatically ready for assessment.
Harness
The launcher, Runner, contracts, and context that support the research process.
Inquiry
A temporary question that can change the route to the human goal. It is not one training run.

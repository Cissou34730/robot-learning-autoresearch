2. The task

A two-joint planar arm in MuJoCo had an upper arm of 0.12 m and a forearm of 0.10 m. Each joint could turn from -170 to +170 degrees. Targets appeared 0.06 to 0.20 m from the base at any bearing. The hand had to stay within 1 cm of a target for 100 uninterrupted steps during a 500-step episode. The goal was at least 196 successes in the official 200-episode assessment. That assessment was terminal: it ended the campaign.

The PI was the AI principal investigator. The PI made scientific decisions. Runner was the harness software. It ran operations and recorded facts. Coder and reviewer were AI agents that changed and checked code for the PI. An inquiry was a bounded research question opened by the PI.

3. The final controller

T4 is a hybrid controller. It is not a policy that learned the task alone.

For each target, the saved controller calculates the joint angles of one elbow branch. It selects the branch that the joint limits allow. A PD term moves the joints toward those angles. The PPO policy adds its learned action. The policy also observes the remaining distance to the selected joint angles. A slew limiter then limits the change in action at each 20 ms step.

All of these parts are in the saved controller runtime. The official test ran the same runtime.

6. What went well

Diagnostics led to repairs

Each diagnostic had a clear next action. The traces in I2 located oscillation near the joint limits. The limiter repaired it. A small ablation then showed that the limiter was necessary.

The traces in I3 showed that T3 did not use the other elbow branch. This led to the design of T4.

Balanced goal reviews

At 97.2% on pooled fresh panels, the PI chose further work. At 400/400, the PI requested the official test.

Both decisions compared the official prospects of the best candidate with the prospects of further work. Both decisions noted that development results can overstate success.

Honest negative result

I3 closed as a negative result for the tested implementation only. The PI did not claim that the method itself failed. The next inquiry changed the part that T3 had omitted.

Clean launch and evidence

The launch check confirmed a clean harness. Protected evaluations ran normally. The PI used paired panels with new seeds before the official test.

7. Issues and observations

The pass uses a hybrid controller

T4 uses an analytic branch choice and a PD term with the learned policy. The pass shows that this hybrid controller meets the goal.

It does not show that PPO alone can meet the goal. The maintainer accepts the hybrid design for this task.

One extra trace

M17 traced the executed branch after M16 had already shown 400/400. This trace did not change the next action. It took about one minute.

Same-panel reuse

T2 was selected as best-known partly from the task-reference panel. The PI used that panel again for later comparisons.

The final selection of T4 also used new paired panels. This reduces the risk of an optimistic estimate. It does not remove it.

Paired panel
Two controllers run on the same episodes, so that each episode can be compared.
PD term
A fixed control term that moves the joints toward set angles in proportion to the error and its rate of change.
Policy
The learned controller that selects the arm's actions.
Slew limiter
A rule that limits how much the action can change in one control step.

Campaign structure

Most inquiries opened with a one-recipe evidence boundary

OI-002 assessment

The original indefinite-continuation symptom did not recur

The change in contracts/program.md correctly defined the closure boundary: sufficient evidence could support a positive, negative, limited, or inconclusive result. The inquiry prompt also told the PI to compare accumulated evidence with that condition before choosing another action.

This worked inside inquiries. Negative and limited results closed cleanly. The campaign did not show indefinite continuation inside one inquiry.

The campaign does not show that the new reminder caused one-recipe inquiries. The same train-and-evaluate pattern existed in earlier campaigns before this implementation. Inquiry granularity was largely determined at opening, where many closure conditions explicitly prescribed one intervention and its evaluation.

The supported conclusion is narrower: OI-002 achieved bounded closure in this campaign. Scientific effectiveness remained insufficient. A replacement closure mechanism is not justified by this record.

Root-cause analysis

Boundary formation and boundary enforcement are separate

  1. Symptom: 14 of 15 inquiries were one training run plus one evaluation. Seven later inquiries repeated the same arrival-shaping route.
  2. Boundary formation: Many inquiries opened with closure conditions tied to one recipe and one matched evaluation. The broader method question could remain unresolved after that recipe was tested.
  3. Boundary enforcement: The new reminder correctly closed the inquiry when its declared evidence became available, including after negative and limited results.
  4. Scientific progression: Whether the later interventions justified continuing the same method requires assessment of their distinctions and evidence. Repeated failures do not automatically invalidate a method.
  5. Cap interaction: Small inquiries consumed the temporary 15-inquiry allowance. The cap exposed this granularity but did not cause it.
  6. Attribution limit: The campaign included other prompt changes, and the one-training pattern predates OI-002. No isolated causal comparison exists.

Issue boundaries

  • OI-002: bounded closure, now closed with observed behavior.
  • OI-003: available detailed evidence was not always inspected or interpreted against aggregate success.
  • OI-004: checkpoint nomination, evidence selection, and scientific progression. The bounded repair is implemented; campaign effect is unmeasured.
  • OI-013: the operational effect of the temporary inquiry cap.
  • OI-015: the intended T17 change was not established as an effective experiment distinct from T16.
  • None of these issues authorizes the Runner to reject a scientific method.

Limits and next investigation

The harness should not change before the causes are separated

  • It does not isolate OI-002 from the handoff and goal-review wording changes.
  • It does not prove that the closure sentence increased training frequency.
  • It does not prove that a different intervention would have reached 98%.
  • It does not show that repeated negative recipes invalidate the broader method.

OI-002 is closed and its current wording stays. The next investigation is OI-004: checkpoint nomination, evidence selection, and scientific progression. It must distinguish supported redirects from progression that outruns missing evidence or repeats a route despite its recorded decision frontier.

The OI-004 repair must not require evaluation of every checkpoint, prescribe a scientific method, or treat fewer operations, fewer inquiries, or a changed direction as automatic improvements.

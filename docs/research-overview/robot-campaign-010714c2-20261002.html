Task and terms

A learned policy controls a two-joint arm. Targets cover radii of 6–20 cm and the full angular range.

The end effector must stay within 1 cm of the target for two continuous seconds. This requires 100 consecutive control steps. Each episode permits at most 500 control steps.

The official goal is at least 98% success on one fixed 200-episode panel. Development measurements do not count toward that result.

PI means principal investigator. PPO means proximal policy optimization, the learning algorithm used here. IK means inverse kinematics. PD means proportional-derivative control. T identifies a training operation, M a measurement operation, I an inquiry, and E another recorded operation. Candidate names in this report refer to checkpoint 120832 unless stated otherwise.

Sequence of work

  1. Startup intervention. The PI expanded the training radius range from 14–20 cm to 6–20 cm. T1 trained a fresh PPO policy before any measurement or inquiry.
  2. I1: locate the failure pattern. M1 evaluated T1. M2 repeated the same panel after a radius-bin correction. T1 succeeded in 98 / 160 episodes. Of its 62 failures, 58 never entered the tolerance region.
  3. I2: test a geometry interface. T2 combined target-polar and radial-tangential inputs with a near-target velocity-sensitive training signal. M3 recorded 0 / 160 successes against T1's 98 / 160. The PI rejected the tested recipe.
  4. Goal review: test physical control capability. M4 tested nine fixed IK joint-PD controllers. Each failed all 160 cases. M5 reduced the gains. Each nearest-branch controller then completed all 160 cases.
  5. I3: test action attenuation. E12 restored the T1 recipe. T3 failed a formatting check before training. T4 repeated the identical request after repair. M6 showed a gain. T5 repeated the method with a new training seed, but M7 fell below T1. The PI closed the inquiry.
  6. I4: test continuous branch-error inputs. T6 and T7 replaced four wrapped IK angle errors with sine-cosine pairs. Both used the identity physical action mapping. M8 showed large gains over T1, but T6 increased hold interruptions. E18 accepted the route using the stronger T7 result.
  7. Role and readiness review. E20 assigned T7 the best-known role. M9 evaluated the same frozen candidate on new development seeds. E21 then requested official assessment. The official result failed the goal.

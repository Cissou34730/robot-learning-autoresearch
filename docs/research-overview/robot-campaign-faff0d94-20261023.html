2. Read this first: the prompt confound

Campaigns c06b15e4, 4991570c and faff0d94 cannot be used as evidence about moving content into the system prompt. The adaptation commits 0b64ea66 and ec21e7bf added behavioural directives to the PI system prompt. These directives never existed on harness (d07a21c9) and were not in the first OI-041 commit 3afe89f2. The maintainer's coding assistant wrote them during the adaptation; the maintainer did not draft them. Each later campaign therefore changed placement and behavioural instructions together.

3. What the arm had to do

A simulated two-joint planar arm had to reach targets 6 to 20 cm from its base, at any bearing. Its hand had to stay within 1 cm of the target for 100 uninterrupted control steps (two seconds). The official goal was at least 196 successes in 200 episodes, with a frozen learned policy.

The PI is the AI principal investigator. Runner executes operations, enforces boundaries and records results. Coder and reviewer agents implement and check scientific code. A panel is a set of test episodes. Research evaluation uses the PI-owned evaluator; it is development evidence, not the official result.

4. The final controller: analytic control with a gated learned residual

Target and arm state: select one feasible inverse-kinematics (IK) branch per episode and latch it.

Compute joint torques analytically

Computed-torque joint feedback: mass, bias and passive-force terms drive the joints toward the IK solution.

Add a bounded learned correction

Everywhere else

Add the PPO residual (bounded, tapered to zero inside the 1 cm hold region).

Local gate (T5 and T6 runtime)

Suppress the residual, with a smooth taper, in a core around 12.5–14.5 cm and 128–146° where only one IK branch is feasible.

This is a hybrid controller, not pure PPO. The analytic part does most of the work. Diagnostics with the residual fixed at zero succeeded on M4 (80/80 fresh seeds), M6 (43/43 replay seeds), M10 (99/99 replay seeds) and M12 (200/200 fresh seeds). Every T3 failure that was replayed with zero residual (three from M5, three from M9, one from M12) was a non-entry that the zero-residual controller solved on the same seed. These replays place the tested failures in the learned residual.

The final candidate T6 contains no new learning. T6 was a transfer "training" run from T3:checkpoint-115712 with PPO updates disabled and VecNormalize statistics frozen (120,832 rollout steps). Only robot_learning/train.py changed for it. The PI called it "runtime-only candidate materialization, not policy learning". [State]

Rule B. contracts/instruments/coding.md (lines 24–32, decided 22 October, see OI-046 in the log) states that only a training operation creates a candidate, and a runtime change reaches a candidate only through a training run. T6 follows the wording. It does not follow the intent: the network never trained under the gate and paid no learning cost. In effect it is the repackaging operation that the maintainer had postponed. This gap is in the contract and does not depend on the prompt.

Runner's exact serialized-component comparison records T6's policy as equal to T3 at 1 of 24 corresponding checkpoints and its normalization component as equal at 0 of 24. The PI recorded that T6 "preserved policy and normalization digests". This report did not investigate the difference.

7. Decision behaviour

These are observations from one campaign. Section 9 gives the attribution.

Differences from the last clean pass (cfa88359)

  • Refine instead of assess. At the S7 goal review T3 had 396/400 on fresh panels and pass probability 0.858. The PI opened I3 (quote in section 5). In cfa88359 the PI assessed at 0.97 after one fresh panel and the task reference, without a third inquiry.
  • Persistence on the same ~1% failure. I3 set its own limit ("do not continue open-ended tuning") and closed with "rather than continue tuning". The next goal review ran M12 and opened I4 on the same mechanism. A reasoning summary in the I4 session reads: "Building an exact-copy candidate ... seems like a credible next step rather than prematurely closing the inquiry." and "I think I should keep working on creating the exact runtime-only candidate." At that point T3 had 10 failures in 800 post-selection episodes.
  • Confirmation by panel count. T3 was measured on nine panels; the final choice between T6 and T3 rests on 2 episodes in 400.
  • Bundled change. T2 changed environment, observations and reward together and scored 0/160; the cause was not isolated.
  • Learning displaced. The analytic controller carried the task; the learned residual caused the failures and was then gated off locally. The final candidate's weights are T3's, untrained under the gate.
  • Rule B workaround. A training run that updated nothing produced the candidate.
  • Missed symmetry. The mirror sector of the failure region was never tested.

Similarities with cfa88359

  • Robotics framing. The PI reasoned in IK branches, joint limits, computed torque and acquisition versus hold. Robotics-to-RL vocabulary ratio of PI text: 0.52 (messages 0.80), against 0.48 for cfa88359 and 0.14 for 5d94bf8a.
  • Clean mechanism diagnostics. Zero-residual replays on the exact failure seeds (M6, M10, M12) and a gate-only replay that reproduced all 200 M12 baseline episodes (M13).
  • Honest uncertainty. Each role assignment stated its interval and that it was not an official result; selection optimism was named.
  • Bounded final claim. The assessment request said the choice was "not a superiority claim" over T3.
  • No failed or superseded operation.

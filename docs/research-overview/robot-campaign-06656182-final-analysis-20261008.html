Result

The campaign passed. The margin was zero.

The goal is to reach the target and hold the tool inside 1 cm for 2 seconds. The contract for this task is in contracts/scenario.md.

The campaign started on 7 October 2026 at 19:45 UTC. The official assessment passed at 21:10 UTC. The run lasted about 85 minutes.

The campaign used 23 operations. These were 6 training runs, 9 measurements, 5 inquiries, checkpoints, one model-role assignment (E22), and one campaign conclusion (E23). Each training run had a ceiling of 120,000 steps and used about 120,832 steps.

The PI used 5 of 15 allowed inquiries. It closed each inquiry question before it opened the next one.

What happened

Operation What it tested Result
T1, M1 A fresh baseline policy. Training used targets 14 to 20 cm from the base. M1 used a held-out 100-episode panel. 73%. All 48 targets at 14 cm or more succeeded. Failures were below 14 cm. In 22 cases the tool never reached tolerance. In 5 cases it reached tolerance and did not hold.
I1, T2, M2 Does full coverage of the 6 to 20 cm range fix the failures? T2 trained from scratch on the full range. T2 scored 35%. T1 scored 73% on the same panel. Coverage alone did not help.
M3 A trace of how T1 fails. Failures were non-entries near the base and interrupted holds. Strong actions also occurred in successes. Strong actions alone do not explain the failures.
I2, T3, M4, M5 Does one bounded reward term for velocity-aware settling help? T3 started from T1. T3 scored 94%. T1 scored 71% on matched seeds. On a fresh panel T3 scored 94% and T1 scored 76%.
I3, T4, M6 Does a doubled velocity penalty help? T4 scored 85%. T3 scored 95%. The larger penalty hurt. The PI rejected T4.
M7 A sweep of actuator response across radii and both branches. The response was strong and reached its limit. Actuator authority is not the limit.
I4, T5, M8 The T3 recipe with the original 0.25 coefficient. The near-target velocity gate grew from 2 cm to 3 cm. This should make the policy brake earlier. T5 scored 95%. T3 scored 93%.
I5, T6, M9 Does Cartesian end-effector speed work better than joint speed as the velocity signal? T6 scored 90%. T5 scored 98% on the same panel. The PI rejected T6.
E22, E23 The PI assigned T5 as best-known. The PI then requested the official assessment. The official assessment passed with 98.0%.

Reviewer's reading

What the record may mean

This section is the reviewer's reading of the record. It is not proof.

  1. Each inquiry changed one thing. Each inquiry compared the change with a reference on the same seeds. This makes the comparisons easy to read.
  2. Three of five interventions (T2, T4, T6) were negative. The PI rejected them cleanly. The PI did not read more into them than the numbers allowed.
  3. Panel noise is real. T1 scored 71% to 76% on different panels. T5 scored 95% on one panel and 98% on another. A 98% official result with zero margin is a pass. It is not strong proof of a margin.
  4. The I3 and I4 inquiry questions have identical text. This may point to the inquiry-control issue OI-002. The record does not settle this.
  5. The startup trained first (T1) and measured at once. The handoff was sound. This agrees with the OI-001 retrospective, which found 4 of 5 startups sound.
  6. Ten of 15 allowed inquiries stayed unused. Each inquiry question was bounded, tested against a matched reference, and closed explicitly.

Limits

What this campaign does not show

  • It shows one official run. No repeat run exists.
  • It has no counterfactual. It does not show what another PI, prompt, or seed would have done.
  • It passed at the threshold. A second official run could score below 98%.
  • It does not show that the R8 prompt caused the pass.
  • It does not measure how much of the result came from the T3 reward change and how much came from T5.

Glossary

Terms in this report

Inquiry
A temporary question that can change the route to the goal. One inquiry can contain several training runs and measurements.
Checkpoint
This report uses the word for saved model weights from a training run, such as T5:checkpoint-120832. The checkpoint operation is different. It saves a session record.
Best-known
The model role for the strongest candidate that completed evidence supports. The PI must assign it before an official assessment.
Matched panel
A set of test episodes with fixed seeds. Two candidates run the same episodes. The scores are then comparable.
Official assessment
The protected 200-episode test. It ends the campaign with a pass or a fail.

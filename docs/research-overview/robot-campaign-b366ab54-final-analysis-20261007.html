Harness findings

The records expose three lifecycle lessons

Inquiry closure and inquiry wording pulled in different directions. I2 allowed closure after one bounded comparison. Its question asked for the most reliable intervention. A broad success-seeking question can keep work open after its formal closure test is met.
The readiness gate had no margin. M10 achieved exactly 196. Seven T1 research panels pooled to 97.357%. Best-known evidence supported T1 selection. It did not strongly support official readiness.
The final checkpoint described the wrong failure transition. It said an official failure redirects the next inquiry. The lifecycle contract makes official pass or failure terminal for the campaign.
The generated brief is stale after termination. The top reports official failure. Later checkpoint text says the assessment remains unrun and unused. Terminal facts and copied checkpoint facts can conflict in one generated document.

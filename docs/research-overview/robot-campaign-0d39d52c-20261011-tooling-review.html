There was real scientific evidence

The first learned policy completed 150 of 160 development episodes. Expanded diagnostics separated seven non-reach failures from three reached-but-failed-hold episodes. A changed reward recipe then produced two measured checkpoints with zero successes and zero first reaches.

A later same-panel comparison measured T3 at 114/160 and the unchanged T1 reference at 154/160. Those are development results, not official task certification.

There was also avoidable-looking evidence work

The PI repeatedly measured the same frozen candidate and panel while expanding diagnostics, retried malformed queries, reread T1 logs, and retrieved two full M9 diagnostic arrays. These are observed patterns, not a measured attribution of the campaign's extra cost.

Recipe restoration also rolled back measurement capability. The next measurement completed without several diagnostics it had requested.

Reading rule: Observation means recorded behavior or a count. Limit / interpretation marks what that observation cannot establish. Root-cause analysis remains deferred.

What the science says, and does not say

The goal is a learned policy with at least 98% episode success under the official task distribution: reaching the 1 cm target region and completing an uninterrupted 2-second hold. On a 160-episode development panel, the corresponding count threshold is 157 successes. No protected official assessment was completed.

Reach and hold were different failure classes

M7: seven non-reaches, three failed holds after reaching. Its 153 first reaches used the open branch where branch was defined. Open-branch use does not prove that a folded route is necessary, nor that route choice caused failure.

High near-target velocity, saturated action, and repeated internal excursions make braking and stabilization plausible investigative targets. They do not isolate a cause.

The reward intervention had a bounded negative result

M8 checked implementation of the new braking/margin reward, not learned policy behavior. T2 combined the new reward with training seed 1; both measured checkpoints failed to reach on the M9 panel.

I2 closed with a negative result. This does not disprove the broader method or uniquely attribute the result to reward design rather than training seed or other recipe effects.

Evidence panel · Why the coder share is not an overhead estimate
  • All 12.093369 coder AIU had already been recorded by the T2 request.
  • The later 11.300468 AIU was all PI spending; there were no new coders after that boundary.
  • Earlier inline coding was not separately metered. It lived inside earlier PI costs. Subtracting the new coder total from the current total would not isolate a like-for-like counterfactual.
  • Repeated measurements, log rereads, large artifact retrievals, handoffs, and review all coexist in this campaign. Their individual incremental costs have not been experimentally isolated.

The recorded difference is worth investigating. It is not evidence that native coding alone caused the increase, nor that eliminating the custom evidence tool would remove it.

Subsequent harness repairs: implemented, effectiveness unmeasured

Update at 00:03 CEST, 12 October 2026: the parent maintainer reports that the approved OI18, OI8, and OI15 corrections are implemented, with validation and publication being finished. These changes occurred after the campaign stopped. They do not change its scientific evidence or demonstrate better policy performance, lower campaign cost, or end-to-end campaign effectiveness.

Implementation and check results below are maintainer-reported, not an independent review of harness code by this reporting child. Same-session lifecycle behavior is unchanged. Tracking remains in docs\harness-experiment-log.md.

OI18 · Custom query tool retained

Filters now require typed path and equals fields; null is supported. Selecting an array for filtering, projection, or aggregation explicitly expands its rows. A missing filter field is reported distinctly from zero matches.

Default selected-value pages are bounded to 20 rows / 16 KiB. Full retrieval remains permitted through explicit paging. The tool is retained, not replaced with jello.

Training-log queries default to metadata discovery without values. Record retrieval requires selected metrics or explicit all_metrics; console output prints selectors.

OI8 · Leaner automatic delivery

Responses no longer repeatedly solicit detail access. Automatic completion summaries no longer embed a full artifact inventory or a full repeated paired-seed list.

Public facts, current state, and source pointers are retained. This reduces repeated automatic material; it does not establish a measured reduction in context errors or campaign AIU.

OI15 · Explicit restoration scope

restore_recipe now requires an exact, nonempty paths list including robot_learning\training\current_params.json. Unselected files are preserved; campaign laboratory files cannot be selected.

This prevents accidental blanket rollback through explicit source selection. It does not automatically preserve instrumentation inside an arbitrarily selected mixed-purpose file.

If learning and instrumentation share a selected file, the PI must carry measurement capability forward through scientific coding. The contract states this explicitly; there is no automatic guessing or AST merge of scientific code.

OI24 · No measurement-strategy repair bundled

M3/M5/M7 repeated the same policy/panel while adding diagnostics; M4/M6 checked equivalence. Some added information was useful. This was not three independent success replications.

No algorithm repair for fragmented or repeated measurement is included in these corrections.

OI25 · Fidelity remains a separate concern

Preserve denominators, distinct counters, panel identity, and explicit missing fields when evidence is summarized. The concrete discrepancies above are the basis for this topic.

No evidence-fidelity algorithm repair is bundled, and the interface changes do not prove those discrepancies will not recur.

OI26 / OI14 · Cost and causation remain unmeasured

Measure coder and main-PI review work without treating all coder AIU as added overhead. Keep causal conclusions separate from descriptive comparisons and bundled interventions.

No coding-review/cost algorithm repair is bundled. Root-cause attribution and scientific effectiveness remain unmeasured.

Campaign 5d94bf8a / final report / 23 October 2026

The first system-prompt campaign plateaus and fails at 193/200

The arm failed the official test with 193 successes in 200 episodes. The goal was 196. The best candidate plateaued at about 97% from the second hour. Six later interventions were all negative and used two thirds of the campaign's AI usage.

This was the first campaign on the OI-041 branch (3afe89f2). It moved the PI persona, the contracts and the scientific model into the SDK system prompt. It is the only clean system-prompt data point so far: later commits also added behavioural directives. With the physics taken out of the first message, the PI worked in RL terms and became more defensive. One campaign does not show that the prompt change caused this.

The arm, its ±170° joint limits, zero gravity and the reset are mirror-symmetric about the x axis. The asymmetry belongs to the learned policy, not the task, and it passed through the transfer chain from T1. The PI described a "rear sector" with shoulder-limit proximity and never compared it with the mirror sector.

6882b9ec failed at 193/200 with the same weakness: −155° to −120°, 31% failure. That campaign ran before OI-041.

In cfa88359 the PI tested reflection symmetry and passed at 199/200 with a reflection-canonicalized policy I/O, trained fresh. Neither scientific model, before or after OI-041, states the mirror symmetry.

5. The assessment decision

Pooled research evidence for T3 was 2,524/2,600 (97.1%), a pass chance of about 0.3. The PI cited 0.267 (nine panels, semantics 604d52985c97) and 0.382 (two 400-episode panels, semantics d844c174d575). It chose the fixed task-reference panel, M35 198/200 (pass chance 0.778), because that panel uses the protected execution path.

The task-reference panel composition is normal: 45/200 rear-sector episodes (22.5%). It held 2 sector failures where about 5 were expected. It is one fixed 200-episode panel, and the PI had changed its research evaluation semantics during the campaign. The decision repeats 6882b9ec.

The PI's request said: "This is not the official panel and its result is not counted toward the official score." and "this conditional estimate assumes independent representative episodes and may be optimistic because the artifact was selected as best-known using other candidate evidence." The caveats were stated; the decision still rested on the most favourable panel.

6. Behaviour: RL-centric and defensive

What the records show

  • Robotics-to-RL vocabulary ratio in PI records: 0.14. The ratio was 0.48 in 6882b9ec and cfa88359, and 0.72 in the information run 5e7172ca on harness.
  • Six inquiries only validated or compared checkpoints.
  • All six interventions protected T3: four transfers and two frozen overlays. None retrained a changed method from scratch.
  • The analyst read 6 files instead of 38 and omitted the training range. Startup trained the unchanged recipe; full-range exposure came about 30 minutes later.
  • 14 goal reviews cost 34.4 AIU.

With the physics taken out of the first message, the PI framed the problem in RL terms and hedged more. The system prompt gave program.md, whose guidance is mostly limits on claims, system-level weight in every turn. The new tone added "Claim no more than the tested implementation and the recorded evidence show". The SDK "bias to action" text, the only counterweight, was removed. This is a coincidence in one campaign, not a demonstrated cause.

10. Candidate follow-ups and what happened next

The log listed four candidate follow-ups, not approved at the time:

  • Analyst: read the full recipe, including training environment and reward, and state the system's physical symmetries and invariances.
  • Startup message: restore the model's decision-relevant synthesis.
  • Inquiry objective: restore the persistence sentence.
  • Rebalance the system prompt: remove the caution added by the new tone.

New capabilities, such as task-reference panels with PI-chosen seeds, wait until the core behaviour is fixed (maintainer decision, 23 October). The adaptation that followed (0b64ea66, then ec21e7bf and ba92677f) went beyond these items and added new behavioural directives, which is why the later campaigns are information only.

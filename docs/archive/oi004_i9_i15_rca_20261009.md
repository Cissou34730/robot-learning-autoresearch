# OI-004: evidence selection and scientific progression in I9-I15

Campaign: `1ae27a27-6880-4ca6-9d50-e6498e771cb7`. Analysis date: 2026-10-09.

## Conclusion

I9-I15 support OI-004 through concrete failures to connect available evidence to candidate selection and subsequent scientific interpretation. The strongest finding is that identical binary success outcomes were treated as preserved behavior even when first reach and hold interruptions changed. There is also an unexamined equivalence between the supposedly different T16 and T17 experiments, and a consistent terminal-checkpoint selection pattern without a recorded comparison with the learning trajectory.

The supported process-level diagnosis is: progression from a terminal candidate's task score to another recipe sometimes outran the evidence about what the intervention actually changed. This is narrower than a claim that the PI should have abandoned the research direction. The records do not establish the cause of the PI's reasoning errors, prove that another candidate would have won, or justify a Runner rule choosing scientific actions.

## Scope and method

Only the I9-I15 interval is scientific evidence for this RCA. It includes the intervening goal-review measurement M25 between I13 and I14. Earlier M1/M10 findings quoted by these records are inherited premises, not independently validated evidence in this analysis. T3 is used only as a reference remeasured during this interval. No other campaign supports the conclusions.

## 2. I9 set aside available mechanism evidence

E36 says the available summary and structural inventory do not establish whether arrival speed fell. That limitation was carried into E37's transition to another representation. However, the completed M11 artifacts contain `first_reach_joint_speed_rad_s` and `closest_approach_joint_speed_rad_s` for the matched panel.

On the seven reference-failure seeds defined within M11, T10 has lower closest-approach speed in all seven. Of the four cases where T3 first reached tolerance, T10 first reaches only one. On that shared reached case (seed 4201), first-reach speed changes from 7.1974 to 4.1000 rad/s. Across the full panel, first reaches fall from 157 to 143 and successes from 153 to 142.

These are not equivalent arrival states: low closest-approach speed can accompany failure to reach, so these values do not establish a successful braking repair. They do support a more specific interpretation: the measured speed change came with a substantial reach loss. The decision could have used that tradeoff rather than leaving whether the speed intervention changed the recorded behavior unanswered.

Sources: M11 at line 56 and its two detailed evaluation artifacts; E36/E37 at lines 57-58. The seven matched rows were inspected in the M11 artifacts. This is an evidence-use failure with consequences for progression, and overlaps OI-003. It is not proof that moving to a representation intervention was wrong.

## 5. T16 and T17 are an unexplained experimental equivalence

The T17 request describes a new distance-derived velocity envelope, narrower angle conditioning, overshoot weighting, and terminal taper. The recorded scientific snapshots contain a real `policy_io.py` change between T16 (`ba2e90f...`) and T17 (`9988ffc...`). Identical I14/I15 question text therefore does not establish an identical intended experiment.

The saved evidence reveals a stronger, different concern:

- All 24 checkpoint training-success and reward records match exactly between T16 and T17.
- At all 24 corresponding checkpoints, the uncompressed `policy.pth` and `policy.optimizer.pth` archive members are byte-identical, and `vecnormalize.pkl` is byte-identical.
- The 160 M27/T16 and M28/T17 episode-result rows match exactly, including reward and step counts, and their detailed research diagnostics match exactly.
- The terminal `policy_runtime.pkl` files have different hashes. The complete runtime artifacts must therefore not be described as byte-identical.

For the terminal checkpoint, both policy-member hashes are `40c2850ab162140b54cf1f0a8a186acfd59872005b3b037ffadd74a018863de0`.

This establishes duplicate saved learned state and duplicate recorded panel behavior despite a different intended controller. It does not establish why. The archived data do not distinguish an inactive intervention, ineffective action after other processing, runtime/source delivery problems, or another equivalence mechanism. No gate-activation or action-difference measurement was performed in this audit.

E63/E64 reject the distance-envelope recipe and move toward a different terminal-state representation without addressing this equality. The record therefore leaves unresolved whether the intended distinction between I14 and I15 was exercised. That limits how much independent scientific evidence I15 adds about the new controller design.

M28 also records eight interruptions and the same modest margin improvement as T16; its closure emphasizes zero success discordance with T3. It supplies no evidence of better complete holds, but it is not behaviorally identical to the T3 reference, which has four interruptions.

## RCA: what is established and what remains hypothetical

**Observed symptom:** seven new terminal candidates failed to exceed the reference's 153/160 complete holds. This fact alone is not a scientific-process defect.

**Established evidence-to-decision failures:** I9's available speed diagnostics were not incorporated into its stated interpretation; I11 carried an incorrect reach-preservation claim; I15 did not address the effective equivalence with T16 visible in training and evaluation records.

**Supported contributing pattern:** candidate selection repeatedly used the terminal checkpoint without a recorded trajectory-based rationale. This left alternative candidate performance unknown while conclusions and subsequent interventions proceeded.

**Process-level explanation:** task success sometimes substituted for the distinct evidence needed to establish how the intervention affected the proposed mechanism. The next intervention could then be selected while important distinctions remained unresolved: changed versus unchanged behavior, insufficient versus unmeasured braking margin, or different source code versus a distinct effective experiment.

**Unproven causes:** prompt wording, context exhaustion, the fixed training allocation, a particular internal PI reasoning habit, or a specific execution bug causing T16/T17 equality. None is isolated by these records. There is also no evidence that earlier checkpoint selection would have achieved the goal.

## Implications for OI-004

The issue should concern the evidential basis for candidate nomination and progression, rather than a quota on related inquiries or a requirement to abandon a method after failures.

A future correction should preserve PI choice while making three distinctions durable: why the chosen candidate represents the relevant learning evidence; what changed in the measured mechanism versus the aggregate outcome; and which uncertainty still limits interpretation of a null or negative result. An operation that resolves one of those uncertainties remains a PI scientific choice. No fixed checkpoint rank, compulsory extra evaluation, mandatory diagnostic sequence, or automatic route rejection follows from this RCA.

The highest-value follow-up supported by this interval is to explain the T16/T17 experimental equivalence before counting I15 as an independent effective test of the new controller. That requires additional authorized scientific evidence; this audit only identifies the gap. The I11 preservation error can already be corrected from existing artifacts.
